## 0. Executive command

Build and run V13 as a broad, multi-day constructed-world program around one question:

> Can a bounded reader use common structure, attention, costs, opportunities, target-specific
> correction, communicative goals, and production roles to narrow a maker model more efficiently
> and more safely than either generic inverse planning or uncorrected self-projection?

V13 is not a larger rerun of V12. It must build five objects V12 did not contain:

1. a **nested similarity basin**, separating common substrate, group or convention, expertise,
   individual history, and current-state similarity;
2. an explicit **attention policy** that allocates finite inspection or precision across evidence
   without creating information;
3. a multidimensional **cost and opportunity model** in which effort can be caused by motivation,
   competence, knowledge, constraint, risk, or imposed demands;
4. a maker **goal model** in which helping, neutral expression, persuasion, concealment, and
   misleading are communicative goals rather than a magical source type;
5. a **role-relative production graph** in which a director's lower-order instruction can become a
   subordinate's high-order goal, and central control competes against a genuinely equivalent
   shared-brief construction.

The program has nine mandatory trunks and one cross-cutting attack matrix:

| trunk | cards | plain question |
|---|---:|---|
| I — integrity and calibration | 14 | Are the new worlds live, matched, reproducible, and timed honestly? |
| C — common prior and nested similarity | 16 | What does self supply beyond any equally local prior, and what does common substrate supply beyond a broad population? |
| A — attention and entry | 14 | Can finite attention make useful evidence available without changing the evidence itself? |
| O — opportunity and cost | 18 | What can paid cost and forgone alternatives identify, under which confounds and weighting rules? |
| P — projection and correction | 14 | How is a cheap local prior corrected when the maker differs? |
| G — communicative goals and trust | 16 | Can maker goals, source reliability, artifact evidence, and uptake remain separate? |
| H — hierarchy and many hands | 16 | Which interaction traces distinguish central direction from shared or distributed organization? |
| Q — active epistemic foraging | 12 | When does PyMDP add useful action selection rather than decorative inference? |
| L — Sounding Line bridge | 12 | Which validated rulers and predicted shapes deserve text-side implementation? |
| X — adversarial matrix | 20 attacks | Which positive results survive cheap features, false context, equifinality, and model misspecification? |

There are **132 mandatory cards before X**. Every mandatory card must reach `LANDED`,
`SCIENTIFIC_CLOSED`, `INSTRUMENT_FAILED`, `VOID`, or `RESOURCE_BLOCKED` under the rules below.
Unrelated trunks continue when one branch dies. Downstream method cards use an oracle or a
deliberately graded upstream input when an empirical upstream estimator fails.

The expected execution envelope on the curator's machine is **72 to 96 wall-clock hours** at the
selected below-normal worker count. Duration is not evidence and is never manufactured. A discarded
runtime pilot selects a frozen scientifically useful workload tier before any discovery result is
read. If the named program is still predicted to finish early, expand independent worlds, maker
families, cost ecologies, team ecologies, and untouched transfer domains in the frozen order in
§8. Never sleep, repeat an identical seed, or inflate rows from the same maker to fill time.

## 1. What V13 can and cannot establish

### 1.1 Claim ceiling

V13 can establish:

- logical sufficiency of the declared constructed mechanisms;
- identifiability or non-identifiability under specified world families;
- the sample, information, and compute price of each effect;
- whether attention allocation, opportunity-aware inference, correction, trust gating, and active
  probing improve proper prediction in those worlds;
- whether exact and PyMDP policies agree where PyMDP is used;
- which simulated shapes are worth testing in Sounding Line.

V13 cannot establish:

- that people perform any operation in this specification;
- that a broad random-profile population is an adequate model of humanity;
- that a simulated common substrate is a limbic system, midbrain, affective system, embodiment, or
  culture;
- that PyMDP has empathy, attention, trust, values, or care;
- that cost reveals one true motive;
- that coherent dependency identifies a director;
- that an artifact uniquely identifies its historical maker process;
- that a Sounding Line reader can recover the same variables from text;
- that an idealized reader that exceeds a planted human-like heuristic is “super-empathic” in any
  general or moral sense.

Every verdict carries one ceiling:

- **METHOD** — known-answer ruler or implementation identity;
- **CONSTRUCTED MECHANISM** — consequence of the declared world and reader;
- **BOUNDARY** — demonstrated non-identifiability or failure within the tested family;
- **INSTRUMENT FAILURE** — the manipulation or measurement could not answer the question;
- **VOID** — the intended latent did not reach observations or actions;
- **RESOURCE BLOCKED** — the exact requirement could not be executed with the available machine;
  this state never counts as a scientific negative.

### 1.2 Minimal theory contact

Ordinary inverse planning supplies the general problem. V13 tests candidate efficiency and safety
structures around it. Its strongest theory-contact hypothesis is:

> A reader inside a relevant common-structure basin can construct a useful local prior cheaply,
> allocate attention toward diagnostic features of the maker's situation, and correct that prior
> with target-specific evidence. The benefit is graded and relational. It need not give access to a
> private inference unavailable to another reader.

This statement survives if a non-self local prior performs as well as self. In that outcome,
`self` is one biologically cheap way to instantiate locality rather than an informationally
privileged route. The distinctive claim moves to the availability and construction cost of the
local prior, not its label.

### 1.3 Reader operations under test

Use these as separable computational operations, not a serial neural story:

1. **Initialize locality:** construct a prior from common, group, expertise, and self information.
2. **Estimate applicability:** infer which similarities matter to this decision.
3. **Allocate attention:** choose evidence channels or likelihood precision under a budget.
4. **Differentiate the maker:** update away from self and group priors using target evidence.
5. **Infer goals:** jointly infer proximal, standing, and communicative goals.
6. **Gate epistemic weight:** represent source reliability and artifact support separately from
   inferred goals.
7. **Choose uptake:** decide whether prediction, process learning, belief update, or preference
   movement is warranted.

Helping, neutrality, persuasion, concealment, and misleading belong to operation 5. Reliability is
operation 6. Uptake is operation 7. V13 must not collapse them into a single bard score.

## 3. Constructed world

### 3.1 Nested maker and reader structure

Represent a maker model as a composition rather than one flat profile:

\[
M_m = F(z_{common}, z_{group}, z_{expertise}, z_{individual}, z_{state}, c_{episode}).
\]

The factors mean:

| factor | constructed role | prohibited interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `z_common` | factorization, action constraints, and mappings shared by one simulated population | human biology or a human universal |
| `z_group` | culture, convention, institution, or model-family-like regularity | a literal culture or demographic |
| `z_expertise` | domain-local transition and observation competence | general intelligence |
| `z_individual` | persistent personal profile and habits | true values without prospective evidence |
| `z_state` | current goal, attention allocation, and temporary pressures | emotion or consciousness |
| `c_episode` | tools, instructions, role, alternatives, constraints, source history | ground truth when merely asserted to the reader |

Create at least four valid common-substrate families whose factorization differs enough that a
reader cannot receive the correct likelihood merely through a label. Within each, create at least
four groups, four expertise ecologies, and a continuous individual distribution. Cross actual
similarity with claimed similarity and cheap surface similarity.

### 3.2 Prior routes

Every C, P, and affected Q card compares relevant subsets of:

1. measured self prior;
2. equally local non-self prior;
3. within-common-substrate population prior;
4. within-group prior;
5. within-expertise prior;
6. broad all-family population prior;
7. entropy-and-distance-matched generic local prior;
8. random local prior;
9. permuted-self prior;
10. anti-similar self prior;
11. target-specific learned prior;
12. oracle ceiling.

For routes 1, 2, 7, 8, and 9, match prior entropy, expected divergence to truth over the sampled
world family, free parameters, coordinate access, compute, and evidence. Where exact simultaneous
matching is impossible, optimize the match without outcome access, report residual imbalance, and
use reweighting or a sensitivity bound before interpretation.

### 3.3 Attention

Implement two independent attention analogues:

- **selection attention:** finite observations, regions, records, or probes are chosen for
  inspection;
- **precision attention:** the same evidence is available, but likelihood contributions from cue
  families are tempered by predeclared nonnegative precision weights.

The cue families are: surface, common structure, group convention, mechanics, goal consequences,
opportunity set, paid cost, anomaly or mistake, communicative shaping, source history, and process
records. Attention may change which evidence is sampled or how noisy evidence is weighted. It may
not alter ground truth, silently add a label, or sharpen the correct cue alone by construction.

Record the maker's own attention allocation separately. Reader–maker attention matching is one
hypothesis, not the definition of good reading.

### 3.4 Opportunity and multidimensional cost

Every choice record contains:

- alternatives actually available;
- alternatives believed available by the maker;
- alternatives visible to the reader;
- chosen action;
- forgone actions;
- counterfactual consequence of each action;
- cost vector for each action;
- actor competence and uncertainty about costs;
- whether each cost was voluntary, imposed, sunk, anticipated, or discovered late.

Use at least these cost dimensions:

| cost | examples inside the world |
|---|---|
| time | delay or number of steps |
| physical or execution | action energy or motor difficulty |
| cognitive | planning or memory demand |
| epistemic | uncertainty and price of acquiring information |
| opportunity | value of the best forgone route |
| social | status, obligation, or coordination penalty |
| risk | outcome variance or downside exposure |
| imposed | externally required work that does not express preference |

Paid cost can be caused by stronger reward or motivation, low competence, low knowledge, hard
constraints, exploration, risk tolerance, social obligation, or error. Plant factorial worlds in
which each rival is true. A reader receives no credit for saying only “the maker cared more.” It
must distinguish matched alternatives or predict a held-out choice.

### 3.5 Maker goals, reliability, trust, and uptake

Represent communicative behavior through ordinary goal variables. At minimum include:

- accurate transmission;
- comprehension support;
- persuasion toward a true or false claim;
- self-presentation;
- concealment or omission;
- strategic misleading;
- neutral production with no reader-directed goal.

Separately represent:

- `q_goal`: posterior over maker goals;
- `q_source`: posterior over source reliability by domain and time;
- `q_content`: posterior support supplied by artifact evidence;
- `q_process`: posterior over maker process;
- `uptake_policy`: prediction use, process imitation, belief update, preference update, or refusal.

A maker can have prosocial goals and be unreliable, adversarial goals and state a true fact, or
aligned values and poor competence. These crossings are mandatory.

### 3.6 Hierarchical production

Use a directed event graph with actors, roles, goals, alternatives, decisions, dependencies,
acceptance, veto, and downstream scope. Add role-relative goal fields:

- `goal_id` and `goal_owner`;
- `assigned_by_event`;
- `inherited_from_goal`;
- `role_level` and `scope`;
- `local_priority` for this actor;
- `project_priority` in the shared production;
- `private_secondary_goals`;
- `observed_cost_vector` and `counterfactual_cost_vectors`;
- `cost_source` and uncertainty;
- `attention_target` when recorded.

A director's secondary specification may become a subordinate's primary assigned goal. Do not use
one absolute goal-level number across all actors.

Construct central director, shared brief, editor, ratifier, rotating leadership, institutional
filter, and distributed coordination worlds. The central and shared-brief pair must have the same
global dependency rule, marginal coherence, event count, quality, surface, and final-goal
distribution. Only actor-specific interventions or interaction traces may distinguish them.

## 4. Solver architecture and PyMDP boundary

### 4.1 Required paths

Use three paths where relevant:

1. **exact enumerative or dynamic-programming reader** for small known-answer worlds;
2. **PyMDP reader** for active observation, action, stopping, and epistemic-value policies;
3. **cheap baselines**, including frequency, outcome-only, count-only, uncertainty sampling,
   nearest neighbor, fixed goal-first, fixed mechanics-first, surface classifier, and oracle.

PyMDP is mandatory only when the reader selects an action or observation. Passive posterior
recovery does not become more meaningful by routing it through PyMDP.

### 4.2 Version and device discipline

The repository pins `inferactively-pymdp==1.0.2` and uses the legacy NumPy API. Preserve that path
for V13. Current PyMDP is JAX-first and supports batched execution, but migrating this constructed
program would change solver semantics and closed-version reproducibility. A JAX port may be an
explicit secondary performance experiment after exact identity gates, never the only path.

The V13 runner must set numeric-library threads to one before importing NumPy, run below normal
priority, and hide accelerators from the process (`CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` empty and JAX CPU-only if
JAX is imported). V13 must use no GPU memory while Sounding Line runs.

### 4.3 Calibration triangle

Before a PyMDP result is interpreted:

- exact and PyMDP posteriors agree in factor-independent identity worlds;
- exact and PyMDP rank the best probe similarly when probe likelihoods are genuinely distinct;
- a utility-only agent fails the epistemic positive control;
- divergence is mapped as factor coupling, horizon, policy count, and precision increase;
- confidently wrong approximations are reported, not averaged away.

Solver disagreement is a solver result. It is never interpreted as a property of empathy, trust,
or the maker.

## 5. Shared card contract

Before implementation, create `results/v13/QUEUE_MANIFEST.json` and a tier-parameterized
`results/v13/EXPECTED_CELLS_TEMPLATE.json`. After the discarded runtime pilot, instantiate
`results/v13/EXPECTED_CELLS.json` and `results/v13/WORKLOAD_LOCK.json` before discovery. A card
record contains:

- card id, trunk, wave, question, construction, target, estimand, null, alternative, strongest
  rival, claim ceiling, solver paths, dependencies, and closure;
- every required factor, level, crossing, domain, world family, prior route, attention policy,
  cost ecology, and reader policy;
- pilot, discovery, transfer, adversarial, and confirmation lineages;
- independent unit and minimum effective sample;
- expected cell count and minimum rows per independent unit;
- source, card, schema, and preregistration hashes;
- estimated and actual wall time, parent-plus-child CPU time, peak RSS, workers, and throughput;
- unique output, checkpoint, and committed completion-ledger key.

The validator must compare the manifest to the literal cards in this specification. A card cannot
be resolved by writing a paragraph, producing one representative cell, or calling a shared module
that omits its declared factors.

### 5.1 Evidence lanes

Use four disjoint lanes:

1. `pilot`: runtime and memory only; results discarded and never promoted;
2. `discovery`: full forest and estimator comparison;
3. `transfer`: fresh common-substrate families, domains, roles, and cost ecologies;
4. `confirmation`: untouched worlds used only for hashed promoted effects.

Lineage is derived from a root seed plus lane, world family, domain, card, and replicate. Test that
no generated object shares an ancestor across lanes.

### 5.2 Required gates per causal card

Every causal card has:

- **live gate:** intervention changes the intended latent or policy;
- **placebo gate:** irrelevant matched intervention does not change the target;
- **positive gate:** a known readable manipulation is recovered;
- **surface gate:** cheap source, length, count, label, or entropy cues cannot solve it;
- **oracle gate:** the target is identifiable when the correct variables are supplied;
- **prediction gate:** the inferred object constrains a held-out choice, continuation, probe, or
  response;
- **calibration gate:** uncertainty or abstention responds in the correct direction.

Failing the live or oracle gate makes the card `VOID` or `INSTRUMENT_FAILED`, not negative evidence.

### 5.3 One-repair rule

One predeclared repair is allowed for a failed instrument. The original attempt, criterion, hashes,
and result remain. A repair may fix a dead manipulation, coding defect, or floor failure. It may not
change the target after seeing an inconvenient result. After one failed repair, close or mark the
instrument failed and continue downstream with oracle inputs where useful.

### 5.4 Conditional reporting rule

If a mechanism predicts benefit in one region and harm in another, the primary result is the
predeclared interaction or conditional surface. A pooled mean may be shown only afterward. It may
not be the headline, criterion, or closure statistic.

### 5.5 Plain-language result record

Every verdict begins with:

1. **Question** in ordinary language.
2. **What happened** without variable names.
3. **What changed in the project world.**
4. **What remains a rival.**
5. **Claim ceiling.**
6. **Numbers**, matrices, and diagnostic detail.

Sentences such as “0.08 top-1 against a 0.25 floor” may appear only after the reader has been told
what was recovered and why it matters.

## 6. Program-wide scores and inference

### 6.1 Primary scores

- log score and Brier score for bounded posteriors;
- expected calibration error and calibration slope;
- risk–coverage and selective error when abstention is allowed;
- information gained per unit inspection or probe cost;
- regret against the exact policy;
- held-out continuation, counterfactual choice, or intervention prediction;
- false confident attribution rate under equifinality;
- actor- and level-specific attribution, never token share alone.

Top-1 accuracy is secondary. An estimator that becomes more accurate by abstaining nowhere and
becoming overconfident does not pass.

### 6.2 Independent units

The independent unit is a world construction, maker, reader, source history, or production team as
declared. Rows emitted by one maker or team are repeated observations, not new makers. Use
world-level aggregation, hierarchical bootstrap, cluster permutation, or partial pooling. Report
the full conditional matrix before any pooled effect.

### 6.3 Multiplicity and promotion

Discovery ranks candidates and controls false discovery within each trunk. Confirmation tests only
the frozen primary estimand of a promoted card, plus its named null and strongest rival. Allocate
confirmation worlds before discovery. Hash the full estimator, preprocessing, factor levels,
exclusion rules, threshold, and report template before opening confirmation.

### 6.4 Pursuit and warrant ledgers

Maintain separate ledgers:

- pursuit: `OPENED`, `PROMISING`, `STALLED`, `EXHAUSTED`, `PROMOTE`;
- warrant: `DESCRIPTIVE ONLY`, `ANALOGUE EVIDENCE`, `MECHANISM CANDIDATE`,
  `CONFIRMATORY SUPPORT`, `BOUNDARY ESTABLISHED`, `INSTRUMENT FAILED`, `VOID`,
  `RESOURCE BLOCKED`.

## 9. Trunk C — common prior and nested similarity

All sixteen cards are mandatory. The primary target is an interaction among prior route, relevant
similarity, and evidence dose. A pooled average cannot resolve this trunk.

| id | question | required construction and controls | required outcome or closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Can each reader measure its own production model? | Reader produces in every common family and two domains; hold out choices and transitions | Self model beats frequency and group priors on held-out self behavior without identity labels |
| C02 | Does the nested similarity ruler recover each planted level? | One-factor and crossed differences at common, group, expertise, individual, state, and surface levels | Monotone recovery per level; surface similarity separately identified |
| C03 | What does a common-substrate prior buy over a broad population? | Same evidence and compute; within-common population versus all-family population | Gain, calibration, and evidence savings by target level; no use of `human` terminology |

## 19. Promotion and strong closure criteria

### 19.1 Promotion families

V13 has five possible constructed-world “flight” results:

1. **Nested common-ground law.** A common or local prior selectively improves efficiency,
   calibration, or continuation prediction inside its relevant basin; target evidence corrects it
   outside that basin; the effect survives fair local controls, fresh families, and false
   similarity.
2. **Cost-aware maker inference.** A factored cost-and-opportunity reader separates multiple cost
   causes, predicts counterfactual and cross-domain choices, responds to menu salience, and remains
   calibrated when menus are incomplete.
3. **Attention as safe allocation.** A finite-budget attention policy improves information per cost,
   reallocates under surprise, survives adversarial salience, and does not create confidence in
   null worlds.
4. **Factored epistemic vigilance.** Communicative goal, source reliability, content evidence, and
   uptake remain separable; false context produces conflict or abstention rather than obedient
   replacement; the architecture transfers across sources.
5. **Readable interaction hand.** Role-relative interaction traces distinguish a true controller
   from an exact shared-brief rival, predict the next intervention, survive crossed subordinates
   and rewrite, and abstain on identical artifacts.

Every flight remains a constructed engineering result. None is human empathy evidence.

### 19.2 Confidence-reducing outcomes

The following reduce confidence in the associated mechanism rather than being re-described as an
implementation inconvenience:

- an equal local prior, common prior, and broad population all perform identically across valid
  similarity and evidence ranges;
- local priors produce projection but target evidence cannot correct them without an oracle label;
- cost-aware inference fits history yet does not predict changed costs, domains, or roles;
- opportunity salience does not alter a planted neglecting reader despite a live manipulation;
- attention policies add no information, save no cost, and cannot recover from salience attacks;
- factored trust loses to an outcome-only or scalar reader on fresh sources and false context;
- central and distributed histories remain indistinguishable even with interaction records that
  an oracle can use;
- active probe sets are live but exact information selection does not beat passive or random
  observation;
- every promoted effect vanishes when the causal variable is preserved and surface or label is
  changed.

### 19.3 Curator interruption

Do not interrupt the curator for ordinary positives, nulls, closures, repairs, runtime tier
selection, or branch completion. Interrupt only if:

- a result would justify changing the constructed-world ontology before remaining cards can be
  interpreted;
- a required action is destructive, paid, externally communicative, or needs new credentials;
- a resource block makes the 132-card program impossible under every frozen tier;
- a human, neural, or real-world claim appears unavoidable to describe the result, which should be
  rare and is probably a wording error.

## 22. Research anchors and how they constrain V13

These sources motivate competing constructions. They do not override local gates or turn a
simulation into human evidence.

### Inverse planning, costs, and opportunities

- [Baker, Saxe, and Tenenbaum — action understanding as inverse planning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19729154/)
- [Jara-Ettinger et al. — the Naïve Utility Calculus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27388875/)
- [Jern, Lucas, and Kemp — inverse decision making and choice sets](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5572562/)
- [Aboody, Zhou, and Jara-Ettinger — information gain and information cost](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33739438/)
- [Hu, Moon, and VanEpps — choice-set-size neglect and attention manipulations](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09567976251400333)
- [Inzlicht, Shenhav, and Olivola — effort is both costly and valued](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6172040/)
- [Ziano et al. — mixed preregistered replications of the effort heuristic](https://online.ucpress.edu/collabra/article/9/1/87489/197632/The-Effort-Heuristic-Revisited-Mixed-Results-for)

The direct design consequence is that cost must have rival causes, menu information must be
manipulated separately from choice outcomes, and human-like underweighting must compete against
ideal and resource-bounded readers.

### Self-projection and correction

- [Epley et al. — egocentric anchoring and adjustment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15382983/)
- [Zhao, Sened, and Tamir — egocentric projection as a conditionally rational strategy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10473870/)
- [Payne, Bird, and Catmur — target-specific feedback improves mental-state inference](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/17470218251404419)
- [Eyal et al. — perspective taking versus obtaining target information](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29620401/)

The consequence is a local prior with similarity-contingent utility, insufficient adjustment as a
measurable failure, and target-specific evidence and feedback as candidate corrections. V13 must
not assume that more imaginative narration improves accuracy.

### Attention and epistemic vigilance

- [Feldman and Friston — attention as precision inference](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2010.00215/full)
- [Sperber et al. — epistemic vigilance toward source and content](https://dan.sperber.fr/wp-content/uploads/EpistemicVigilance.pdf)
- [Johnson, Hashtroudi, and Lindsay — source monitoring](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8346328/)

Precision weighting is one formal construction, not a settled human mechanism. Source reliability,
content support, and attention allocation remain separate variables.

### Communication and teaching

- [Shafto, Goodman, and Griffiths — pedagogical reasoning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogpsych.2013.12.004)
- [Goodman and Frank — pragmatic language interpretation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27868183/)
- [Yoon, Tessler, Goodman, and Frank — competing communicative goals in polite speech](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7672308/)

The consequence is to represent helpfulness, persuasion, self-presentation, and misleading as
goals that alter evidence selection, not as one trustworthy-maker bit.

### Collective production and hierarchy

- [Mathieu et al. — shared mental models and team performance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10783543/)
- [Hutchins — distributed cognition](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262082310/cognition-in-the-wild/)
- [Sobrepere and Greve — goal hierarchies as interacting instrumental structures](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/joms.13202)

The consequence is an exact shared-brief rival, role-relative goal levels, interaction traces, and
distributed leadership. Global coherence alone does not identify a central actor.

### PyMDP

- [Official PyMDP documentation](https://pymdp-rtd.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)
- [Heins et al. — PyMDP software paper](https://joss.theoj.org/papers/10.21105/joss.04098)

Current PyMDP is JAX-first and supports batched workflows, while this repository deliberately pins
the legacy NumPy API. V13 preserves the existing solver and uses exact calibration rather than
silently migrating.

## 23. Pre-mortem

V13 will produce a persuasive but worthless packet if any of these occur:

1. **Self wins because the generic prior is farther from truth.** Joint matching and sensitivity
   bounds are mandatory.
2. **A broad random population stands in for humanity.** Nested common structure is the object.
3. **Commonality is a label.** Cross true similarity, claimed similarity, and surface resemblance.
4. **Attention writes the answer.** It may select or weight evidence, never inject truth.
5. **Cost becomes motivation by definition.** Competence, knowledge, constraint, risk, obligation,
   and error must compete.
6. **Menu size replaces opportunity.** Menu composition and counterfactual utilities matter.
7. **A human limitation is treated as the normative optimum.** Compare human-mimic, exact, and
   misspecified readers.
8. **Projection is called empathy.** Correction and calibration are part of the target pattern.
9. **Helpfulness becomes reliability.** Goal, source, content, and uptake remain factored.
10. **Trust becomes one personality scalar.** Test multiple dynamics and do not diagnose the
    curator from introspection.
11. **A director is planted through random subordinates.** The shared-brief rival must share the
    same dependency law.
12. **Coherence becomes control.** Actor attribution requires interaction evidence.
13. **Same artifact gets different histories anyway.** Equifinality requires abstention.
14. **PyMDP decorates a passive result.** Use it only for active policies and compare exact.
15. **Global averages erase the mechanism.** Conditional sign reversals are primary.
16. **One maker emits thousands of independent rows.** Aggregate at the true independent unit.
17. **The coding agent builds only roots.** The expected-cell validator counts every card and cell.
18. **Duration is faked.** Only useful independent expansion is allowed.
19. **Worker CPU is mismeasured.** Account for the process tree.
20. **Fresh-clone validation depends on ignored markers.** Use the committed completion ledger.
21. **A simulator result enters Sounding Line as data.** L exports hypotheses and rulers only.
22. **A model is said to have empathy, attention, trust, or values.** Use operational reader terms.

correction, trust factorization, the exact shared-brief control, prospective prediction, attacks,
and confirmation, V13 is not complete.
