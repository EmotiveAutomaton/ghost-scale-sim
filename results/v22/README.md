V22 tests seven finite constructed mechanisms under the
[version protocol](../../docs/versions/v22-mechanism-tests/PROTOCOL.md).

The opening setup validates instruments; it is not a finding about the seven
scientific claims. Source-bound scientific packets retain raw public observations,
separate evaluator truth, training/validation sets, learned weights, all forecasts,
per-target contrasts, independent checks, full replays and CPU records in the local
campaign archive. Public summaries and hashes are written here after review.

Use CURRENT_STATUS.json as a dated snapshot. A PLAN means admitted, COMPLETE
means numerical checks and full replay passed, and a named scientific report
means the interpretation and full documentary record have been reviewed.
