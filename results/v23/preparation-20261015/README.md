This is an implementation and engineering record, not a new family nomination.
See the [report](../../../docs/versions/v23-research-division/PREPARATION_REPORT.md).

- `CONTROLS.json`: 63 distinct final scoped controls, exact test identities and
  hashes of the retained local test reports. Earlier failed attempts remain charged.
- `QUEUE_SNAPSHOT.json`: timestamped packet and controller states, reservations,
  complete/replay hashes and charged-cost bound. Replay completion alone is not
  independent numerical acceptance or a scientific decision.
- `TWIN_VALIDITY.json`: source and complete-replay bindings for eight fixtures.
- `twin-engineering/`: all 145 retained synthetic scientific products from the
  original pass, with COMPLETE and REPLAY receipts. Evaluator truth is separate
  from public observations. The local evaluator key and machine paths are omitted.

Full twin pipelines are not admitted; these illustrative shapes do not permit a
real-shape citation. Native engine transport verifies interoperability with the
pinned committed baseline, not scientific superiority or human intent.
