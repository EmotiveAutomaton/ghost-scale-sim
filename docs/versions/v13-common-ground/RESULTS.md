# V13 — Common Ground. Results

**Skeleton written before any scientific number was read (2026-08-25); Pass A written
2026-08-29 after the program reported idle, the full validator returned 0 and the fresh-clone
receipt was green.** Every number below is read from a verdict under
`results/validation/soundingline/v13/`; the generated table of every card is
[`RESULTS_PACKET.md`](RESULTS_PACKET.md), the Sounding Line ledger is
[`BRIDGE_PACKET.md`](BRIDGE_PACKET.md). The program, its criteria and its workload were locked
before any result was seen (`results/v13/prereg_v13_lock.json`, `WORKLOAD_LOCK.json`); the
changes made after data are listed under *Deviations after data* and nowhere else. Spec:
[`V13_SPEC.md`](V13_SPEC.md); implementation translation: [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md).

The record: 132 mandatory cards and the 20-attack matrix, all resolved. 123 landed, 9 closed as
instrument failures, none void, none resource-blocked. 71 promoted cards were re-run on the
untouched confirmation lineage: 69 held; two (A09, O11) held their criteria but failed a control
of their own at confirmation and are reported as withheld. Every attack card landed.

Everything here is a property of a constructed world. The claim ceiling on every card is
**method**, **constructed mechanism** or **boundary**; nothing below is a claim about people.

## Pass A — read first

### 1. Where the project world moved

**The self-first reader's advantage survives a truly equal local prior, and it is smaller than
V12 said.** V13 was built around one question: can a bounded reader use common structure,
attention, costs, communicative goals and production roles to narrow a maker model more
efficiently and more safely than either generic inverse planning or uncorrected self-projection?
The first thing it did was rebuild V12's headline against a fairer rival. V12 compared the
self-first prior with a generic prior matched on entropy alone; V13's identity trunk showed that
prior sat measurably closer to the truth than the self prior it was compared with, and that
matching on that distance as well removes about half of V12's near-maker gain (+0.72 nats
becomes +0.37). Against a non-self prior matched on both entropy and expected distance to the
truth — the "equally local" rival the spec demanded — the self prior still scores +0.26 nats
better on the makers nearest the reader after one artifact, −0.16 on the farthest, and +1.21 on
makers built to be the reader's opposite; pooled over everyone the difference is +0.31. That
shape held on the confirmation lineage and reappeared on fresh families to the second decimal
(+0.26 near, −0.16 far). Under the attack that rematches the rival on both quantities the pooled
gain narrows from +0.38 to +0.12; the near bin is the surviving region. Under false similarity
the gain vanishes (+0.39 to +0.02), which is the right direction: perceived commonality does not
create projection. On the maker's *hidden next goal* the self prior buys almost nothing (+0.04
near, −0.03 far). What V13 could not read is the layer below: the two cards asking what a
*common substrate* prior buys over a broad population both failed their own controls at full
scale and are withheld, so the "common ground" the version is named for has a locality result
and no substrate result.

**Trusting a claimed group is the most expensive mistake in the program.** Using a maker's
claimed group as a prior is worth +1.34 nats when the claim is true and the convention shared,
and −24.5 when only the claim matches. Ninety-seven percent of what a maker's own history buys
(+0.19 nats from eight artifacts) is the target's own; a same-label twin's history buys +0.01.
Carrying a source's learned reliability to a namesake costs −1.80. The reader pays for labels
exactly where it should.

**Pooling readers hurts.** Four readers pooling their posteriors scored −1.15 nats *below* the
best single reader when their errors were independent and −1.91 when they shared a false note;
the correlated pool's confidence exceeded its accuracy by +0.33 against +0.13. Exchanging
posteriors gained +0.53 nats in one construction but pushed calibration error from 0.30 to 0.48.
The spec's worry that agreement masquerades as confirmation was confirmed in the ensemble's
calibration, not in its accuracy.

**Learned precision is worth a great deal; safe allocation was not shown.** On identical
evidence, learned channel weights scored +14.1 nats over uniform weighting and wrong weights
−2.9, with the wrong weighting confidently wrong 26% of the time against 9%. Under adversarial
salience the salience-driven reader lost 1.2 nats and scored 6.5 below a reader using learned cue
reliability, and that margin survived the attack built to hijack it. But two of the flight's
requirements failed: an adaptive reader that reallocates under surprise kept 0.03 of its mass on
its own profile against 0.04 for a fixed reader — no gain; and in a null world, counting the
surface twice raised confidence by +0.20 with no change in accuracy, the planted failure. A
precision reader that can be handed the same evidence twice is not safe, and the bridge card that
would have exported attention to text is killed on exactly that ground.

**Costs identify their causes only when the cost model is right.** With the scalar total held
fixed, the factored reader named the weighted cost dimension 79% of the time and predicted
held-out choices +0.66 nats better than a total-cost reader; motivation and competence were
separated 67% against 25%; imposed work supplied zero preference evidence to a reader that read
the record and 1.02 nats to one that did not. Recovered tradeoffs predicted four of five
prospective targets (+0.7 to +1.1 nats) and failed the fifth (a new role, −0.28), so the
prospective criterion failed. Under a hidden or false choice set the effect grew (+0.50 to
+1.20); under a *misspecified cost function* it reversed (+0.49 to −0.40): a wrong cost model
becomes confident wrong attribution. Calibration under incomplete menus did not improve (0.17
against 0.16). An exact reader beat the planted choice-set-neglect heuristic by +0.01 nats — the
"super-empathic idealized reader" the spec forbade claiming does not exist here to claim.

**Communicative goals, source reliability, content and uptake stay separate, and the reader
reads all four.** A maker's stance was named 85% of the time from twelve artifacts whose claims'
truths were revealed, with a surface classifier at 16%; a false note left 2% of the mass on the
wrong side against strong evidence and 34% against weak, where a reader that took the note as
truth was wrong every time. Belief moved with the source's truthfulness (+0.80) and not with its
alignment (0.00); preference moved with alignment (+0.64). Apology restored nothing; costly
action +0.13 and repeated reliability +0.17. On fresh sources the architecture held (84%
accuracy) and recovered from a reversal in six claims at 54% accuracy. Two attacks narrowed it:
false biography and context cut the vigilance gain from +0.71 to +0.14, and a regime switch from
+0.35 to +0.10. The reader is not overridden by an assertion, but an assertion costs it most of
its margin.

**The interaction hand is readable from records and from nothing else.** A director and an
exactly matched shared brief — same subordinates, proposals, corrections and artifacts, differing
only in who issued each correction — were told apart 100% of the time by the interaction reader
and 50% by every artifact-only and coherence reader; every record level short of the full log
sat at chance. The project goal survived a global rewrite 98% of the time; subordinate style 25%.
Attribution followed the subordinate's style in at least 84% of teams in every crossed cell and
followed new roles 100% after a swap. The result held on fresh team scales and domains at 100%,
survived its strongest distributed rival, died under a shuffled policy (it follows the policy,
not the labels) and died under equifinal histories (the reader abstains). Predicting the *next*
intervention failed: the graph model scored −1.08 against −0.98 for the best baseline.

**Active reading: exact selection works where a probe changes what can be seen; the present
PyMDP reader does not agree with it often enough.** Inferring the maker's attention allocation
(78% from six artifacts) and inspecting a channel it did not sharpen gained +0.09 nats; a
goal-aware challenge gained +0.54 over passive reading, and an adversary who anticipated it took
8.7 nats of that away; the exact stopping rule lost 0.08 nats to the frontier at the middle
price. Commissions were again nearly inert as probes (+0.02 against +0.43 for a free look), as in
V12. PyMDP agreed with the exact probe 61% of the time in discovery and 70% on fresh families
against bars of 80% and 70%. The Q trunk's own closure rule — close the present PyMDP reader if
exact succeeds and PyMDP fails — is triggered.

**The program ran in 55 wall-clock hours against a 94-hour forecast** (630 CPU-hours over twelve
below-normal workers), with four silent process deaths, each resumed by the watchdog from
checkpoints with ledger growth. Nine of 132 mandatory cards closed as instrument failures at full
scale after passing the six-world smoke; none was repaired after data, and the record keeps them
as refusals to report, not as nulls.

### 2. The five strongest results or boundaries

1. **Self privilege is real, selective, and half the size V12 reported.** +0.26 nats near, −0.16
   far, against a rival matched on entropy and on distance to the truth; confirmed; identical on
   fresh families; narrowed by fair rematching to a pooled +0.12; abolished by false similarity.
   (Constructed mechanism; the correction to V12 is a method result.)
2. **A believed group claim is worth −24.5 nats when only the claim matches**, +1.34 when the
   convention is really shared; a namesake's reliability transfers as −1.80. (Constructed
   mechanism.)
3. **Ensembles of readers lose to their best member and become overconfident**, more so when
   their errors are correlated. (Constructed mechanism; matches the spec's correlated-ensemble
   attack, which the other flights survived.)
4. **The interaction hand is a records-dominant variable.** Full interaction log 100%, every
   artifact-level and partial-record reader at 50%, abstention on identical artifacts, death under
   equifinal history. (Boundary, confirmed and transferred.)
5. **A precision reader is not safe against duplicated evidence, and a cost reader is not safe
   against a wrong cost model.** Double counting inflates confidence +0.20 at zero accuracy;
   misspecification reverses the cost-aware advantage to −0.40. (Boundaries; both were the
   spec's named confidence-reducing outcomes.)

### 3. Which competing explanations gained or lost probability

- **Locality explains most of self-first, but not all of it.** The equal-local rival ties the
  self prior on the hidden next goal and pooled over makers after rematching, and loses to it in
  the near bin every time it was measured. *Gained:* self-first as correspondence in the near
  bin. *Lost:* self-first as a general advantage; V12's number as stated.
- **The common substrate as a mechanism: unread.** Both cards withheld. Neither gained nor lost;
  the closure rule ("close only if neither common nor local priors improve anything after one
  repair") has not been reached because the repair has not been made.
- **Attention as a shortcut versus attention as allocation.** Learned precision's +14 nats and
  its survival under adversarial salience favour allocation; the failed reallocation and the
  double-counting inflation say the allocation is not self-protecting. *Gained:* attention as an
  entry-efficiency heuristic with a named unsafe region. *Lost:* "safe allocation" as a flight.
- **Cost as motive.** The spec's rival — that a cost vector cannot identify its causes — lost
  where the cost model is correct (79%, 67%, 0.000 for imposed work) and won under
  misspecification. The equivalence-class reading survives only for the weighting family (linear
  and its neighbours within 0.01 nats).
- **Trust as a scalar.** Lost: the factored reader kept 83% of its mass on the truth against 73%
  for a single-trust-number reader under contrary history, and belief and preference moved on
  different levers. But the confirmation of that card failed its calibration control, so the
  margin is withheld at confirmation and stands on discovery only.
- **Coherence as the signature of a director.** Lost again, more cleanly than in V12: coherence
  at exactly 50% against an exact shared-brief twin.
- **PyMDP as more than decoration.** Lost at this construction: exact selection earns its keep
  in three constructions; PyMDP agrees with it below both bars.

### 4. What should enter Sounding Line next

The bridge ledger ([`BRIDGE_PACKET.md`](BRIDGE_PACKET.md)) is mechanical: an item is pursued
only where every licensing card landed with its criterion held. Three pursue, three defer, six
are killed. The controls are exact:

- **Pursue — communicative goals in the reader (L02).** Extend the probe schema's reader
  posterior from an audience distribution to a goal distribution with separate reliability and
  content fields. Records: claims with eventually revealed truths, source histories by domain.
  Baseline: a helpfulness or valence score. Positive gate: a constructed corpus where sources
  with planted goals are surface-matched and the goal is recovered only when truths are revealed.
  Withdraw if goal recovery in text needs an unmatched surface cue.
- **Pursue — partitioning unexplained activity (L06).** Anomaly axes (origin, handling,
  awareness) in the process record. Baseline: label every unexplained span a mistake. Gate: the
  reader abstains on unhandled anomalies and separates handled ones on a planted corpus. The
  abstention rule transfers; the recovery rates do not.
- **Pursue — resolution required before any topology score (L08).** An admissibility check at
  the stated block and event counts, from the record-level ladder. Gate: the ruler reaches its
  validated accuracy at the stated resolution on a planted corpus; below it the score is a number
  without a ruler.
- **Defer** until the records exist: the controller–subordinate ruler (needs actor-attributed
  correction events; only the artifact-floor result transfers), the factored trust gate (needs
  revealed claim truths; the scalar stays until the factored reader beats it on fresh sources),
  and the artifact-only versus record-dominant table.
- **Killed:** cost-aware decision records (text carries no per-alternative costs), attention
  manipulations (text cue families overlap — the double-counting failure above is the reason),
  nested similarity (no text analogue of the matched prior yet, and the substrate cards are
  withheld), purpose-first testing, and standardized author-purpose questions.

### 5. What remains unmeasured

*This section is not shortened to make room for results.*

- **No human anywhere.** Nothing here says that people project, allocate attention, read costs,
  gate trust, or attribute a director; every effect is a consequence of a declared world and a
  declared reader.
- **The common substrate.** The simulated substrate is a factorization of a constructed goal
  space. It is not a limbic system, an affective system, embodiment or culture, and the two cards
  that would have measured what it buys are withheld.
- **PyMDP** has no empathy, attention, trust or care; here it has a 61–70% agreement rate with
  an exact policy and nothing else.
- **Cost reveals no single motive.** The factored reader identifies planted causes given the
  correct cost model; under a wrong model it attributes confidently and wrongly. Nothing in the
  program measures which cost model a person or a text corpus obeys.
- **Coherence does not identify a director, and an artifact does not identify its history.**
  Both were shown at floor; a text corpus without operation records has only the floor.
- **Text transfer is a prediction, not a result.** Every bridge item is a gate to run in
  Sounding Line, not a claim about it.
- **The "idealized reader" is not super-empathic.** It beat the planted neglect heuristic by
  +0.01 nats on complete menus and tied it on incomplete ones.
- **Inside the program:** the correction curve under a truly matched control (the V12
  reproduction card failed its own control: near makers needed *more* correction than far ones,
  the opposite of the expectation, and its calibration gate failed); the nested similarity ruler
  at two of five levels; the routes-converge placebo; the forgone-alternative live gate; the
  purpose-first entry control; the anti-similarity transform placebo; and PyMDP agreement on fresh
  families. Each is a withheld reading, listed in Pass B with its failed control.
- **Engineering, not science:** the four silent process deaths have no established cause.

### 6. Questions for the curator

Only where the answer changes the next branch.

1. **Repair and re-run the withheld instruments, or leave them withheld?** Eight discovery cards
   (C02, C03, C05, C12, A02, A06, O04, P01), one transfer card (Q12) and two confirmations (A09,
   O11) failed their own controls at full scale. Two of them are the only readings of the common
   substrate mechanism (C03, C05) and one is the V12 correction curve under the matched control
   (P01). The C-trunk closure rule allows one repair before closure. Repairing after data is an
   instrument recalibration, disclosed, with the failed verdict kept beside the replacement, as
   the five healing re-runs were; it is also 5–10 hours of compute and a second amendment to the
   frozen confirmation packet. My recommendation is to repair C03, C05 and P01 only.
2. **Amend the report interface's lane blind spot?** `runners/report_v13.py` is hash-locked and
   reads discovery verdicts only, so it prints "not run" for the five transfer-only cards (A14,
   C16, G16, H16, Q12), all of which resolved in the transfer lane and are in the ledger. Fixing
   it is a lock amendment. My recommendation is to leave it and let this note stand.

### 7. Recommendation

**Retain selected rulers; continue Ghost only on the withheld readings.** Retain as validated:
the equal-local matched prior and the near-bin self privilege it isolates; the factored
vigilance architecture as a reader architecture; the interaction-hand rulers with their
records-dominant boundary; the cost-cause separation with its misspecification boundary; the
abstention rules (identical artifacts, equifinal pairs, unhandled anomalies). Drop the present
PyMDP reader by its own closure rule. Do not carry "attention as safe allocation" as a flight;
carry learned precision as an entry-efficiency heuristic with the double-counting boundary
attached. Send the three pursued bridges to Sounding Line as written. The only reason to run
more Ghost compute is question 1 above; nothing else in the program is waiting on a world that
has not been built.
