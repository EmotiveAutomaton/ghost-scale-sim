# K, N, E and F prospective implementation choices

These choices fill unspecified timing and reader details before scientific
admission. All maps are descriptive constructed mechanisms, architecture
untested. Each cell has a forty-world excluded pilot, 200 main worlds in eight
blocks, complete replay, independent reconstruction and known answers. Resource
limits stop at the last verified cell. E/F use their separate three-hour pools.

K runs 3,000 steps. A new gap is planned immediately, consuming that step.
Otherwise attention work has priority; if it does no work, the oldest planned
gap with a caught open window gets one unit. At most one work unit per step.
A deadline restores stress once at age 60 and adds one cost of three. Stress
is measured before that step's planning/work; a new gap therefore contributes
during its planning step. NOPLAN deadline replicas are matched controls.
Unequal-work textures cycle gap sizes, holding all counter-based random draws
paired. Completion-time estimates use Kaplan–Meier with each unfinished gap
censored at its own observed age and whole-maker-block bootstrap intervals;
completed-gap means are labelled conditional. The attention-only reader is a
separate extension, not supplied by these payoff maps.

N runs 1,024 steps. Arrivals occur before selection; lowest-index ties are
deterministic. REACHABLE falls back to the largest gap if no way is open.
A watcher sees only the selected need. Its exact 343-state filter marginalizes
unseen arrivals and current openings; an independent sparse-matrix recurrence
reconstructs the entire path. All-open cells are exact-alias controls and
excluded from discovery. Raw likelihood infinities are retained.

E uses B2-style beta evidence with ten prior pseudo-observations, one harmful.
Training A has 64 or 256 exposures with harm probability one half. Extinction B
has 16, 64 or 256 harmless exposures. UNLEARN pools these observations.
BRAKE retains the A association and subtracts a context-weighted inhibition
chosen to match UNLEARN exactly in B. Similarities to B are s in A, one in B,
and s squared in C; s is 0, .25, .5, .75 or one. Testing has 128 observations in
each context, frozen beliefs and B2's clipped logistic alarm response.
Renewal outside B is fixed by these definitions; its detectability is open.
The reader observes training harm count and test contexts. Identical-context
cells are aliases. This is a declared extension, not an unchanged B2 rerun.

F starts the priced rule at weight 64, matching RULE-OWN's tremble across
the public prices. There are 128 baseline steps, then enforcement for 16,
64 or 256 steps, then 512 post-departure steps. Enforcement probability is
0, .25, .75 or one; fine is 4, 16 or 32; drift is .01, .05 or .2.
After baseline each step updates the weight toward current probability
times fine, including toward zero after departure. The fine enters the
weight once and is actually levied on punished sales. Plenty, MIXED,
no buying, initial stock eight, no replenishment. The reader observes
money, need, offers, actions and the enforcement schedule. Timing, stock
depletion and the assumed continuing drift are explicit mechanism limits.
