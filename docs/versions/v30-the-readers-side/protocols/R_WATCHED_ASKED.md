whole-world blocks. Watched records include every one of 3,000 clock steps;
separation means the true reason leads every rival by three natural-log units

truth with probability q and otherwise repeats that claim. Staged actions and
HEAT are observed actions, not q-corrupted answers. All these choices are
prospective and separate from human evidence.
