# V hidden-ledger reader admission

BLIND receives only its own invitations and binary venture outcomes. RECORD also
receives public offer occurrence, offered price and accept/refuse. Neither sees
actual money, need, stock, private temptation, spending or physical random draws.
STATE and INSIDE remain separately admitted visible arms. Venture outcomes in
the hidden arms mean cooperation/defection, not the agent's private monetary gain.

The hidden reader retains X3's finite candidate roster and class-balanced prior.
A bootstrap filter per candidate propagates income, need, unobserved public
activity and spending, conditions on the permitted observations, marginalizes
private temptation prices, and resamples below half its particle count.
Candidate parameters are not resampled away. BACKFIRE timing preserves X3's
pre-offer shutdown convention for the current private opportunity.
MIMIC is evaluator-only and off the reader roster, as LIVE20 already was.

Freeze population ladder 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, 2048. Four retained full-record
fixtures cross BLIND/RECORD with RULE/plentiful/LOW/no-tail/loss2 and
MIMIC/scarce/MIXED/tail.1/loss10, four worlds each. Adjacent levels must change
forecasts by mean at most .01, 95th percentile .025, maximum .10, and alter
invitation decisions on at most 1% of steps. These are finite numerical checks,
not a universal accuracy claim. Stop if the ladder or 1,800-CPU-second check
allowance is exhausted. No production hidden arm is admitted on a failed gate.

Full replay, independent choice-probability arithmetic and retained observable
paths check each cell. Explicit attacks alter hidden ledger fields, private
prices and physical uniforms without changing reader outputs. State propagation
and resampling conventions are shared between implementations; this limitation
must accompany any result. Both visible and hidden arms share V's eight-hour cap.
