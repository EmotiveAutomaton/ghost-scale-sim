## What merits the next week

The strongest follow-up is a harder persistent-state test where useful evidence is
limited, policies genuinely change, and observation access is uncertain. Keep an
equally informed direct calculation and the cheap task compiler. The present
small state space makes exact inference a sufficient, inexpensive rival.

For learned representations, investigate why compact split/flat readers lose to
that rival before scaling the same fits. Public role alignment helps, but it does
not supply evidence for psychologically identified coordinates. More varied
training evidence and an explicit generalization question are more useful here
than additional seeds in the unchanged failed cell.

The theory's targets remain separate: a program can enact technique without
recovering its original history; current purpose can change while expertise
persists; a numerical tradeoff is only this generator's preference parameter.
Neither calibrated ambiguity, selective transfer nor the population counterexample
establishes human values, consciousness or a universal anti-capture guarantee.
