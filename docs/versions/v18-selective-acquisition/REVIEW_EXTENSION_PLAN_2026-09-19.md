## The question worth the remaining time

Can a reader build a useful, revisable model of a maker when evidence is costly,
the maker changes, sources repeat one another, and the reader's own purpose changes?

The most useful extension connects three outcomes that must remain separately
measured: predicting the maker, reconstructing what actually happened, and learning
something the reader can enact. A reconstruction can serve one without serving
the others. The reader's purpose may therefore determine both which evidence is
worth seeking and which distinctions its memory must preserve.

These are analyst proposals derived from the existing framework, not amendments
to the curator's theory. They concern methods and mechanisms in constructed
worlds. They do not establish human psychology, neural anatomy, or human values.

## What the current record already establishes

V18.2 completed 25 packets: 21 current discovery packets and four retained
superseded originals. History improved prediction when current maker state was
hidden. The small learned split and flat encoders did not reliably outperform
the direct predictor, and the exact finite posterior remained a strong rival.
Selective procedure acquisition helped particular bounded learners, while a
public-law compiler solved the construction controls. Model-family expansion
restored support, but better latest-event fit did not guarantee better future
prediction. These are reasons to make the next comparisons harder, not to rerun
the same worlds with more seeds.

Earlier versions already tested acquired craft, goal changes, paid queries,
source dependence, and restricted hypothesis families. Sounding Line has also
compared exact persistent state with full-history inference. Those comparisons
are inherited controls. The proposed additions are uncertain access, unknown
change times, transfer between reader purposes, imperfect source knowledge,
genuinely different decision rules, and fair computational comparisons.

## Six connected studies

The table states each proposed question, its new comparison, and the review
decision it could inform. Priorities describe implementation order, not an
obligation to consume the entire compute allowance.

| Study | New comparison | What tomorrow's review would learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Evidence for the reader's purpose | Select observations for prediction, historical discrimination, or useful enactment; include failed access | Whether useful inquiry depends on the reader's purpose and actual access |
| 2. Old skill, new purpose | Real acquisition followed by unknown goal, belief, tool, or opportunity changes | What should persist, what should update, and what evidence separates those cases |
| 3. Independent correction versus repetition | Unknown or partly known copying structure and selective reporting | When apparent corroboration creates false certainty, and what corrects it |
| 4. Confidently wrong explanations | Reweighting versus bounded family revision, assessed on untouched future evidence | When a coherent explanation earns trust or merely absorbs anomalies |

### 1. Evidence for the reader's purpose

Give several readers the same initial artifacts and the same observation menu.
One wants to predict the maker's next action; another wants to discriminate
between historical processes; another wants to learn a technique for a different
task. Allow observations that distinguish these objectives: a failed attempt,
an opportunity declined, a changed tool, a private-information reveal, or a
demonstration of a reusable procedure.

Compare task-directed selection with general uncertainty reduction, a fixed
diagnostic menu, random selection, and no purchase. Give a direct predictor the
same realized observations. Separate the selector's contribution from the
inference method's contribution; a sophisticated selector cannot claim credit
for evidence withheld from its rival.

An attempted query can fail or be unavailable. Match attempted queries rather
than successful responses, retain nonresponse explicitly, and charge preparation,
selection, observation, learning, and final execution. Cross uniform missingness
with query-dependent missingness and an adverse hidden-state-dependent case.
The reader does not get the evaluator's true access mechanism for free.

Judge success on separately generated future tasks and enacted outcomes, not
on the selector's own predicted utility. Include a deliberately uninformative
menu and an already-resolved case. Stopping can reflect irrelevance or cost
while uncertainty remains; it need not mean the reader is certain.

This is a constructed mechanism test of purposeful inquiry and a method test
of query selection under missing access. Published active-learning work documents
biased nonresponse and remaining failures of proposed corrections; it does not
prove a solution for the hidden-state-dependent case proposed here.
([Robinson et al., 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10618-024-01026-x))

### 2. Old skill, new purpose

Start with actual practice and acquired procedures. Change the maker's purpose
without announcing the change time. Separately change its private information,
available tools, considered opportunities, or skill. Include stationary controls,
gradual drift, and a return to an earlier goal.

Compare static memory, a short recent window, gradual forgetting, and a reader
that treats persistent craft and current purpose as different update problems.
A known-change-time reader supplies an explicitly privileged ceiling. Give the
direct chronological rival all the same observations and budget.

The important outcomes are future predictive accuracy, delay before useful
adaptation, false resets of still-useful skill, and actual performance when the
reader applies what it learned to its own goal. Merely announcing a different
goal or decoding a skill label is insufficient. A changed action may reflect a
changed opportunity rather than a changed preference.

Use observationally indistinguishable pairs as a required control: choices alone
must not identify which member generated them. Then add a discriminating
opportunity or intervention and test whether that changes recoverability.

Online change-point inference supplies a useful reference algorithm, but its
standard independence assumptions do not automatically hold for coupled slow
skill and fast goal state. The finite coupled filter would be our adaptation,
validated against enumerated cases.
([Adams and MacKay, 2007](https://lips.cs.princeton.edu/pdfs/adams2007changepoint.pdf))

### 3. Independent correction versus repetition

Build small source networks containing independent observations, copies,
shared mistakes, and truthful examples selected to support a misleading account.
Hold independent roots fixed while increasing the number of repeated reports.
Then introduce genuinely independent counterevidence.

Compare naive independent fusion, a reader with partial provenance, a reader
that must infer source dependence, and a known-source-graph ceiling. The complete
graph is evaluator knowledge unless the observation condition explicitly supplies
it. Include mistaken provenance and truly independent sources so caution can
also fail through excessive discounting.

Measure prediction, false confidence, correction after contradiction, and retained
ability to learn a useful technique. Count independent source roots and maker
lineages, not copies, as the relevant units. The desired result is an operating
boundary: when repeated agreement adds evidence, when it merely adds volume,
and when source knowledge is itself too weak to settle the question.

This elaborates context and trust as evidence-handling mechanisms. It does not
equate attention, epistemic weighting, procedure application, belief uptake, and
value change. Only the outcomes actually implemented may be claimed.

### 4. Confidently wrong explanations

Construct truth inside the reader's candidate family, near its boundary, and
outside it. A coherent but wrong explanation should be able to fit the observed
prefix. Give the reader a finite, declared revision menu containing both useful
and misleading additions, rather than always supplying the correct repair.

Compare fixed-family updating, cautious weighting, abstention, an empirical
predictor, and bounded family revision. Choose revisions using the prefix only;
assess them on withheld future observations and actions. Cross context order and
source independence. Record the cost of proposing and checking explanations.

The decisive distinction is between explaining what has already been seen and
earning accurate expectations about what comes next. Report concentrated wrong
posteriors and failed repairs, including cases where abstention is useful. Do
not reinterpret every anomaly as hidden expertise.

Adaptive selection can amplify model mismatch; robust experimental-design work
also finds query rankings sensitive to priors. A small declared ensemble of
alternative priors and access assumptions is a feasible stress test, without
claiming to reproduce a published robust optimum.
([Sloman et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13698);
[Go and Isaac, 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v180/go22a.html))

A 2025 model-discrepancy study provides a recent example of combining discrepancy
learning with experimental design. Its physical inverse problems motivate a
comparison; they do not supply evidence about maker inference, and its full
numerical stack is unnecessary here.
([Yang, Chen and Wu, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05372))

### 5. Memory that transfers

Compare full-history recomputation, the exact finite posterior, a candidate
summary of future-test predictions, a strong direct history reader, and compact
flat versus role-organized learned state. The direct learned reader receives
the entire permitted history within the declared envelope. Match training data,
selection effort, parameter budgets where meaningful, and measurement conditions.

Evaluate on new maker lineages, withheld combinations with balanced marginal
and pairwise coverage, and a new query family. Ask whether a memory adequate for
predicting the maker also preserves what the reader needs to enact a technique
or distinguish histories after its purpose changes.

An exact predictive summary is a control only after its coverage and update
closure have been checked. An arbitrary bank of future questions is not
automatically a sufficient state. When the summary fails but the full posterior
succeeds, the summary lost information. When both lack identifying evidence,
the problem is evidence availability. When exact and simple fitted methods work
but neural models fail, optimization, capacity, or sample efficiency remains the
live explanation.

Report accuracy together with memory, total training work, per-update work, and
the number of subsequent uses needed to repay preparation. Equal predictions
with lower total cost can be a useful result; exact cache equality by itself is
already known. A role split has to earn its advantage against equally informed
rivals. Unsupervised coordinates are not uniquely identified merely because a
probe decodes the intended label.
([Littman, Sutton and Singh, 2001](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/1983-predictive-representations-of-state.pdf);
[Locatello et al., 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/locatello19a.html))

### 6. Different kinds of worlds

Build architectural severity into the preceding studies. Change the maker's
decision rule, learning mechanism, opportunity selection, or observation process;
do not count a new seed or label permutation as a new architecture.

Begin with one complete four-factor, two-level design of 16 cells whose factors
are fixed before scientific outcomes are viewed. For example: compensatory
versus satisficing choice; separable versus coupled procedural acquisition;
exogenous versus maker-dependent opportunities; independent versus shared-source
error. Each mechanism needs its own known-answer construction checks.

Use common lineages for paired comparisons within a cell, then report results
by cell and the declared interactions. Additional decision rules are separate
holdouts if time permits. Do not claim unmeasured interactions from a reduced
design, or treat these 16 cells as a representative sample of all possible worlds.

The review product is a map of conditions under which each method helps, ties,
or hurts. Successful finite severity narrows an architecture limitation; it
does not remove it universally.

## Optional elaborations, admitted only after the core works

**Intervention-consistent roles.** Replace an internal skill, purpose, or belief
slot and compare behavior with the corresponding intervention in the known
generator. Include wrong mappings, shuffled targets, incompatible alternative
partitions, and admissibility checks for mixed states. Intervention-trained
models receive extra supervision: give fair rivals the same counterfactual
targets and label the comparison accordingly. This tests a declared functional
organization, not unique psychological coordinates.
([Geiger et al., 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/geiger22a.html))

**Compression boundary.** On a sufficiently small enumerated history set,
compare finite codebooks by retained future-prediction quality and storage.
Claim an exact optimum only when exhaustive enumeration or a valid certificate
supports it. An iterative information-bottleneck fit is not automatically a
global optimum and does not establish a neural compression mechanism.

**Repositories.** The inspected
[antra repository](https://github.com/hansonhl/antra) is an MIT-licensed reference
for intervention bookkeeping; its basic graph operations use ordinary Python.
[Pyvene's primary tool paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-demo.16/) describes
a broader PyTorch intervention library. Neither is needed for the first finite
role-swap test. The inspected
[ruptures repository](https://github.com/deepcharles/ruptures) offers BSD-licensed
offline change-point analysis; it is a possible retrospective cross-check, not
an online decision-time oracle. No repository was cloned or installed for this
report. Any later adoption must pin source and license, inspect dependencies,
and pass a local known-answer adapter check first.

## Validity contract

1. **Validate every new ruler.** Enumerated probabilities, impossible distinctions,
   uninformative queries, stationary controls, and deliberately detectable changes
   precede scientific interpretation. Instrument validity and scientific effect
   size have separate thresholds and statuses.
2. **Keep evidence fair.** Audit complete serialized reader inputs for hidden
   truth, future outcomes, solver state, and forbidden source access. Give direct
   rivals the same allowed history, queries, and counterfactual supervision.
3. **Separate development from discovery.** Freeze menus, capacities, revisions,
   thresholds, cell roster, and reporting contrasts before held-out exposure.
   Preserve failed fits and unavailable comparisons. Do not tune until positive.
4. **Count independent units correctly.** Repeated targets, fit seeds, repairs,
   and copied reports stay clustered within their original lineage or source.
   All assigned failures remain in denominators. No malformed prediction becomes
   a silently imputed valid score; zero-probability events retain infinite loss.
5. **Test calibration within and beyond the model.** Correct-model coverage checks
   validate computation under those assumptions. Separate wrong-family tests
   measure how those assumptions fail. Narrow intervals are not identification.
6. **Charge the full method.** Include failed access, preparation, selector search,
   fitting, stored state, repeated use, and final execution. Report both matched
   information and matched budget comparisons when they answer different questions.
7. **Make each packet independently inspectable.** Retain source and environment
   identities, unit-level evidence, complete accounting, independent reaggregation,
   and bounded execution replay. Verify checkpoint/resume on disposable fixtures.
   Document which fits were replayed versus retrained.
8. **Preserve provenance when healing.** Keep the failed source and output; assign
   the corrected packet a new identity and rerun every affected comparison.
   Previously valid V18.2 packets remain closed.

Default interpretation is descriptive discovery. A second fit seed is not an
independent world architecture; a fresh-seed replication is not automatically a
confirmation. Any confirmation requires an explicitly frozen claim and untouched
reserve, with sufficient remaining time and resources. It is not required merely
to decorate tomorrow's report.

## Publication and review products

Publish each complete named batch after its verification and full documentary
write-through: first the exact instruments and purposeful-evidence comparison;
then changing state, provenance, and revision; then learned memory and severity;
finally the review audit. Use ordinary commits and pushes. Partial cells remain
visible as incomplete and do not supply method rankings.

The final review should contain a short claim-and-boundary table, a map of method
wins/ties/failures across world conditions, evidence-versus-computation diagnosis,
cost/benefit curves, and six mechanically selected worked examples. Examples should
include an ambiguous history, useful enactment without historical identification,
a goal change preserving skill, copied false corroboration, a failed context repair,
and a memory that loses a distinction needed by a new reader purpose. Include
counterexamples and unavailable comparisons, not only successes.

Raw research notes remain separate from the theory store. The cited literature
was inspected at abstract or relevant-method level, not reproduced as benchmarks.
Three authorized Terra research reports informed the proposal; load-bearing
recommendations were checked against primary sources and the local record.

The core deliverable is a better boundary map: what the reader needs to observe,
what it must retain, what it can safely reuse, and what should make it revise its
account. An empty result, an exact tie, or a principled ambiguity can answer those
questions as usefully as an advantage for the proposed representation.
