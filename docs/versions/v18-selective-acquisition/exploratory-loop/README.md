# V18.4: continuing exploratory review

The owner commissioned this extension on 19 September after V18.3 undershot the
available runtime. The task is to derive new experiments from completed results
and further Terra research, run useful work through at least 20 September 05:00
PDT, and repeat analysis, design and execution whenever a queue completes.
This is adaptive discovery, not a new preregistered confirmation or a population
claim. Earlier results and their failures are immutable predecessors.

The scientific question is why useful organization sometimes transfers and
sometimes fails: missing training support, an inadequate objective or decoder,
selective updating under the wrong change process, mistaken provenance, and
compression that serves the wrong future purpose are distinct explanations.

## Execution and validity contract

- One low-priority CPU worker and one numerical thread. The installed optional
  PyTorch environment may supply an isolated CPU child while its parent waits.
- Continue reviewed exploration through at least 12:00 UTC. The inherited final
  science cutoff is 13:00 UTC and delivery is 15:00 UTC on 20 September. Carry
  prior consumption into the unchanged cumulative 64,800 CPU-second ceiling.
- Every packet freezes source, specification, inputs, environment, seeds and
  known-answer checks. Resume retains completed units and failed attempts.
- Cases use a new V18.4 lineage namespace. Pair methods within world; repeated
  queries, architecture cells and fit seeds are not independent observations.
- A mathematical/control failure withholds the affected interpretation. An
  experimental reversal is a result, never a reason to alter a gate or score.
- No desired scientific ranking is a validity gate. Independent probability,
  normalization, zero-information, exact-recovery, corruption and replay checks
  test instruments. Development selects models; test scores do not select them.
- An event review lands results in the version record, exchange, FINDINGS and
  hypothesis table plus synthesis, then commits and ordinarily pushes the batch.
- Each completion identifies remaining competing explanations and admits the
  next discriminating experiment. Queue drain is a refill event. A claim that
  useful work is exhausted requires a retained candidate-by-candidate rationale.
- Estimated durations are forecasts. Pilot measurements replace them; making
  an experiment slower or duplicating completed science is not exploration.

## Initial research map

| Family | Prior result that motivates it | New discriminating comparison |
|---|---|---|
| P: prospective compression | Old-optimal codes tie yet differ on future questions | Canonical/random/entropy tie rules versus product codes, training-purpose portfolios and worst-case training regret; untouched purpose combinations |
| L: learned support and objectives | Recurrent advantage reverses on new questions | Count-matched even/odd/full latent support, query diversity, capacity and training schedules; exact and observable-state rivals |
| U: adaptive updating | Selective updating helps goal changes but hurts stationary/skill changes | Mixtures of role-specific hazards, unknown switch patterns, equal-trace prediction and actual decisions |
| S: source investigation | Copied evidence misleads; independent correction helps conditionally | Paid independence audits versus new evidence, noisy provenance and own-policy versus common-trace comparisons |
| R: family revision | Revision helps missing rules but hurts a correct family | Selection, averaging and paid expansion under copied evidence, ordered surprises and unrepresented truth |
| I: intervention generalization | Supplied interchange training helps flat and split readers | Limited intervention coverage, joint compositions and equally informed alternative role organizations |
| C: preparation economics | Cached queries are cheap for every reader; fitting is not repaid | History reuse, changing questions, incremental updates and accuracy-matched workload costs |

These are a reviewed backlog, not claims that all implementations already exist.
Executable packet manifests and event reviews identify admitted work. Research
returns are kept outside the theory store, with load-bearing sources verified.
