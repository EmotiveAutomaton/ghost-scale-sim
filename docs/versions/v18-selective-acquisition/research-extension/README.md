## Design and scope frozen before discovery

Eight study families are required. Execution, validity, scientific outcome,
verification and publication are separate fields in the coverage ledger.

| Family | Question | Required comparisons |
|---|---|---|
| A | Does reader purpose change useful investigation? | Prediction, historical-state discrimination and enactment selectors; uncertainty, fixed, random and no-query rivals; uncertain access; matched observations; stopped and uninformative controls |
| B | What should persist when a maker changes? | Acquired craft with unknown goal, belief, skill, tool and opportunity changes; stationary, gradual and returning-state controls; static, recent, discounted, selective-transition and known-change readers |
| C | When is corroboration independent? | One/two/four independent roots, repeated descendants, shared errors, selective disclosure, partial and wrong provenance, independent contradiction |
| D | When does revising an explanation help? | In-family, near-family and absent-family truth; fixed, empirical, cautious, abstaining and bounded-revision readers; correct and misleading candidate additions; prefix-only choice and future scoring |
| E | Which memories transfer to new questions? | Exact posterior/full history, predictive summaries with closure checks, direct history and compact flat/role-organized PyTorch readers; withheld combinations, new questions, capacity/learning gates, storage and total work |
| F | Which findings survive changed mechanics? | Complete 16-cell decision-rule/acquisition/opportunity/source-noise factorial; separately declared held-out laws and coefficient redraws; cellwise effects and interactions |
| G | Do proposed internal roles support the right interventions? | Behavioral-only and intervention-trained readers; equal counterfactual information for rivals; wrong mappings, shuffled labels, alternative partitions and valid-state controls |
| H | What does finite compression preserve? | Exhaustively enumerated small codebooks, equal-rate flat and product-constrained encodings, old versus new query families; exact finite optimum only within the enumerated class |

The finite board adapter reuses V16's pure executable primitives and actual
repeated-fragment learner, never a previous campaign entry point. Latent roles
include acquired repertoire, current goal, private routing belief and persistent
tradeoff. Ordinary outcomes can leave goal and belief observationally equivalent;
interventions may distinguish them. Reader inference receives public serialized
observations, not latent truth or future outcomes. Evaluator truth lives separately.

The new world changes decision rules (compensatory or satisficing), acquisition
(successful isolated or interference-coupled practice), considered opportunities
(exogenous or state-dependent), and source error (independent or shared).
These are declared miniature architectures, not a sample representative of humans.
New coefficient draws do not count as new architectures.

Selectors are scored on independently generated future query contexts and on
native execution of the learner's own three-cell task. Historical discrimination
means the declared curriculum/state family, not unique recovery of an arbitrary
life history. Demonstration uptake uses native practice and learned fragments.
Fresh empty boards are supplied by the apparatus with explicit provisioning cost;
the design does not claim to solve arbitrary physical reset planning.

## Resource and execution contract

One low-priority scientific worker and one numerical thread. No GPU, cloud, API
inference, dependency synchronization, or changes to Sounding Line. Its installed
PyTorch interpreter may execute an isolated Ghost-owned CPU child through a strict
serialized boundary. The parent waits; there is never a second concurrent science
worker. All child CPU and attempted work, including failed fits and verification,
are charged. Runtime fingerprints and cross-environment numerical controls precede
neural admission.

Original cumulative ceiling: 64,800 CPU seconds, of which V18.2 recorded
407.515625. These are ceilings, not occupancy targets. Engineering wall time is

Plans, source snapshots, unit blocks, model weights, and completion receipts are
immutable. Resume verifies identities and skips only independently bound complete
work. A manual finite queue has terminal events; no periodic model polling or
automatic sample expansion. Each named verified result batch is committed and
pushed after full documentary write-through.

## Admission and analysis

Every instrument needs known-answer negative and positive controls before discovery.
Eligibility gates do not depend on scientific advantage. Menus, sample sizes, fit
budgets, model selection, candidate revisions and summaries are frozen per packet
before held-out outcomes. Timing uses the first 16 units and a full representative
block; a forecast is not a result. Development/pilot cases never enter discovery.

Paired comparisons share lineage and counterfactual randomness. Repeated probes,
training seeds and revisions are averaged within the original maker/world lineage.
Source experiments additionally identify root observations and their descendants.
Invalids, missing responses, failed training and incomplete cells retain explicit
denominators. Zero predicted mass retains infinite proper loss; no silent clipping.
Prediction, historical-state uncertainty, enactment, calibration and work costs
remain separate outcomes. No single composite score stands for all of them.

Finite references, a separately written executor/probability check, input-boundary
tests, independent reaggregation, source-extracted replay and interruption/resume
fixtures establish scoped validity. Exact posterior equality checks are controls,
not new findings. Calibration under the fitted model does not establish robustness
outside it. Rank reversals and nulls retain their full status.

All findings are descriptive discovery unless a separately frozen untouched-reserve
confirmation is explicitly admitted. No human, neural-anatomy, unique-ontology,
or general alignment claim follows from these constructed mechanisms and methods.
