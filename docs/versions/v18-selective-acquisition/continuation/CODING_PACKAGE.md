### G1 — checking a method when useful steps temporarily look wrong

Use the native assembly world to distinguish harmful routines from necessary detours. A verified development witness already exists: parents `[-1,0,0]`, defaults `[0,0,0]`, start `[0,0,0]`, target `[1,0,0]`, program `[4,5,6,1,2,9]`. Removing the two dependent parts, rotating the support and reattaching the parts is legal and ends with STOP. Mismatch follows **1,2,3,2,1,0,0**. A rule rejecting every immediately worsening step cannot implement this route. This witness is a target-realization check, not the scored discovery sample.

Construct balanced monotone, required-detour, genuinely harmful and unreachable cases. Verify reachability and the existence or necessity of a detour with exhaustive search in the small native world. Avoid comparing a working method against an impossible goal without reporting that fact. Extend the execution limit to admit the witnessed six-action route; do not inherit a four/five-action ceiling that prevents the phenomenon.

Compare no gate, local mismatch gate, whole-routine endpoint check, bounded two/four-step lookahead, and a known-law viability ceiling. Include state search with no acquired routines. Whole-routine checking can succeed when a suitable complete routine is stored but fail when useful dependencies span fragments; test that difference explicitly. At use time, check the actual current state, not only the empty training state.

Cross correct, incomplete and misleading dependency information. Error is in the learner's supplied/learned world model, while the evaluator executes the true physical law. Charge every simulated check. Measure false rejection of useful plans, acceptance of harmful plans, and downstream success at equal total computation. Distinguish inability to infer a missing dependency from failure to act on an available one.

**If local checking fails only on detours:** proceed to knowledge acquisition in G2. **If lookahead fixes it:** test its cost and limits on deeper structures in G3. **If a cheap primitive planner dominates:** preserve that boundary and test whether acquired methods become useful under a real planning constraint, rather than weakening the primitive rival arbitrarily.

### G2 — learn the missing dependency; choose useful evidence

Keep physical laws separate from learner hypotheses. Give a learner incomplete executed demonstrations and let it infer which operations depend on which supports. Compare an explicit version-space/dependency model, a surface transition table or nearest episode, and the known-law ceiling. Use the same observations. Failure to observe a relation is not evidence that it is absent.

The learner may acquire one or two additional observations from a fixed menu: an action outcome, a short routine demonstration or an allowed context cue. Compare uniform query choice, a fixed diagnostic sequence and a decision-relevant query rule. A reduction in uncertainty is only secondary; the main measures are better held-out predictions and legal task completion per observation and total work.

Provide random, task-relevant and selectively presented demonstration sets, matched on count and recorded task coverage as closely as the construction permits. Keep the selection mechanism explicit. Include a donor whose routine is legal under a different law. Failed demonstrations have a defined retained outcome; do not draw new donors until one happens to be compatible. This directly repairs the *next experiment's* acquisition contract without retroactively filling V17's failed branch.

Score recovery of dependencies and predictions separately. Under observation-equivalent laws, report the compatible set and test whether the query splits it. If evidence changes one dependency, measure whether unrelated beliefs/plans are inappropriately revised. Do not describe this learner as acquiring another person's values: its learned objects are physical/contextual dependencies and useful operations.

**If targeted observations help:** transfer the query rule to new topology families. **If uniform observation works as well:** keep it as the stronger simple rival. **If the candidate law family excludes truth:** retain model misspecification as a deliberate separate condition and measure detection/abstention, rather than automatically declaring inverse inference refuted.

### G3 — structural breadth, longer dependencies and learned representations

Start with the already implemented graphic and three-part assembly worlds. Then introduce a bounded parameterized assembly adapter for **3, 5 and 7 parts**, with shallow forks, chains and multiple support groups. These are varied laws/topologies within an assembly family, not seven independent architectures. Preserve the graphic family as a separate check where the same question is meaningful.

For the new adapter, define attach actions `0..n-1`, remove `n..2n-1`, rotate `2n..3n-1`, STOP `3n`; support removal/rotation is illegal while a dependent remains attached. At n=3, enumerate legal states/actions and verify exact agreement with the native executor before larger cases. Construct target states by valid executed paths, retain the path privately, and include a separately labeled unreachable stratum if used. Set episode/search bounds before scoring each stratum. No claim rests on a target the apparatus cannot realize.

Compare primitive state search, stored episodes, native learned fragments and fragments plus exception episodes at matched online computation and explicit storage budgets. Reuse native Stitch only where already runnable; admit at most one small source-compatible adapter after a working non-Stitch branch is producing results. More compression is not the objective. Test which representation improves behavior under changed dependency, missing observation and longer useful routines.

Freeze training and transfer law/topology strata. Do not claim unseen structure by permuting part names. Cache pure executions within a method consistently, account for cache construction and amortization, and do not share hidden evaluator search with a reader. Adapt the state-search implementation for depth bounds correctly: a state reached with less remaining action budget must not suppress a later route with more feasible remaining budget.

**Positive:** choose a fresh law/topology contrast and the strongest equally informed direct rival for G4. **Null:** determine whether retrieval, representation, planning or missing evidence is limiting. **Reversal:** keep the counterexample, test its explicit dependency explanation and allocate the next block to that boundary. Increase sample breadth only while it changes these conclusions; do not reproduce V17's millions of restricted structures.
