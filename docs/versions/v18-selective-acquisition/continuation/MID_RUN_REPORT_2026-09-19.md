## What the completed evidence currently supports

- **Acquisition versus search:** the large original focus advantage disappears
  when equally informed common state search receives 128 total operations. Every
  balanced four-cell success condition reaches 100%; acquisition still changes
  work. The original result therefore depends on its memory/search setting.
- **Checking versus necessary detours:** on correct-law native detours, local
  mismatch checking gives 0% success at 2,048 operations, while ungated primitive
  search and endpoint checking of an intact demonstrated routine give 100%.
  Checking learned fragments by their endpoints gives 66.67%; longer lookahead
  repairs this at additional cost.
- **Evidence versus representation:** useful observations improve dependency
  forecasts and, under ambiguity, legal task completion. An equally informed
  direct compiler matches the strongest task success, often much more cheaply.
  These tasks do not establish that an explicit learned graph is necessary.
- **Learned representations versus simpler rivals:** seven-part stored episodes
  reach 93.20% success, primitives 90.82%, native fragments 89.42%, and fragments
  with exception episodes 88.54% at the declared storage/work setting. Grounded
  Stitch has a native three-part cost advantage but a graphic cost disadvantage;
  the adapter provides no five- or seven-part Stitch result.
- **Recoverable history:** endpoint evidence leaves four maker strategies
  ambiguous. Informative indexed process observations identify the correct
  strategy within the known finite family; uninformative observations do not.
  A direct template predictor matches the inverse ceiling. This is not recovery
  of an unsupplied goal, beliefs or values.

These are constructed-mechanism findings. The sparse attachment-table comparison
also provides a constructed-method boundary: its local abstraction expresses
legality, but sparse observations cover too little of the test space to replace
dependency learning. Its relative advantage appears under a deliberately wrong
candidate family, where both predictions remain inaccurate. All purpose-built
comparisons remain **miniature — architecture untested**.
