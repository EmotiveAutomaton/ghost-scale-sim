# V22 C2: Similarity, process evidence and preference records

Do shared processes and records help different inference targets? In the independent constructed world, eight preference records improve loss by 0.423 nats without a shared library and 0.460 with one. A reliable shared-library benefit itself is not established when similarity is hidden. Disclosing the sharing relation identifies the binary preference without records, and a coupled preference witness also helps. The unconditional claim that similarity cannot inform preferences fails; the result depends on what is disclosed and coupled.

The primary reader sees its own binary library and preference, a rendered operation, noisy library witnesses and preference records. The evaluator knows whether maker and reader share each latent bit; that sharing status is withheld from the primary reader. Nonsharing means the opposite binary value, not an unrelated draw. A self-projection heuristic imposes a 0.9 matching prior and is explicitly separate from exact inference.

With neither sharing disclosed nor a coupled preference witness, zero records give exactly 0.6931 nats preference loss and no record-ablation benefit. Eight related records reduce loss by 0.4231 [0.2347, 0.6009] without a shared library and 0.4597 [0.3097, 0.6039] with one. At 32 records the improvements are 0.6910 and 0.6712 nats, respectively. Two-record intervals cross zero. These are records-versus-no-records comparisons on the same forecasts and targets.

Neither a reliable process advantage nor a preference-sharing effect over the practical margin is established in those primary contrasts. Sparse cells contain only two cases per fully crossed stratum per world. The supplement retains comparisons at each preference-sharing setting rather than hiding this behind pooled scores. The primary construction withholds sharing information and is symmetric; it cannot establish that real similarity never helps.

The disclosed-similarity positive control has zero preference loss without records: disclosure of either sameness or binary opposition identifies the preference. This is supplied information, not inferred similarity. With hidden sharing but a coupled preference witness, no-record preference loss is 0.5047 instead of the independent control's 0.6931. Unrelated records are ignored by exact inference and cannot substitute for valid preference records; the record-ablation and self-projection controls remain in the public strata.

At 8,192 labels, 63 of 256 target strata have headroom, 29 parity and 164 unresolved. There are modal-accuracy/proper-score reversals in 24 strata; those are finite diagnostic comparisons, not 24 discoveries. This card supports a conditional information distinction and rejects the unconditional preference-separation claim. It does not establish a unique maker representation or a psychological similarity mechanism.

## Verification and scope

All 2,048 original case posteriors, 16,384 reader-case forecast bundles and 32,768 world/target score strata have independent numerical receipts. The complete 102-file scientific replay matches. This review rechecked every retained original/replay file hash, the plan, immutable source archive, environment declaration and receipt bindings; it reused the matching numerical work without rerunning science.

There are eight coefficient worlds, 256 development cases per world, two label budgets and two fits per budget. All four fits stopped on validation. Fits are averaged within worlds; intervals resample whole paired worlds 4,096 times. These descriptive 95% intervals do not adjust for the many comparisons and do not constitute confirmation. An original matched headroom interval must clear 0.02 nats; missing worlds or unresolved gates cannot support superiority. Report-stage intervals use a separate fixed bootstrap seed; immutable admission gates remain unchanged.

The independent checker establishes numerical correctness within this finite model, not adequacy of its mechanism assumptions. The parameter-law draw has only a small finite support; eight world identities need not be eight distinct law values. Miniature — architecture untested. No human conclusion follows.
