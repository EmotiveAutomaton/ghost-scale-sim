We tested whether structured reading gains an advantage under execution or presentation shifts with fewer training labels. All 24 comparisons fail across both registered fits. All 16,056,320 new forecasts verify, with no exact-zero truth probabilities. These are constructed-method results; confirmation remains untouched.

The 35 new blocks complete eleven first fits and all 24 second fits for context evidence at zero, one, four or sixteen retained episodes, with 128, 512 or 2,048 training labels, under both registered shifts. Thirteen earlier first fits are reused by verified immutable hashes. This brings the campaign to 642 verified packets. The maximum-label discovery candidate is separate and unchanged.

Both fits are averaged inside each of 32 whole coefficient worlds before pairing methods and applying 4,096 fixed-seed descriptive world-bootstrap resamples. Fits and evaluation rows are not independent worlds. These intervals remain conditional on shared training worlds.

Capped joint log loss is minus the natural logarithm of probability assigned to the true seven-part answer, with probabilities floored at one trillionth; lower is better. The declared advantage requires at least 0.02 natural-log units over both direct rivals and the legal template, a positive lower world interval and no increase in wrong attribution at 90% confidence. Exact zero mass is separate; the capped loss is not an uncapped proper score.
