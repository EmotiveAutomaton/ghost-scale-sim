## Scientific question and targets

Can a learned structured account recover contribution distinctions better than
matched direct reading, and which distinctions require retained episode evidence?
These are constructed-method tests. The world cannot establish human intent,
human values, endorsement or historical correspondence in real text.

The finite generator has 32 persistent maker configurations, eight contexts and
24,576 weighted trajectories per coefficient lineage. There are two linked content
bits and one presentation bit. Selection, revision and dependency repair are the
three substantive operations. A failed revision restores the initial content.
Tool provenance varies independently of skill. A request can differ from the adopted
aim. Inspection is latent unless explicitly observed. Complete observation still
withholds adopted aims. Governing purpose is retained as an unmeasured hypothesis
slot; there is no inferred values or author-share score.

The scored joint target is the seven-bit tuple: proposal route, selection, revision,
operation order, inspection, content aim, presentation aim. It has 128 alternatives;
it is a declared abstraction of process, not every microscopic execution variable.
The two aims can co-occur. Four evidence tiers are artifact, permitted context,
sparse record and complete observed record. Zero-probability supplied facts constrain
all readers equally. Histories contain past observations from the same maker, never
past private aim labels. Anonymous IDs are not features.

## Population, fits and proper scores

Training lineages are 0-7. Engineering/calibration uses 8-23; unit checks also expose
specific deterministic fixtures 24 and 56. Discovery uses 24-55 and claims no blind
world-selection on these checks. Confirmation uses untouched lineages 96-127.
Challenge lineages 128-159 are separate. Splits hold entire coefficient worlds and
their histories, projections, shifts and endpoints together. Coefficient identities
are checked without lineage IDs before admission; duplicates are refused.

Independent lineages are the units of uncertainty. Pair methods inside a lineage;
average repeated fit seeds inside each lineage before a fixed-seed 4,096-resample
world bootstrap. Do not count rows, repeated queries or fit seeds as independent
worlds. Intervals remain descriptive for this finite generator. The 32 discovery
redraws provide architecture sensitivity; they do not validate a universal human model.

All learned arms share labels, observations, candidates and evaluation cases. Rivals
are a training-frequency prior, legal-support uniform template, direct frequency
table, 64-hidden-unit joint MLP, independently trained bit-factorized MLP and a
structured conditional-evidence model. The latter estimates a naive-Bayes joint
account; it is not an exact learned causal interpreter. The supplied-law Bayes
reader is labelled oracle. It cannot establish learned success. The two MLP heads
use approximately matched parameter counts; other arms are not capacity matched.
Storage, parameters, training/decoding CPU and acquisition costs are separate outputs.

The primary G1/G7 test is context-tier joint log loss, structured versus BOTH direct
rivals and the legal template. A positive result requires an improvement of at least
0.02 natural-log units against each, a positive lower descriptive world interval,
and no increase in confident wrong attribution. Freeze eligible configurations before
opening confirmation. G7 includes a fixed diagnostic comparison even if no discovery
candidate passes. No subgroup can replace the primary after results are seen.
Log loss uses a declared probability floor of 1e-12; exact zero-truth mass is
reported separately, especially for forced omission. This capped log score must not
be described as an uncapped proper score. Squared probability loss is proper.
Joint squared probability loss, accuracy, goal/tool/order/inspection loss, confidence,
and unsupported attribution are secondary. Forecast probabilities and stored scores
are checked independently. Report ambiguity and learned decoder failure separately.

## Finite useful forest

The opening wave covers every G0-G6 consumer, all evidence tiers, memory lengths
0, 1, 4, 16, 64, 256, and small/medium label budgets. Successors test sample-efficiency
curves through 131,072 labels (32,768 at history 256), two fit seeds, changed-tool and
presentation-shift challenges, cue reliability/copying/fees, candidate budgets and
observed inspection. Long histories test whether state inference helps or introduces
irrelevant evidence; larger label counts test decoder limitation, not runtime padding.

Every admitted card has an exact design, source/environment identity, output namespace,
CPU cap, measured forecast and prerequisites. G0 is required for contribution science;
G3's retained-world diagnostic is independent. Conditional successors are recorded
before outcomes and require an agent's result review. Precision expansion depends on
interval width and unresolved learning curves, not the sign of an effect. No extra seeds
solely to fill time. If useful work runs short, record the frontier and service gap;
do not reinterpret idle time as continuous execution or scientific exhaustion.

## Resources and operation

Gear 1: one below-normal scientific worker, one numerical thread, CPU only; no package
or environment synchronization and no Sounding Line edits. Finite new CPU envelope:
90 hours including setup, failures, checks, replay and scientific computation. At least
18 hours are protected for confirmation/verification/closeout; discovery cannot consume
more than 72 hours. The fixed wall deadline always takes precedence. Final measured
admission may reduce this envelope, never silently enlarge it.

Worker working-set ceiling 8 GiB; minimum free disk 200 GiB; V20 retained artifact cap
64 GiB. Check host headroom before launch and at four-hour native checkpoints. Pause
new Ghost admission if Sounding Line reports interference, host free memory drops below
16 GiB, or cooling/controller limits require it. Never alter the sibling controller.
Per-card estimates include full fit, independent check, raw output and consumer. Refuse
blocks that cannot fit before the science cutoff. A failed/partial block keeps its
namespace and costs; repair uses a new identity. No result or source is overwritten.

Freeze at least 24-36 hours of measured runnable demand if useful implemented designs
support it, and a conditional backlog at least 1.25 times remaining useful capacity.
The forecast receipt must report any shortfall explicitly. Do not inflate forecasts.
A finite backlog is conditional work, not evidence it has executed or been accepted.

Use existing native ownership, hidden module-form workers and event-only review.
Completion, failure, disappearance, queue drain and aligned four-hour operational
checkpoints cause bounded review; healthy computation does not trigger model polling.
Keep the notifier disarmed while a live operator owns its review. Preserve V19's
separate final closeout and already running Sounding Line services.

per-row scores, independent arithmetic, a readable evaluator casebook and frozen code.

complete extracted-source replay for each admitted implementation family and any repair;
retain hashes for all other immutable blocks. Do not substitute a tiny unrelated fixture
for reproduction of a claimed number. If a semantic defect is found, identify and
reanalyse affected descendants before interpreting them.

and publish each new nameable verified batch. Midpoint report is due 30 September
