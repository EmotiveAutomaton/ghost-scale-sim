## 4. Freeze the scientific core before outcomes

Use two linked content units and one presentation unit, three substantive
operations, and at most 32 persistent maker configurations initially. Compute the
actual trajectory/state count before admission. A fourth operation needs a
complete timing/checker pilot, not an assumed combinatorial budget.

Keep proposal source, skill, inspection, selection and revision distinct. A task
request need not be the adopted goal. Preserve a defined governing-purpose
hypothesis alongside local aims, without turning synthetic labels into human
values. The world must contain endpoint aliases for human/tool routes, hidden
inspection, operation order and alternative aims, plus copied context with no
additional source information.

Use four separately reported evidence tiers: artifact only, permitted workflow
context, sparse event record and complete observed event record. Complete
observation does not reveal unobserved aims. Truth, evaluator strata and candidate
labels never enter the blind packet or adaptive selection. Anonymous IDs must not
encode the hidden answer. Unknown review scope remains unknown.

The proposed G1 primary contrast is lineage-paired log loss for past-process
recovery under workflow context, structured versus the strongest matched direct
reader. Report artifact-only performance separately; witnessed tiers are both
useful tasks and revealing-evidence controls. Also score tool involvement,
recorded handling, order, local aims, unsupported attribution and joint coherence.
The source's 0.02-nat margin is a planning default: freeze the actual primary
contrast, practical margin and secondary attribution/cost requirement before new
scientific outcomes. Do not select a headline after inspecting subgroups.

Every primary comparison includes a training-frequency prior, a direct reader,
a legal-support/template rival, the structured joint account and a labelled
exact supplied-law ceiling. Match observations, labels, candidate availability,
population and legal restrictions. Charge proposal, execution, reranking,
training and decoding; equal smoothing alone is not matched capacity.

Start with the resident small-reader stack. Admit a recurrent/transformer setting
only when it addresses a declared comparison and is registered in the one shared
model-setting ledger. Name its architecture, fit/lineage maximum, labels and cost
before fitting. No existing consumed allowance is reused and no hidden parameter
search is spread across repository names.

## 6. Validity and evidence closure

Build the score/report consumer first and exercise one complete block: blind
input, prediction, separate truth, score, uncertainty and readable case output.
The checker independently reconstructs execution/posteriors and never calls the
producer's inference function. Use rational or symbolic checks for decisive
alias/equality claims; numerical proximity alone is not exact ambiguity.

Required controls cover revealing evidence, irrelevant evidence, endpoint
aliasing, repeated-source zero gain, false-context correction, equal enforcement
of supplied facts, omitted truth/unknown mass, impossible joint combinations,
identifier permutations, hidden-field rejection and corrupted forecast/score
rejection. Verify zero support, normalization, joint/multilabel semantics and
proper scores for every branch's actual output type.

Split whole generator/source lineages before outcome inspection. Keep histories,
renderings, perturbations and endpoints together. Pair methods within lineage;
resample independent lineages, not rows or repeated queries. Separate fit
uncertainty from world uncertainty. Reserve at least 20% of scientific capacity
and untouched lineages for confirmation/checking; freeze exact counts after pilot
timing and before use. Any expansion rule depends on precision, not observed sign.

Each accepted result needs raw block hashes, frozen code/environment/input
identities, all attempted/failed outcomes, independent reaggregation, bounded
complete replay and matched reader/evaluator exports. A semantic scoring fault
requires reanalysis of dependent findings. An unrelated fixed fixture does not
justify rerunning valid completed science for presentation.

Preserve the existing findings/theory/exchange write-through and stable hypothesis
IDs. No standalone theory file per branch. No dedicated Ghost theory-lint command
was found in the inspected scripts/tests/runners; do not claim a nonexistent
linter passed. Use actual available document checks and review the scientific
meaning and table summaries explicitly.

## 7. Resource admission and five-day service

After approval, inventory native owners, CPU/RAM/disk and current Sounding Line
throughput. Begin with one worker/thread below normal priority. Preserve existing
cooling/resource settings and leave unrelated applications alone. First-gear
operation is the proposed starting constraint; any later concurrency must be
measured, compatible with the owning controller and within the approved allocation.

Measure complete fit/score/check/export blocks, including child CPU, peak RAM and
disk growth. Freeze finite per-card and campaign CPU/RAM/disk envelopes from those
measurements. Do not copy V19's 96-hour cap into a new acceptance or equate five
elapsed days with 120 CPU hours. At one worker the theoretical five-day maximum
is 120 CPU hours; useful affordable demand, resource headroom and the protected
share determine the actual admitted envelope. No allocation is made by this plan.

Use one existing-style native scheduler and immutable per-worker sources/output
roots. Freeze complete blocks with all rivals and consumers. Before scale-up,
forecast 24–36 hours of runnable work and at least 1.25 times the useful remaining
five-day workload, accounting for dependencies and measured aggregate throughput.
Forecasts are admission checks, not promises manufactured from seed counts. If
finite useful work cannot support them, report the shortfall and frontier.

Implement the next independent family before a small pilot drains the queue.
Completion, failure and queue exhaustion request bounded agent work. Coordinate
the new campaign's native four-hour inspection clock with Sounding Line, retaining
its deadlines through restarts; verify actual delivery as well as configuration.
Do not change V19's event contract or use repeated model polling of healthy work.

The two new campaigns share a science start only when Sounding Line has an
admitted complete core block and shared resource/monitoring readiness. Ghost
should have a core or independent fallback; a documented Ghost delay must not
hold the primary project. The common endpoint is then exactly 120 elapsed hours
later. Delayed Ghost work joins that endpoint and reports its service shortfall.
There is no accepted start date, stop date or model budget yet.

Preserve the final 6–8 hours for completing small blocks, review, export and
delivery while protecting at least the 20% confirmation/checking allocation.
Admit no unit whose conservative execution plus consumer cannot fit. Record idle
time and its cause. Stop admissions before resource limits; do not delete evidence
or relax criteria to recover space or runtime.
