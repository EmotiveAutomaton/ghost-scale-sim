## Population and interpretation

The seven opening packets cover G0-G6 on the same 32 discovery coefficient lineages, 24-55. Each learned packet uses 8,192 labels from training lineages 0-7, one fixed fit seed, no history, and 2,048 test cases per discovery lineage. G1 uses permitted context; G2 uses complete operation records with inspection still latent. Each fitted reader is shared across the test lineages. The 2,555,904 forecast rows are not independent worlds, and the seven families do not supply 224 independent worlds. Intervals below pair methods within each whole lineage, with 4,096 fixed-seed bootstrap resamples. They are descriptive 95% intervals conditional on this training fit and finite generator, not confirmation or universal architecture claims.

Capped logarithmic loss uses natural-log units and a probability floor of 1e-12; lower is better. Exact zero probability for the truth is reported separately. Squared probability loss is the proper-score secondary. Confident wrong attribution means an incorrect modal joint answer with at least 90% probability. All ten stored forecast metrics, supplementary candidate scores and acquisition costs were reconstructed.

The ledger and bounded raw recomputation are identical by construction; their agreement is a reconstruction control, not two independently learned successes.
