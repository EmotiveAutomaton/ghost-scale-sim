# V20 long-history evidence-tier checkpoint

We tested whether 64 retained episodes give the structured reader an advantage across richer evidence tiers. None of the seven new diagnostic conditions beats both direct rivals and the legal template under the declared comparison rule. The artifact-only condition at 512 labels assigns stored zero probability to 2.46% of truths through numerical underflow. All 3,211,264 forecasts verify, and that complete block replays exactly. These are constructed-method results. The context primary remains failed, and confirmation remains untouched.

## Comparison and findings

The seven newly completed blocks use 64 past episodes: sparse and complete records at 128 labels; artifact, sparse and complete records at 512; and artifact and sparse records at 2,048. These are partial learning curves at an operational checkpoint, not a complete tier-by-budget wave. Each block uses the same 32 discovery coefficient worlds, eight separate training worlds, one fixed fit seed, 2,048 queries per discovery world and seven readers. Complete records still withhold private aims and keep inspection latent. All readers receive the same supplied-law support constraints.

Capped log loss is the negative natural logarithm of saved probability on the true joint answer, floored at one trillionth; lower is better. It is not an uncapped proper score. The comparison rule requires improvement of at least 0.02 against both direct rivals and the legal template, a positive lower descriptive whole-world interval, and no increase in confident wrong attribution. These evidence tiers are diagnostics; they cannot replace the registered context primary.

## Floor, secondary outcomes and validation

Of 49 method-condition arms, one has exact-zero truth mass: the structured artifact-only reader at 512 labels gives zero to 1,615 of 65,536 truths (2.46429%). All other arms have no exact zeros. The largest fraction of true-answer probabilities below the log-score floor is 79.85229%; small positive mass and exact zero remain separate. Joint squared probability loss, accuracy, goal/tool/order/inspection losses and confident wrong attribution are retained for every arm in the [aggregates](../../../results/v20/history-tier-wave-1/AGGREGATES.json). No secondary score substitutes for the failed diagnostic comparison.

All COMPLETE records, plans, frozen source archives, environment identities, training parameter counts, fit/decode costs and blind/evaluator roles verify. Every saved forecast, secondary score and summary is independently checked with the existing analysis reviewer. Earlier full scientific opening-family, scoring-repair, 64/256-episode and evidence-tier replays are reused by immutable receipt hash. The complete artifact-only 64-episode/512-label block additionally replays all 548 scientific files and its summary exactly from frozen source. It was selected after observing its zeros as a bounded diagnostic replay, not a prospective replication. A separate retained-count audit finds all 1,615 zero truths inside the common legal support, with finite smoothed likelihoods. Their true-to-modal log odds range from -789.22467 to -745.15851 and exponentiate to stored zero. This is numerical underflow, distinct from candidate exclusion; no saved probability or score is repaired.
