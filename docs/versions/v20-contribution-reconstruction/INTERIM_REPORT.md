## What the comparisons establish

The declared primary uses permitted context to predict a joint seven-part contribution account. A positive result needs at least 0.02 natural-log units of improvement over direct frequencies, the joint neural reader and the legal-support template, a positive lower world interval, and no increase in incorrect attribution at 90% confidence. Capped log loss is minus the logarithm of the probability given to the true answer, floored at one trillionth; lower is better. It is not an uncapped proper score. Squared probability loss is a proper secondary score.

Both registered fits are averaged inside each of 32 coefficient worlds before the fixed 4,096-resample world bootstrap. The intervals are descriptive and conditional on shared training worlds. Rows, fit seeds and the many conditions below are not independent experiments.
