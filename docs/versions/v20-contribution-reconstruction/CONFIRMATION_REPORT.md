Both fits use identical reader evidence and evaluator targets; fits and rows are nested inside worlds. Candidate execution plus the complete-group review, failed helper check and replay consumed 853.546875 CPU seconds against 4,000; additional closeout checking remains covered in the final cost receipt.

No new forecast assigns exactly zero probability to its truth. However, 409,125 probabilities fall below the declared one-trillionth log-loss floor. The capped score hides further differences in that range; zero mass and below-floor positive mass remain separate. Historical underflow and forced-omission failures remain retained.

The six original fixed conditions are diagnostic comparisons. Their failure leaves the failed native discovery primary unchanged. The three positive artifact-only discovery configurations were not promoted to confirmation. Challenge worlds 128–159 remain untouched. These results concern this finite constructed method, with supplied legal support and shared training worlds; they do not establish human intent, a learned causal interpreter or unknown-law recovery.
