We tested whether the structured-reader advantage survives repeated training with 64 past episodes under changed rules. The artifact-only execution diagnostic passes; the presentation-change and sparse-record comparisons fail. All 1,376,256 new forecasts and the positive full-block replay verify, with no exact-zero truth probabilities. These are constructed-method results; confirmation remains untouched.

The result event covered three second-fit packets with 64 past episodes and 131,072 training labels: artifact-only records under execution and presentation changes, and sparse records under the execution change. First fits were already verified. Review and replay acquired the exclusive scientific-worker lock while the original supervisor held with no scientist active. There are now 514 verified packets.

Both fits are averaged inside each of 32 coefficient worlds before pairing readers and applying 4,096 fixed-seed descriptive world-bootstrap resamples.
