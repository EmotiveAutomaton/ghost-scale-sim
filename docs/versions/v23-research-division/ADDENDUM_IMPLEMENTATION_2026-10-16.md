# October 16 addendum: independent implementation

The owner authorized implementation of the unambiguous engineering while the
theory/design questions are discussed separately. This is an execution and
validation update, not a new scientific finding or full adoption of every
proposal in the [revision-two packet](addendum/GHOST_SCALE_ADDENDUM_2026-10-16_REV2.md).

New admissions use a hash-bound supplement: 285 CPU hours, 18 protected hours,
and group ceilings of 48 hours for repairs, 44 for N4, 45 for N7, 40 for twins
and 40 for confirmation. N1 remains resource-deferred with its previous cap.
The original acceptance, previous reallocations and original report/deadline
remain immutable. Existing packets keep their original bindings and limits.
All previous charges and reservations still count against the new total; the
group ceilings do not grant additive spending authority.

The proposed schedule is implemented: eight workers from 08:00 to 22:00 Pacific,
ten overnight and three throughout October 21. **Actual concurrency remains
three**, pending a validated way to measure the specified Sounding Line
interference limit. The code does not equate BelowNormal priority with proof of
less than five percent interference. Slot reductions hold new starts and drain
existing work. Memory/disk floors, one numerical thread, no GPU, and Sounding
Line priority remain unchanged.

The finite addendum controller services N4, N7 and R3. It admits eligible work
immediately and logs each dispatch. A sixty-minute free-slot stall requests a
review; dependency holds do not become admissible jobs. Existing twelve-hour
health checks and failure/family-decision events continue. A passing pilot leads
to checked fits and full batches; a failure remains a failure and is not retried
automatically. The live controller is frozen independently of development.

## Implemented safeguards

The original reallocation validator is retained. New admissions bind the
supplement's exact bytes; changed hashes refuse. A successor receives a new
immutable capsule that replaces only runtime/resource-policy code and preserves
every scientific source, fit, random identity and predecessor binding. Existing
capsules are never edited. Supplement-aware runtimes monitor cumulative recorded
campaign charges, including concurrent work.
Recovery that reuses parts of an existing logical packet checks that packet's
original resource contract. It cannot borrow a larger budget that its frozen
runtime does not implement. A new scientific successor uses the checked runtime
handoff described above.

Large new batches can use smaller disjoint whole-world parts where four worlds
would exceed the packet cap. They may run up to three parts concurrently within
the shared worker limit. Out-of-order completions are assembled in frozen world
order. After a part fails, already-running siblings drain, their reservations
remain held, and no replacement is invented. Assembly itself claims a worker
slot. Only complete raw evidence and complete original/replay coverage can enter
a scientific decision. Registered grids, sample sizes and calibrated looks do
not change.

Twin batches now have a complete-world partition assembler. Makers, shared
articles/items and engine graphs stay within their world; they are never treated
as independent replication units. A single world or indivisible fit that exceeds
four CPU hours still receives a resource disposition. Measured references and
source/module gates remain required. The T2 clock reader accepts canonical
`values` and the legacy `seconds` alias and rejects conflicting arrays.

Confirmation can use concurrent execution parts while retaining serial decisions
in the frozen e-LOND order. Its preregistered 128/256-world allocation rule is
unchanged; this is not a claim that the realized size was chosen before discovery.
The higher confirmation allowance is a CPU ceiling, not a wall-clock guarantee.
No confirmation order or reserved exposure has been opened by this update.

statistical qualifications; they are not new V23 jobs.
