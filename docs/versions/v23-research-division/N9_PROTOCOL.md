# N9: an offered branch and a declined branch

Declared before sampling under the October 17 packet. Twelve settings cross
generalization width (conditioning scene only; conditioning plus neighboring
scene), history (0/8/32) and timing (hidden/observed). Six reset probes cross
three scenes with an absent/present direct offer, in paired random order.
Sixteen reliability laws .60–.95, 64 cases per setting per block; mechanism
identity cycles across cases and blocks. Adjacent mechanism cases share random
uniforms and probe order. Inference resamples whole blocks, never cases.

Baseline considers/takes the bold branch slowly. Fear prunes it within the
generalization width; expertise prunes only in the conditioning scene. Dislike
considers/declines it slowly everywhere. Local inattention is an independent
fair omission at each decision. Pruning/omission is fast. Direct offers force
consideration for fear, expertise and local inattention without changing value;
dislike continues to decline. Reliability mass goes on these rule responses
(a mixture for local inattention); remaining mass is uniform over action ×
latency. This is a stipulated consideration model, not a model of trauma learning.

Ordinary history uses the conditioning scene without offers. Untimed actions
there exactly alias fear, expertise and dislike. Narrow fear and expertise remain
identical even with probes: no positive control requires distinguishing them.
This structural limit is part of the result, not an instrument failure.

Exact inference integrates all five mechanisms and sixteen reliability values.
Timing-hidden histories and posterior probe updates omit timing. Primary is
true-mechanism log-loss reduction from history to all probes. Offers-only uses
offered actions; timing-only uses unoffered latency when observable; actions-only
uses unoffered actions across scenes. The last qualification is necessary:
offered actions themselves can separate pruning and dislike, so the packet's
action-alias prediction cannot apply to actions under every intervention.

All six joint probe responses are forecast before observing them. Six direct
rivals forecast the same joint targets with the same history/visibility metadata;
timing-hidden joint forecasting is a separate diagnostic with unobserved timing
as a withheld target, not extra input for the primary mechanism posterior.
No evaluator mechanism/law enters any reader feature. The canonical ledger
retains only observed history and untaken probe menus, with full round trip.

Known answers cover baseline and narrow-width aliasing, changed-probe recovery,
high-reliability recovery, probability products versus log space, no hidden-label
or future-target input, and ledger integrity. Full-grid pilot/replay and six
frozen fitted rivals precede a reserved 12,288-case batch. Every raw response,
prediction, posterior and score is retained. Complete replay, independent proper
score reconstruction and block intervals precede acceptance. Constructed
mechanisms, miniature — architecture untested; outside confirmation.
