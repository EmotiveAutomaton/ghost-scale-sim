# N15: source-specific trust or general vigilance

Declared before production under the October 17 packet. Four mechanisms cross
0/8/32 history records. Sixteen .60–.95 reliability laws, 64 cases per setting.
History concerns source A only: each record publicly reveals a failure (chance
.25), binary stakes (fair) and whether the maker checks. Counts update before
the checking choice. History contexts, failures and response uniforms are paired
across mechanisms; only the mechanism's response law changes.

Intended checking probabilities are: fixed=.2; source tracking=.1+.8 times
failures/(2+records) for the relevant source; global vigilance uses that same
ratio pooled across sources; stakes-only=.1+.8 times stakes. Reliability adds
uniform binary noise. This is a declared precision-to-checking link, not a
measurement of human trust or a fitted psychophysical law.

Six reset probes reveal a new failure of A or B, then measure checking of A
at balanced low/high stakes. Each starts from the same post-history counts;
one probe cannot contaminate the next. The exact reader integrates mechanisms
and reliability, reconstructing candidate counts from the public failure record.
All six responses are forecast before observing any target. Six direct rivals
get the same public history and reset menus, without mechanism or actual law.

Primary is history-to-all-probes true-mechanism log-loss reduction. The ablation
uses only same-source failures. Tracking and global vigilance alias exactly in
the source-A history and same-source ablation. A source-B failure changes global
vigilance but not source-A tracking, making their distinction possible. Large
histories can dilute a single added failure; that loss of sensitivity is retained.

Controls verify these aliases, second-source effects, planted recovery in every
reliability law, probability products against log inference, ledger round trips
and future-target/evaluator exclusion. Full-grid cost/replay and six frozen fits
precede one reserved 12,288-case batch. Retain all observations, forecasts and
posteriors; complete replay, independent proper scores and whole-law bootstrap
intervals precede acceptance. No case-level bootstrap. Constructed mechanisms,
miniature—architecture untested; outside confirmation.
