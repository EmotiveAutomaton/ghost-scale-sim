# N12: concurrent goals and the last pass

Declared before production under the October 17 packet. Four mechanisms cross
conflict rate 0/.2/.5; sixteen reliability laws .60–.95, 64 cases per setting.
Twelve chronological observed choices and six withheld later choices share
public binary recommendations A/B/C. A is fair; B and C independently disagree
with A with the declared conflict probability. At zero conflict all agree.
Adoption time is uniformly one of indices 3–8 and is hidden from the reader.
Mechanisms share contexts, adoption times and response uniforms across settings.

Before adoption all mechanisms follow A. Afterwards: emergent chooses an equal
mixture of A and B, replacement follows B, author-final follows A, editor-final
follows C. Reliability mixes these response laws with uniform binary noise.
Thus final authority is stipulated causal control over the choice, not credit,
authorship law or a psychological claim.

Overlay/exact inference integrates mechanism, adoption time and reliability.
The single-top reader assumes one root throughout the artifact: B for emergence
or replacement, A for author-final, C for editor-final. The change-point reader
allows the before/after switch but still maps emergence to one root B. These
explicit restricted likelihoods distinguish representational and temporal
misspecification; the oracle is not given hidden adoption times. Six direct
rivals forecast the same withheld targets from the same public records.

Primary is the overlay reader's true-mechanism log-loss improvement over the
single-top reader. Also retain each reader's mechanism log loss, absolute error
of the posterior mean adoption time, final-authority posterior and error on
overlay mechanisms only. Authority-defined flags prevent non-overlay cases
from masquerading as correct authority recovery. At conflict zero all mechanisms
and adoption times alias exactly. Author-final leaves no response-law change,
so its adoption time remains unidentified even with conflicts; report that limit.

Known-answer controls cover zero-conflict aliasing, expected-count planted
recovery under every law, independent probability products, ledger round trips
and hidden-label/future-target exclusion. Full-grid pilot/replay, six frozen fits
and a reserved 12,288-case batch precede production. Retain every observation,
prediction and posterior; complete replay and independent score and law-block
interval reconstruction precede acceptance. Bootstrap laws, not cases.
Constructed mechanisms, miniature—architecture untested; outside confirmation.
