# N10: soft corrections and an unexplained direction

Declared before production under the October 17 packet. Twelve settings cross
hidden share 0/.1/.3, catalog correlation 0/.5 and low/high correction noise.
Sixteen .60–.95 reliability laws each contain 64 cases. Half the cases at positive
share have a hidden component; the other half are matched negatives. Share zero
contains only negatives, so detection AUC there is unavailable, not perfect.
Thirty-two corrections and six withheld corrections per case use paired context
orders and random draws across positive/negative mechanisms. No measured D34
unexplained-correction aggregate was delivered at declaration; noise defaults
are inverse temperatures 5 and 2, fixed before production.

Six possible corrections are the signed Cartesian basis in three dimensions.
Two catalog goals are the first two positive axes. A hidden goal has correlation
rho with its catalog axis and residual magnitude sqrt(1-rho²) on the third axis;
the residual's sign reverses with an observed binary context. Conditional choice
uses a softmax of vector alignment. Hidden share mixes the catalog and hidden
choice laws. Prescribed reliability then mixes uniform noise over six responses.
All likelihoods remain strictly positive. Share-zero analysis tests the .1
alternative; other settings use their declared alternative share.

The soft reader retains catalog projections, combining the two third-axis signs.
Soft-plus-residual retains their signed alignment with context. Both integrate
hidden-present/absent, two catalog goals, two residual directions and sixteen
reliability values. A cyclic context permutation is a frozen shuffle diagnostic,
not a randomization p-value. The privileged oracle knows mechanism and law;
it is explicitly separate from deployable readers. The negative hard comparator
intersects strictly positive catalog alignments and records empty support; it
does not invent a prediction after support empties.

Primary: binary hidden-mechanism log-loss reduction of residual over soft.
Additional readouts: empty support, within-law positive/negative detection AUC,
shuffled AUC, full hidden-vector cosine conditional on a planted hidden component,
and predictive log loss. The correlated-catalog failure is a prediction to test,
not encoded as an enforced error. Known answers span all reliability laws and
include positive soft support, planted likelihood recovery, independent products,
held-out/evaluator isolation and ledger round trips. Fixed and fresh six-rival
training uses the same public features and withheld targets.

Complete cost pilot and fresh replay precede six frozen fits and one reserved
12,288-case batch. Retain raw inputs, truth, posteriors, predictions and diagnostics;
full replay, independent proper scores and law-block bootstrap intervals precede
acceptance. AUC is reconstructed from retained case scores within each law;
aggregate inference resamples laws, never cases. Constructed mechanisms in a
miniature; architecture untested; not a model of people or eligible for confirmation.
