## Issues resolved before discovery

- **D5's cost estimate was materially wrong.** Its complete-world pilot reached
  its per-pass CPU limit before finishing one world. The two finished condition
  timings alone project to 77.43 CPU-hours for 40 worlds plus replay, excluding
  the remaining condition and invariance refits. This is a measured extrapolation,
  not an exact bound over every world. The owner explicitly directed deferral to
  V24. Preserve the failed pilot; do not count it as a scientific null or repaired
  witness. The original Stage 15 FAIL remains. [Resource disposition](D5_RESOURCE_REPORT.md).
- **N16's starting evidence and probe inventory needed precision.** Budgets count
  additional probes beyond each original setting's evidence. N4d retains its
  original 0/2/6 conditioning probes; new probes use a fresh unchanged menu draw.
  Choices use the original six instances without replacement. All policy
  propensities are logged; deterministic policies have zero support for some
  actions, so unrestricted retrospective reweighting is unavailable.
- **N16b needs a binary/four-category bridge.** Binary conflicts coarsen an
  ordinary N13 categorical response through a declared payoff map. Both readers
  see exactly the same response likelihood and context. The bridge exposes no
  hidden goal. Stake order is an exact alias under the exclusive-category law;
  it cannot establish human stake sensitivity. The original margins and power
  requirement remain fixed. [Protocol](N16B_PROTOCOL.md).
- **N17 cannot turn heterogeneous real-data aggregates into one exact target.**
  The cited quantities use different cohorts and response channels. Independent
  fitting and validation banks retain target errors explicitly. Synthetic quality
  is supplied to the readers; an equivalent real quality measure is a separate
  prerequisite. A finite-null held-out e-value controls taste detection, and
  the empirical no-taste gate can still fail. [Targets](N17_AGGREGATE_TARGETS.json),
  [protocol](N17_PROTOCOL.md).
- **N18's weak-situation advantage is constructed.** The detector threshold is
  conditional on a symmetric binary error channel and known norm/noise law.
  Exact enumeration, rather than extra pseudo-independent cases, measures
  information. Only the independent laws enter uncertainty intervals.

The local position before consulting the cited active-design paper was that
misspecified policies may waste probes, while a full scoring reader can still
use them. The paper supports testing sensitivity to misspecification and noise;
it does not guarantee that exponent-0.5 likelihood tempering repairs these finite
models. Treat this as an empirical mitigation comparison. [Sloman et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13698).
