# Contingency changes distinguish two aliased mechanisms

Can a changed contingency distinguish mechanisms that produce identical earlier
choices? In the constructed N4b probe, changing the contingency improves the
true-mechanism log score for the transition and intrinsic-recipe mechanisms by
0.149–0.179 nats. It does not improve that score for the habit mechanism.
Unchanged probes remain aliased to numerical precision. This is a finite
constructed-mechanism result, separate from the unfinished main N4 study and
from confirmatory family selection; it establishes nothing about human values.

All twelve declared settings ran with sixteen independent worlds and sixty-four
cases per world: 12,288 cases. Eight or thirty-two identical-history observations
precede the binary probe. The hidden generating mechanism is never a reader
feature. A forecast is scored before the probe outcome is used to update the
mechanism posterior. The post-probe score therefore measures identification from
newly observed evidence, not forecasting with a future label.

The table reports improvement in the log probability assigned to the true
mechanism. Intervals are descriptive 95% whole-world bootstrap intervals, not
simultaneous confidence statements or e-LOND confirmations. Every unchanged
setting and both changed-habit settings have gains below 1e-16 in magnitude.

| Generating mechanism | Earlier records | Changed contingency: mean gain | Descriptive interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Transition map | 8 | 0.17493 | 0.10643–0.24553 |
| Transition map | 32 | 0.16618 | 0.09957–0.24036 |
| Habit | 8 | approximately zero | numerical roundoff |
| Habit | 32 | approximately zero | numerical roundoff |
| Intrinsic recipe | 8 | 0.17859 | 0.10917–0.24948 |
| Intrinsic recipe | 32 | 0.14877 | 0.08816–0.21498 |

The habit mechanism retains posterior probability one-third after either probe
outcome: the other two mechanisms trade probability around it. A single probe
therefore does not completely identify all three mechanisms. Increasing the
amount of aliased earlier history does not resolve that ambiguity.

The separate binary direct rivals include both fixed training budgets, two fit
seeds each, and two fresh-law fits. Their validation scores and saved-model
predictions were independently checked before the full batch. They predict the
probe outcome; they are not presented as trained classifiers of hidden mechanism
labels. Complete fresh-process replay covers fitting and the full scientific
batch. Independent reconstruction checks public/truth identities, all retained
proper scores, native forecasts, conditional posteriors and whole-world means.
