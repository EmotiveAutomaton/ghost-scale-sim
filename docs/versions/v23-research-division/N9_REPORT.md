# N9: complete conditional study

Can an offered branch distinguish an option never considered from one considered and declined? In N9, combined probes change true-mechanism log loss by 0.001–0.992 nats across twelve settings. All 12,288 cases, 132 independently reconstructed intervals and full replay verify. Narrow fear and narrow expertise remain exactly aliased. This is a constructed-mechanism result, not evidence about trauma or people.

Offers separate pruning from dislike by construction; timing can supply a second observation of consideration. With long, timed histories some mechanisms are already resolved and extra probes add little. Actions across different scenes can distinguish location-specific pruning from global dislike; the action alias applies to the conditioning scene, not every action intervention. Fear and expertise with the same narrow width remain observationally identical, regardless of more samples. Timing-hidden joint forecasts are separate withheld-target diagnostics, not additional observed input.
