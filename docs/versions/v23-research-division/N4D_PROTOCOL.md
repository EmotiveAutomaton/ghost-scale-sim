# N4d: what a maker sacrifices

Declared before production under the October 17 packet. Four mechanisms cross
0/2/6 observed conflict probes, twelve settings. Sixteen reliability laws .60–.95,
64 cases per setting. Two incompatible goals have public stakes (1,3) or (3,1),
independently fair per probe. Six probe menus and uniforms are paired across
mechanisms and observed-count settings. All six outcomes are forecast before
any probe is revealed; the primary uses only the first declared number.

Lexicographic always favors goal one; weighted favors the larger stakes;
reversed favors goal two; stakes-blind random is fair. Reliability mixes each
rule with uniform binary noise. Baseline has no informative observations.
The exact reader integrates all four mechanisms and sixteen reliability values.
The hidden-stakes ablation integrates independent fair stakes and sees no actual
stakes. It intentionally aliases weighted and random rules at that resolution.

Primary is prior-to-probe true-mechanism log-loss reduction. Report the same
gain with stakes hidden. Zero probes exactly preserves the prior. Fixed stakes
that favor goal one alias weighted and lexicographic; varied stakes can separate
them. A finite random menu need not realize both stake orderings, a retained
sampling limitation rather than a reason to replace cases.

Controls verify all stated aliases, changed-probe information, every-law and
high-reliability recovery, probability-space/log-space agreement, canonical
ledger round trips and evaluator-label exclusion. Six direct rivals forecast
the same pre-probe targets. Complete cost pilot/replay and frozen fits precede
one reserved 12,288-case batch. All raw evidence and scores are retained; full
replay and independent law-block intervals precede acceptance. Resample laws,
not cases. Constructed mechanisms, miniature—architecture untested; outside
confirmation. This small study does not reopen N4's resource-deferred main grid.
