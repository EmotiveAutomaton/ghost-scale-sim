# X2: seeing the freeze and the first breach

Can a reader distinguish a sum that froze from a rule held from the start? For weight 4 and a freeze after 20 refused sales, under mixed prices, plenty and 10% buy offers, 54% remain unresolved after observing the first 1,024 steps (95% block interval 45.6-62.4%). A reader entering at step 1,024 leaves all 200 worlds unresolved. These are descriptive constructed-method results, not evidence about people.

All 168 planned settings passed the excluded whole-grid pilot comparison. Forty-four settings were excluded by its declared design gate, leaving 124 settings and 24,800 fresh scientific worlds. This includes 24 post-breach settings. Six known-answer checks, twenty applicable common-seed preflight comparisons, full pilot replay/reconstruction, byte-identical full scientific replay and independent whole-grid reconstruction pass.

The early and late observations above are distinct simulation cells, not paired interventions. In the late cell, 99.5% froze before entry. Once a weight-4 sum has frozen, its individual action law equals the corresponding weight-4 rule. Class-averaged evidence need not equal individual likelihoods. The late reader uses its declared uniform prior over possible current streaks and the already-frozen state, without evaluator prehistory.

In the early cell all 200 freezes were observed and dated; mean absolute dating error was 136.4 steps (95% whole-block interval 112.5-162.6). The date is the first conditional frozen-state posterior crossing 0.5. A date is not proof that the model class has separated. Late-entry dating conditions on the one in-window event and is too sparse to interpret as general timing accuracy.

For the first-breach contrast, scarcity, occasional probe prices, weight 4 and 10% buy offers produce a breach in all 200 worlds of each type. Both types are distinguished from each other in all worlds by the end. Mean records after the breach to a sustained three-nat pairwise distinction are 16.4 (95% whole-block interval 13.9-18.6) when the agent resumes trading and 20.9 (18.1-24.2) when it becomes more resistant. Available post-breach records average 1,802.4 and 1,806.2 respectively. These are conditional means, not medians or all-class separation times. The all-class unresolved shares are 1.5% and 4%; the aggregate retains the unbounded block intervals exactly as calculated.

The post-breach rule change is fixed by construction: THAW resumes its live selling law, whereas STAIN's sell lapse falls to one quarter. The measured waiting times, incidence, uncertainty and failure to distinguish are open results. Fewer than 20% freezing in a pilot is a design exclusion, not a claim that every world remains live. Censored separation, missing breaches and missing dates remain explicit.
