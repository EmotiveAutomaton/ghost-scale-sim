# B3: verified primary map

When do separate social readouts improve prediction over one total? With independent traits, cross-weight 0.5 and an assertiveness penalty of 0.5, two readouts gain 0.01144 nats per held-out decision (95% block interval 0.00994-0.01294) after 256 training records. Adding a third readout gains 0.00621 (corrected block-refit interval 0.00491-0.00703); in the opposed-trait population it loses 0.00070 (-0.00090 to -0.00047). These are descriptive constructed-method results, not evidence that people use these readouts.

The pilot excludes eight exact equal-weight aliases, leaving 16 settings and 3,200 fresh worlds. Seven known-answer controls, full replay and independent reconstruction pass. The main two-readout fit is per agent; the three-readout extension fits shared rank-two loadings, which requires refitting inside the uncertainty calculation. B3-H1 preserves all point estimates and supersedes only the latter intervals and derived first-positive prefixes. Four thousand whole-block bootstrap samples refit both models on paired training and held-out records; independent covariance-based reconstruction agrees within 4.7e-15. The original summaries remain retained. At equal weights, extra readouts are identical by construction. The fitted axes are abstract and are not identified as named psychological gauges.
