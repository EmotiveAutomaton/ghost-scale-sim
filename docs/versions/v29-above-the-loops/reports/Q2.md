# Q2: a bill with and without control

Can a reader distinguish low concern from low belief in control? In the 168-cell constructed map, a passive-prior agent with deficit sensitivity 1 is correctly identified at log odds 3 in 13.3% of 150-step uncontrollable worlds (95% block interval 9.0–17.7%), 58.7% of controllable worlds (52.8–64.5%), and 85.0% after an uncontrollable then controllable episode (80.5–89.5%; 300 steps total). These are descriptive constructed-method results, not evidence about people.

The reader evaluates eight declared combinations of control prior and deficit
sensitivity. The complete map has 144 episode-order/length cells and 24 additional
watched-control cells, with 300 fresh worlds in twelve blocks of 25 per cell.
The separate pilot has 40 worlds per cell; 0 cells were excluded.
Episode orders and record lengths are not interchangeable interventions.
