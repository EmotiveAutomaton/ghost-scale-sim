# B3 frozen primary protocol
The eighteen main cells and six third-readout cells use 200 agents, eight blocks, and 512 records. Traits are uniform over 0,.25,.5,.75,1; generosity is independent or exactly one minus assertiveness. Acts are independent Bernoulli draws conditional on traits. Observers maintain Beta(1,1) cumulative act means: respect is mean assertiveness, trust is mean generosity minus chi times respect. Deference and entrusting probabilities are sigmoid(2*(respect+mu*trust-.75)) and sigmoid(2*(mu*respect+trust-.75)), clipped to [.02,.98]. These scales and learning rates are explicit analyst choices because the packet leaves them open.

ONE-TOTAL fits one pooled Bernoulli probability per agent; TWO-GAUGE fits a probability for each decision. Both use Jeffreys Beta(.5,.5) count smoothing. At mu=1 the known design matrix has rank one, so the two-gauge fit is constrained to the same pooled probability; an extra parameter is not fitted to noise. At each training prefix 8,16,32,64,128,256, the next equally long block is held out for log scoring. The curve's first pointwise 95% block interval above zero is descriptive and is not a multiplicity-corrected decision. Nested prefixes are not independent observations. Inverting the known two-output logistic map gives unconstrained fitted respect/trust estimates when mu differs from one; conditional trait-group summaries retain counts. Those estimates are not available at mu one.

B3b uses chi .5, all three mu values, both populations, and independent warmth on the same five-level grid. Liking is the cumulative Beta(1,1) warmth mean; keep-near probability is sigmoid(2*(liking-.5)). Here two versus three means a rank-two logit fit versus a saturated three-readout fit, with a fitted intercept for each decision. Two abstract fitted axes may rotate; they are not forced to be named respect and trust. SVD and an independent scalar covariance/eigendecomposition give separate numerical fits. At mu one the first two decisions are pooled and the third is free under both readers, preserving the known rank-two identity. This distinction between the main constrained shared-total comparison and B3b's rank comparison limits the claim. A third fitted axis can help only where held-out scores show it; no human three-gauge conclusion follows.

All seven initial controls pass, including held-out isolation, exact mu-one equality, known rank-two reconstruction and independent fit/score checks. Complete excluded pilots, seed replay and whole-grid reconstruction remain mandatory. Lapse and slope textures are applicable; per-world separation censoring and its >30% record-double rule are not defined for this held-out learning-curve family, so that trigger is inapplicable.
