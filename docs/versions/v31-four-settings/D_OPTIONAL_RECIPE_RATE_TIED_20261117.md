# Optional diary estimator: rate tied, no floor

Does the optional diary estimator retain its precision advantage when event rates depend on sensitivity without a rating floor? Two previously verified settings meet the declared recipe trigger: between-world spread is 37.3% and 41.3% lower than the split-day estimator. Their mean biases are -0.00469 and -0.00246. These are constructed-method sensitivity results, not a replacement for a registered primary.

These two trigger notes were missing from the optional-recipe summary set; the underlying aggregates and full verification were already published. No new scientific world or new hypothesis test was run. All 27 eligible triggers in the prior frozen trigger snapshot now have a recipe note.

Each setting uses 2,272 people, ten days, six prompts per day and binary events at mean rate 0.08. Spread is the standard deviation across 200 independent worlds. Bias is the mean difference from the planted correlation.
