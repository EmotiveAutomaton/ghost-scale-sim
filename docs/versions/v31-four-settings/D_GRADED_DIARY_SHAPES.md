# D: graded-event diary-shape map

How well do diary estimates distinguish one sensitivity from competing sensitivities across study sizes? All 216 graded-event mild-floor settings with independent usual levels and event rates are verified: three sample sizes, four diary schedules, three event rates and six populations. These are method calibrations in constructed worlds, not evidence for a human trait.

The complete aggregates also give estimator bias, spread, availability and the fixed ES2 interval classifications. No selection of worlds or clipping of correlations to one is performed.

All 216 cells retain controls, forty excluded pilots, full seed replay and independently reconstructed regression estimates. These graded-event worlds are separate from the binary-event map; both exposure size and the fitted event predictor are graded. Sparse-event diary fits can be unavailable; summary denominators remain explicit. Random-slope moment correction assumes a linear observation model and is not an exact floor-aware Bayesian fit. Pointwise intervals do not adjust for the grid. Miniature architecture is untested.
