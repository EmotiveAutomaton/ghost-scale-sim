# T: prospective fluency distinctions

The four packet kinds have the specified readiness intercepts and prices.
Attention is latent and integrated exactly: without attention behavior follows
readiness; with attention, apply if cost is zero or below the price and no offer
meets/exceeds that price. A habit therefore applies at zero cost without an
offer, but skips any positive cost or takes any positive offer. Use count means
observed applications before the present occasion. Equality with price favors
the opposite action, fixed prospectively.

Cue strengths are uniform on [0,1]. Costs and their rates follow the packet.
Positive offers are equally drawn from 1,2,4,8,16,64,128; their occurrence rates
are 0,0.02,0.10. The effect view observes unit-SD Gaussian marks: habit zero;
heuristic +0.25 when applied; value +1 when applied; identity -1 when refused.
These unspecified effect magnitudes/noise are miniature choices, not estimates
about people. The no-effect view cannot use the retained effect marks.

Primary class evidence uses the four declared laws with equal prior. A separate
action-only rectangular library crosses prices 0,1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128 and
readiness intercepts -1,0,1,2,3. Posterior mean absolute errors measure parameter
confounding; no unequal-size class is selected by an argmax. Attention is known
to the reader in the primary map and its identifiability is not established.

Cross all packet rates, attention values, two views and four truths: 216 cells,
each 2,000 occasions, 40 excluded pilot worlds and 200 fresh worlds in eight
blocks. Three-nat separation must persist to the record's end; unresolved times
remain censored. Universal early separation and observed-probability aliases
are pilot exclusions. Scalar/compiled sequential readers are checked against
vectorized retained-path probability and likelihood reconstruction.

Route: ToMpathy's probe mix. This is a stipulated finite model, not a validated
classification of human habits, values or identities.
