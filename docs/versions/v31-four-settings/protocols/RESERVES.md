# G4 and G7 finite reserves

These are prospective analyst miniatures of the packet's reserve questions,
not asserted human mechanisms. They run after the primary grids and healing,
inside the separate 15-hour reserve including service costs. G6 remains
design-held until its named point-at-it intervention is defined.

G4 uses T's identity readiness, price 64 and offers 128 to do the opposite.
After not applying, a bad feeling is reported with probability 0.75 if its
source's cue is present, otherwise 0.05. The three candidate sources are
re-examination (Knowledge), being seen (a visible need), and rearranging the
plan (Stress). Cue presence is independently randomized at rate 0.2/0.5/0.8,
with a perfectly tied-cue alias control. Views either include those cues or
only action and feeling. Cross attention 0.1/0.5/0.9, offer rate 0.02/0.1,
three sources and both views: 108 independent-cue cells plus six tied controls
at the central setting. Each has 2,000 occasions. The sources have identical
marginal feeling rates; only independently varied contextual cues can tell them.

G7 extends N's three needs (0–6), arrivals 0.15/0.15/0.05 and openings
0.9/0.5/0.1 or all open. An action quiets one open need and leaves a next-step
menu of three, two or one possible actions. The menu labels rotate randomly
on each occasion. Capital is the exact number of distinct need-reducing states
reachable one more action ahead. The option-preserving maker adds 0.5/1/2 times
this count to its immediate need score; its rival adds zero. Both use softmax
temperature 0.5/1, and both readers see the actual gaps, openings and menu.
The menu constrains the next occasion's openings. Twenty-four cells of 1,024
occasions report separation, future options, unmet need and wasted moves.
This is a one-step reachable-futures model, not a theory of all future Capital.

Both retain 40 excluded pilots and 200 worlds in eight blocks, full replay,
independent probability/count reconstruction and known-answer alias controls.
