# W: prospective matching and observation gates

The four settings are provisional theory proposals. A maker updates its context
picture, chooses a held schema, works as a function of smoothed confidence and
may abandon a plan under unresolved stress. The supplied preflight resolved
initial confidence (0.5), the end-of-step leak, and that response times and plan
decisions occur on working steps. Complete supplied paths are reconstructed by
independent full-view arithmetic before production. Initial held schema is
retained explicitly when checking analyst paths.

Owner decision: all pairs involving stress match completed plans per 1,000
steps. Incoming weighting versus swap temperature matches switches per 100
steps; the remaining pairs match work in the true schema. Stress does not enter
the latter measure under the maker equations. Matching keeps those equations.

For each pair, target a 5%/15% increase in switches, or decrease in the other
matching measures, relative to BASE. Prospective search bounds are incoming
weight 0.05..0.95, swap temperature 0.05..2, confidence coefficient 0.05..8 and
stress coefficient 0.05..4. Use four excluded 4,096-step common-random paths to
bracket/refine each response; choose the bracket nearest the BASE parameter.
Validate independently on sixteen 5,000-step paths. Pair difference must be
within the larger of 1% of BASE or three paired standard errors; each target
error within the larger of 2% of BASE or three paired standard errors. Retain
all calibration curves and failures. No matched comparison is admitted when
its target is unreachable or its independent check fails.

V4 uses the complete observed cue/drop/action history with exact likelihood.
V2 is also exact for its specified schema-plus-cue view: the maker's belief
is deterministic given those cues. It cannot see timing or plan evidence.
V1 and V3 have separate bootstrap particle filters, using only their admitted
observations; latent world columns never enter their likelihood. Their sampled
state propagation is shared between arithmetic reconstructions, so independent
resolution and exact-restriction checks are essential. No partial reader is
admitted by a V4 control. Full-length resolution fixtures must pass before
5,000-step production. Aliases under reduced views are fixed, not discoveries.

Nine worlds and two match sizes follow the packet. V4 uses all nine; reduced
views use the centre, calm/clear/actionable and volatile/murky/unactionable
corners. Each admitted sampled cell retains a 40-world excluded pilot, 200
fresh worlds, full replay, independent reconstruction and eight-world-block
intervals. Three-nat separation must persist through the complete record.
W.b and W.c are separate extensions. Resource and validity stops remain explicit.
