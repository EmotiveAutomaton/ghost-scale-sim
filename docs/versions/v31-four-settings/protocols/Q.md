# Q: rank and level

Prospective miniature, architecture untested. There are 64 makers in eight
groups. Personal increments are -1/0/+1 with probabilities 0.05/0.90/0.05;
group increments are -1/0/+1 with probabilities g/2,1-g,g/2. Initially all
standings are zero. The focal maker swaps with a uniformly chosen member of
another group with probability h. Shows have log-odds -2 minus twice the
change in own standing less w times the change in the current group's mean.

The grid crosses g=0/0.03/0.1, h=0/0.01/0.05, w=0/0.5/1 and three views,
2,000 steps, 40 excluded pilot worlds and 200 scientific worlds in eight
blocks. V1 sees own increments and shows. V2 adds seven sorted anonymous
neighbour increments. V3 adds the exact group-mean change reconstructed from
membership and complete standing histories. Membership alone without those
histories is insufficient; V3 is an ideal visibility bound.

LEVEL and RANK have w=0 and 1. MIXED averages the likelihoods at 0.25,0.5,0.75,
with equal priors on the three classes. A separate uniform five-point posterior
reports weight error. Report sustained three-nat separation in steps and shows,
wrong settlement, shows without own events and shows without a true negative
reading change. Unresolved records remain censored. Exclude a pilot only if
every world separates within ten shows, or the compared laws exactly coincide.

V3 is exact. With no moves, V2 is exact and V1 integrates the seven independent
neighbour increments and conditional shared group event by finite convolution.
Moving partial views require a separately checked particle filter; they are
not admitted by the exact controls. Validation compares retained-path arithmetic,
adjacent resolutions and exact no-move restrictions, including full record
length. A failed gate is retained and blocks only the affected reader.

The packet's conditional event-absence follow-on is built separately: when V1
attributes more than one fifth of shows to no own event, compare exact observed
own events with exact rank-reading drops. This tests the event-absence statistic
used by V30 L. L's activation likelihood is not a valid inverse model for Q's
logistic world and will not be silently reused as one.

Route: Sounding Line ES1 and the engine's reading of shows without page events.
