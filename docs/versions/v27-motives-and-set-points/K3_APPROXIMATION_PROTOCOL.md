# Checked changing-state inference

The owner approved particle approximation for K3 on November 7 after exact
changing-state inference exceeded 200,000 hidden states at record 25 of 512.
The exact static controls and completed static primaries remain unchanged.

Before dynamic scientific admission, excluded fixtures cover all five dynamic
laws and all three probe rates at the original 512-record horizon. The hidden
reader receives only actions, restoration sizes and observed shock identities.
Realized stocks, precision, consumption and generating type are never inputs to
its likelihood. Each candidate hypothesis starts from the declared stock prior.

Bootstrap particle likelihood uses systematic resampling at every record.
Deterministic random streams are bound to world identity, candidate set point,
band/overshoot law, particle count and replicate. Dynamic laws at the same point
use common random numbers; distinct replicates remain independent. Each
candidate retains its correct marginal particle distribution. This reduces
artificial differences where the laws predict identical transitions. No likelihood floor is allowed. A
population with zero support is a numerical failure, not evidence against a
model. Exact static likelihoods remain exact wherever the finite recursion is
tractable. Initial resolution levels are 2,048, 8,192 and 32,768 particles, with
two independent replicates per level. The scientific population is selected
prospectively from excluded data only; unresolved checks hold admission.

Validation criteria, declared before the resolution outcomes:

- Independent step weights and resampling agree at absolute tolerance 1e-12;
  complete original-versus-reference likelihood paths agree within 1e-9.
- Short P0 fixtures compare the approximate likelihood with exact recursion.
  At the highest resolution, the maximum prefix log-likelihood error is at most
  0.1 nat across the excluded static controls.
- Across the two highest resolutions and both replicates, the maximum absolute
  difference in marginal dynamic-type posterior is at most 0.05; the maximum
  difference in the S/P-/G comparison margin is at most 0.25 nat.
- Sustained one-nat crossing times agree within 16 records, or all remain
  censored. No fixture may change which side of the 128-record practical bar it
  occupies. Failures trigger higher resolution or an explicitly unresolved
  numerical disposition; no changed bar or silently discarded fixture.

Each setting retains its resolution receipt, excluded identities, all outcomes,
actual CPU cost and source hash. Scientific intervals describe world variability,
not a claim that a finite particle approximation is mathematically exact.
Additional texture dimensions require their own resolution checks before admission.
Previously completed static primaries are referenced, never rerun for occupancy.

declared numerical limits did not. The original source and diagnostic receipt

coupling, preserves the fixture identities, and keeps every tolerance unchanged.

the upper two resolutions under the same rules. Existing excluded calculations
