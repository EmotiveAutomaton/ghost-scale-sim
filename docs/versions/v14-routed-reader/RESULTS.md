# V14 — The Routed Reader. Results (Pass A)

*Written 2026-08-31 from the committed record, after the window's deadline. Every number traces to
a verdict file under `results/validation/soundingline/v14/`; the analyst appendix (Pass B) is
[RESULTS_PACKET.md](RESULTS_PACKET.md), generated by `runners/report_v14.py`. Everything here is a
property of a constructed world and its stated reader. Nothing describes a person.*

## 1. The question

Can one reader recover, from four observation routes that never share a token, three things a
maker never states — how it works (its plan), what it is after in this episode (its goal), and
what it stands for across episodes (its standing preference) — and be honest about which of the
three each route can and cannot reach?

## 2. What was built

A constructed world whose makers have a plan (four kinds, two of them exactly equivalent under
one goal), an episode goal drawn from a standing preference, a competence (how reliably intention
becomes action) and a practice history (which transitions and surface features got worn in).
Four disjoint routes carry evidence: the realized action chain, semantic tokens about the goal,
context choices from the preference, and a costly forensic record of the intended head. On top:
an exact joint reader over the full 96-state grid and its cheaper rivals (independent marginals,
plug-in commitment pipelines); a route-reliability learner; a six-way factored source model for
communicated affect (belief, content support, sought appraisal, selection policy, correction
record, off-audience action); a reward-equivalent hierarchy; and a learning-progress forager.
64 mandatory cards in 8 trunks, 12 attacks, one 24-hour window, tier T3 with all six expansions
(256 discovery worlds, 96 makers per world), pre-registered criteria and three locks.

## 3. What happened, flight by flight

Each flight states whether the result is about a **method** or about a **mechanism of this
constructed world**.

**1. Joint reconstruction advantage — an honest null (mechanism).** The exact joint posterior
beats independent per-latent marginals on the hidden next action by **+0.011 nats** against a
pre-registered bar of 0.02, and beats the best plug-in commitment order by +0.002 (J04); on fresh
families and vocabularies the margin is the same +0.011 (J10, transfer). By two to four episodes
of evidence the marginals' product is nearly the joint, so *for prospective prediction at this
evidence level, jointness buys almost nothing* — while each latent is individually recoverable
(J02, J03, confirmed: 0.51 on the true goal by four steps, 0.85 on the true preference by eight
episodes) and no commitment order wins everywhere (J05). The instruments themselves are clean:
equifinal plans hold 0.91–0.95 of their mass as a class with no single member over 0.53, and one
forensic observation resolves the class to 0.99 (J01, J08); a changed goal and a changed
preference are detected at 0.95 and 0.81 with cross-confusion 0.002 (J09). Attacks: the small
margin survives surface renaming and matched-competence attacks, narrows under surface ablation
and hidden reversal (X05, X11) — consistent with a real but thin effect.

**2. Reliable routing — an honest null with a sharp positive control (mechanism).** Learned route
reliability beats equal weighting by **+0.009 nats** against a 0.02 bar in family (R02), and
*negative* out of family (attack lineage, −0.017). What does hold, confirmed on the untouched
lineage: an ease-driven reader is the planted failure — making the uninformative route cheap
pulls 0.93 of its weight there and costs it +0.107 nats, while the learned reader's score moves
**0.000** across the same ease manipulations (R03; R04: dear accuracy moves the learned reader
0.000, the ease-driven one 0.048). The route–latent map is as planted (action→plan,
semantic→goal, context→preference; forensic worth 0.99 nats about the plan, R01). Duplicated
semantic evidence inflates a naive fuser by +0.07 and a shared-cause fuser by 0.000 (R07). Active
forensic purchase by expected information gain did *not* beat the best fixed policy (R06,
criterion failed: +0.146 net against +0.164). After a domain shift, partial transfer of old
weights ties a full reset (R08).

**3. Competence–history dissociation — the clean mechanism result (mechanism; confirmed).**
Competence moves execution accuracy by +0.33 and leaks 0.001 into the history measure; practice
history moves early-token relevance by +1.55 and leaks 0.000 into execution accuracy (E01,
confirmed). Two makers of identical skill are told apart by *how they practiced* from held-out
early transitions **99%** of the time at a skill gap of 0.000 (E08). The planted attention residue
decays after its reward reverses exactly on the planted law, to 6% within eight episodes (E04);
feedback removes 56% of a stale route bias without costing skill (E05, accuracy 0.93→0.95). Both
factors transfer whole to a fresh domain (E07, discovery and transfer). The one null: at a single
episode, a high-competence maker's relevance tilt is detected no better than a low-competence
one's (E06 — the tilt estimate is competence-independent by construction, so the card's premise
found nothing to find). On the maker's next novel choice the preference model wins over
competence and history models (E10, +0.35 nats).

**4. Affect–source factorization — the strong result (method and mechanism; confirmed).** With
the artifact matched by construction — a sincere fanatic and a strategic propagandist emit the
same assertion, the same evidence distribution, the same intensity — the artifact alone separates
them at exactly chance (0.50) and the reader keeps its larger mass at 0.50 (abstention); the
off-audience action separates them at **90%** (A06, confirmed; A05: four regions 50% from the
artifact, 85% with both probes against a 25% chance floor). Seven owners stay separate: each
moves its own quantity by ≥0.28 and the reader-side quantities move the source posteriors by at
most 0.012 (A01). Projecting one's own response onto the audience is a similarity bet: +0.13 nats
when the reader responds like the modelled audience, −0.51 when it does not (A02). An
audience-aware reader beats a face-value reader by **+11.7 nats** on the belief of a maker that
selects evidence to steer it, and by +0.011 against a plain maker (A07). Influence awareness with
the source's record keeps true uptake at 0.69 while cutting false uptake to 0.02, where the
face-value reader takes both at 0.98 (A08); the acute response habituates 0.58→0.02 while belief
in the content rises 0.33→0.89 (A09); the factored uptake gate moves policy uptake by 0.30 with a
belief side-effect of 0.000, where a scalar gate moves belief 0.35 (A10). The 0.906 separation is
unmoved by every relevant attack.

**5. Learning-progress foraging — mostly nulls, one exportable piece (mechanism).** The
learning-progress policy escapes the noise trap (4% of looks on familiar noise against 41% for
raw surprise) but realizes **no more** held-out gain (+0.23 vs +0.19, under the 0.1-nat bar; F04)
— and under a curriculum whose laws change, raw surprise beats it by **4.13 nats** (F05): progress
measured on the learner's own settling uncertainty fails to re-engage when the world silently
changes. Gain-per-cost is the defensible selector: at least its best rival per cost (F06), 0.08
regret on fresh ecologies, and **100% abstention when no item can teach anything** (F08,
transfer). Pursuit and warrant separate cleanly: the reader spends 83% of its queries on the
attractive hypothesis while its posterior on it stays at 0.21 (F07). F01/F02's criteria failed on
their declared bars (factor rank-correlation 0.91 against 0.3; progress preferring the unresolved
at 36%).

**Infrastructure and the V13 repairs.** All five construction cards held (I01–I05: anchors to
0.0043, exact enumeration to brute force within 0.003, routes independently live, collisions and
equifinality exact, lineages disjoint). The three pre-registered V13 repairs ran and **failed
their criteria** — the repaired instruments are healthy, the V13 effects are not there: a
common-substrate prior is worth −0.000 nats after one artifact against an all-family prior (bar
0.69, I06); the self prior *loses* to the common-population prior for typical readers (−0.69) and
anti-similar ones (−0.77) at one artifact (I07); the self-prior correction near/far asymmetry did
not reproduce (41% vs 44% error removed, I08). V13's C03/C05/P01 stay closed, now with their
repairs on record.

## 4. What did not land

Nothing was withheld as an instrument failure: **all 64 mandatory cards and all 12 attacks
landed**. Eleven discovery cards landed with their pre-registered criterion **failed** — I06, I07,
I08, J04, R02, R06, E06, F01, F02, F04, F05 — and one transfer card (J10). That is the honest-null
set above, each with the number that decided it. A withheld result and a null are different
things; this version has nulls.

## 5. What the validation could not check

The bars are single-construction bars: 0.02 nats is what the spec called an advantage worth
claiming, and nothing here says the joint advantage or the routing advantage would stay thin in a
world with stronger latent coupling or scarcer evidence. The smoke standard (six worlds, small
sizes) passed 24 cards whose gates then failed at scale during the program's first hours; the
mid-run repairs (a receipt rule, and separating gate bars from criterion bars on 26 gate lines)
are recorded as amendments with the superseded verdicts kept beside them — the criteria themselves
were never touched. The coexistence signal saw no Sounding Line stage overrun, so the governor
never fired in anger. PyMDP was not reopened; exact enumeration is the only inference here.

## 6. The window

The first 24-hour window (opened 2026-08-29 12:10) was **voided on record** after the runner's
process tree was killed seven times by the sibling project's orphan sweeper, which terminates any
python whose command line matches `runners[\\/]run_` — the cause of V13's four "unexplained"
silent deaths as well; the void, the cause and the fix (module-form launch) are in
`results/v14/DEADLINE_VOIDED_1.json` and AMENDMENTS.json. The second window opened 2026-08-30
07:07 and ran clean: discovery, transfer and attacks done by hour 6.0 (6.76 wall-clock hours of
card runs), freeze at hour 20.0 exactly — J02, R03, E01, A06, one per flight under the committed
rule, which leaves the foraging flight without a slot (cap 4, flights in declared order) — all
four **held** at confirmation on the untouched lineage; closure at hour 20.1; SHORT_RUN recorded;
no card was refused by the deadline.
