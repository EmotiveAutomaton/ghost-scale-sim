beyond a properly trained, equally informed direct reader? **No learned-maker

establish a maker model as the method that recovers it.

## What the studies found

The rows group the tested questions. A verified result means the finite
instrument, independent calculations and required complete family replay agree;
it does not imply that a proposed prediction succeeded. Every finding below is
a constructed-method result with its supplied-information privileges retained.

| Question | Result and practical implication |
|---|---|
| Did V20's changed-context advantage demonstrate transferable maker structure? | Removing duplicated evidence removes the apparent transfer benefit. The historical V20 confirmation remains on record, with its interpretation narrowed by [the duplicate audit](OPENING_REPORT.md). |
| Does shared-maker inference use personal historical content? | Three descriptive candidates appeared; two survived generic dependence controls, but neither survived the decisive personal-content interpretation test. [Personalization scale-up stopped](LEARNED_PERSISTENCE_REPORT.md). |
| Does modular execution adaptation reach the registered target faster? | No reader reaches the 0.1-nat excess-loss target in the six tested cells. The quarter-label criterion is unmet; supplied two-kernel adaptation does not establish unrestricted rapid adaptation. [Adaptation report](ADAPTATION_REPORT.md). |
| Is all apparent direct-reader error an information limit? | No. The validation-stopped direct reader leaves a 0.167-nat gap on 32 reserved worlds. Considerable historical ambiguity remains even for the exact reader. [Final diagnostic](FINAL_DIAGNOSTIC_REPORT.md). |
| Can record assumptions exclude the true history? | Yes. Learned hard support and hard interpretations of fallible reports exclude truth, creating infinite uncapped losses. Soft weighting helps under supplied reliability. [Reliability](RELIABILITY_REPORT.md), [support](WIDE_CONTEXT_SUPPORT_REPORT.md). |
| Which missing or shared records carry information? | Selective absence can favor inspection while random absence does not. Copied evidence, provenance and role histories require distinct observation models; equally informed direct inference often matches the latent representation. [Inspection](INSPECTION_REPORT.md), [provenance](PROVENANCE_REPORT.md), [authorship](AUTHORSHIP_REPORT.md). |
| Is predictive or executable equivalence historical identity? | No. A predictive memory can discard witnesses, and successful reenactment does not recover the actual route. Knowledge and permission remain exactly aliased without a separating witness. [Memory](MEMORY_REPORT.md), [routes](ROUTES_REPORT.md), [factors](FACTORS_REPORT.md). |
| Does choosing evidence help? | Supplied-law target purchasing improves fee-adjusted loss. Sequential lookahead resolves complementary evidence but does not beat the useful fixed pair on reconstruction; practical computation-adjusted gain remains unestablished. [Acquisition](ACQUISITION_REPORT.md), [sequential study](SEQUENTIAL_REPORT.md). |
| Are contribution, candidate coverage and score choice interchangeable? | No. Selecting a plan changes content without rewriting it. Expanding a finite catalog improves exact truth coverage while the coarse unknown-category score can worsen. Retraction matches recomputation without a CPU/storage advantage. [Selection](SELECTION_REPORT.md), [omission](OMISSION_REPORT.md), [retraction](RETRACTION_REPORT.md). |
| Do partial answers, style locality or model expansion establish a special maker capability? | Fixed-specificity answer benefit is unestablished against the calibrated rival; a small adaptive matched-error difference remains. Supplied parser locality and finite omission detection are useful bounded findings, with double inversion, exact shift invariance and unnecessary expansion limiting broader claims. [Answers](CALIBRATED_ANSWER_REPORT.md), [style](STYLE_REPORT.md), [expansion](EXPANSION_REPORT.md). |

These results distinguish information supplied to a reader from representation,
fitting, historical correspondence and decision policy. They support keeping
source witnesses and alternative histories explicit. They do not support a
general personalization advantage, universal transfer claim, human intent
measurement or deployment guarantee. Several purpose-built worlds remain
miniatures whose broader architecture has not been tested.
