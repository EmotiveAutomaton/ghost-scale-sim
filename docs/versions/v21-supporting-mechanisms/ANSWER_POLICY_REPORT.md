# V21 full, partial and withheld answers

Does a partial answer improve reconstruction beyond simply making fewer claims? The native artifact reader makes 1.07–1.95 percentage points fewer false claims when exactly four to six fields are asserted than the best original same-label rival. The shifted candidates do not establish a corresponding partial-answer advantage. These are verified constructed-method development results on reused forecasts; the calibrated episode-policy rival remains untested, and no promotion or confirmation follows.

Six conditions reuse 196,608 saved forecasts from eight original readers. Artifact conditions use 8,192 labels, context and complete conditions 512, each with four same-tier past episodes, inspection hidden, eight training laws, eight development worlds and 256 cases per world. Changed execution affects current and historical episodes; this is not the current-only adaptation intervention. Two fits are averaged within each world before 4,096 paired whole-world bootstrap draws. Reused cases, fitted seeds, decisions and penalty ratios are not independent replications.

Every reader can assert any subset of seven binary target fields or withhold all fields. The loss is the fraction of fields withheld plus a penalty of 1, 2, 5 or 10 whenever any asserted field is wrong. Withholding everything costs one. These stipulated decision losses are distinct from proper probability losses. The policy minimizes its own expected loss, which need not equal realized loss under miscalibration. The declared near-tie tolerance is 1e-12; lower mask/assignment and fewer assertions resolve ties.

Independent enumeration checks all 2,187 partial assignments for every original forecast, the best assignment at every cardinality, adaptive decisions, all realized and expected losses, false-specificity flags, coverage and retained alternatives. All 7,077,888 original policy rows reconstruct within 1.65e-14. The full artifact-native replay matches all 4,609 files byte for byte, verifying another 1,179,648 decisions by exact correspondence. Seven new packet reviews bind completion, plans, source archives and environment; six upstream score receipts are reused only after matching hashes. The numerical review uses the frozen scientific source and its retained complete blocks.
