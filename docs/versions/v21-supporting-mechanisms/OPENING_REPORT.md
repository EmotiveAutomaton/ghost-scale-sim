# V21 opening: duplicated evidence explains the V20 shift benefit

We asked whether V20's shifted advantage reflects transferable structure or
duplicated evidence. Removing the duplicated evidence feature eliminates naive
Bayes's shift benefit. These are constructed-method results on reused V20 worlds,
not fresh confirmation or evidence of general transfer.

The complete V20 confirmation training inputs were retained. We reconstructed
the naive-Bayes forecasts exactly for both fitted seeds on all 32 former
confirmation worlds, then removed the evidence feature or the claim feature
from both current and past episodes. Native and changed-execution means are
paired by coefficient world; the two fits are averaged within each world before
4,096 whole-world bootstrap draws. Cases are not independent replications.

The table reports changed-execution loss minus native loss, in nats (natural-log
units); negative values mean the shift improved that reader. The interval is a
descriptive 95% whole-world bootstrap interval, not a new confirmation test.

| Reader | Mean loss change | Descriptive interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Original naive Bayes | −0.04769 | −0.05996 to −0.03535 |
| Evidence feature removed | −0.00358 | −0.01331 to 0.00651 |
| Claim feature removed | 0.10046 | 0.08776 to 0.11338 |

The benefit disappears when the duplicated evidence factor is removed. Keeping
the evidence factor while removing the claim instead makes the shift harmful.
This reproduces the errata's cancellation explanation. Carry the historical
comparison as a native-trained naive-Bayes reader losing less than a fixed-schedule
MLP under changed execution because the shift cancels native double counting of
claim and evidence. It is not evidence that structured inference transfers.

The verifier's first attempt repeatedly decompressed lazy NPZ arrays. Its exact
owned process was stopped, the attempt and conservative CPU charge retained,
and the I/O adapter changed to load each array once before the same scalar checks.
The repaired verifier passed, and a regression proves one read per array. Scores,
criteria, scientific sources and historical V20 records were unchanged.

This audit does not establish a better learned maker model, independently reproduce
the errata's converged-MLP advantage, or open fresh confirmation worlds. Those
questions belong to the active headroom, optimizer and learned-reader blocks.
The independent controls validate these finite constructions and arithmetic;
they do not establish the realism of the generator or human historical inference.

## Pursuit and warrant

Pursue genuine maker sharing, dependent-evidence handling, asymmetric history,
probability representation and sparse-mechanism adaptation. The remaining rival
is a converged direct reader with matched evidence and finite training labels;
the fresh-law direct reader separately probes the generator limit.

The warrant from this completed batch is narrower: the old shift benefit depends
on duplicated factors. The next discriminator is exact-law headroom beyond the
converged direct reader, followed by the registered shared-maker and adaptation
comparisons in cells that pass that gate.
