### What the implementation actually does

- The generator enumerates 24,576 weighted trajectories across 32 makers and eight contexts. The seven-bit target combines proposal route, selection, revision, order, inspection and two co-occurring aims. It is a 128-answer abstraction, not a complete psychological history.
- `changed-tool` flips dependency repair under **both** human and tool proposal routes. Use the report's accurate term **changed execution**, not evidence for tool-specific skill inference.
- The learned `structured-joint` arm multiplies feature likelihoods conditional on the current 128-way target. It is a naive-Bayes conditional-evidence model, not a learned causal interpreter.
- Past episodes are concatenated as 13-feature blocks. The exact oracle keeps a shared 32-state maker latent while updating on their likelihoods; the learned structured model does not explicitly retain that latent. Longer histories can therefore expose conditional-dependence and overcounting problems as well as sample scarcity.
- Every learned reader receives the exact oracle's legal-support mask. A result under this mask does not show that a reader recovered unknown mechanics or unknown legal histories.
- Complete observed records still conceal adopted aims; inspection is only visible when the separate observed-inspection setting is enabled. A low exact-answer score is not automatically a decoding failure.
- G3 proves an information witness: histories with opposite orders can have identical maker state and future endpoint distributions. A prediction bank can be sufficient for future endpoints while insufficient for the past. This is not learned-memory superiority.
- G4's oracle chooses acquisitions but shares the learned decoder afterward. It is not a perfect-reader ceiling. G5 expands inside a fixed 128-answer universe. G6's ledger and bounded-raw arms use the same decoder.

## 2. Theory commitments and adversarial alternatives

**Pursuit ledger:** maintained alternatives, genuine shared-maker inference, episode records and targeted acquisition all remain promising engineering ingredients. A successful small personal predictor could provide a useful signal to a larger reader without becoming a historical memory store. The user wants possible historical steps as the process tree; the simulator should test correspondence and support, not quietly substitute a reader-enactable explanation.

**Warrant ledger:** none of these representation choices has a universal superiority result. Direct tables have repeatedly matched richer constructions; V19's supported joint-process comparison did not establish a practical prediction-bank advantage over frozen latent state. Preserving a source ledger may improve historical recovery because it preserves additional information. That does not establish a special inferential representation advantage. [V19 report](https://github.com/EmotiveAutomaton/ghost-scale-sim/blob/e347de02aa098b6fb9a905868924cec1b481d5d7/docs/versions/v19-local-maker/FINAL_REPORT.md).

Local predictions to register before implementing literature-derived ideas:

1. Shared-maker modeling should help most when several dependent episodes jointly identify persistent dispositions, and less when each episode identifies its target alone.
2. A decisive selection may constrain the whole artifact despite zero wording change. Intervention count and character edit distance will therefore be inadequate proxies for decision importance in some exact worlds.
3. Historical records should outperform endpoint-only analysis for distinctions whose traces disappear; some latent aims will remain ambiguous even with every observed operation.
4. More source copies should not equal more independent evidence. An increasingly certain reader can become less accurate under dependent or misspecified evidence.
5. Better fitting the artifact and predicting future outputs need not improve recovery of the historical path.

Rivals: extra labels, privileged support, better optimization, different model capacity, hidden identifiers, duplicate evidence and changing the target can each manufacture an apparent reconstruction benefit. Each branch below names the rival it separates.

## 5. Population, scoring and invariants

Do not call them untouched for a new claim. The executing agent must verify which IDs were actually accessed; choose a fresh disjoint range rather than assuming historical challenge IDs stayed private.

Starter design: 8–16 development worlds per mechanism screen; 32 untouched confirmation worlds for promoted comparisons when pilot cost fits. Two fitted seeds are nested within worlds, not two independent replications. Start with 512/2,048/8,192 labels only where a learning curve distinguishes model bias from variance; expand to 32,768 or 131,072 only if the specified curve remains unresolved. Exact enumeration should replace sampling for information-ceiling calculations when affordable.

Primary engine comparison: proper joint squared probability loss and/or uncapped log score when representable in the log domain, frozen before results. Preserve the old capped score as a compatibility diagnostic, explicitly labeled capped. Report exact-zero probability, positive below-floor mass, marginal losses, exact joint accuracy, top-k coverage, incompatible-joint mass, incorrect 90%-confidence attribution, and computation cost separately. **Do not turn metric disagreement into a single victory label.**

For supplied-law p and forecast q, exact expected Brier regret can be evaluated as `sum((q-p)^2)` while irreducible uncertainty is `1-sum(p^2)`. Their sum is expected Brier loss. Expected log loss decomposes into entropy plus KL only when the declared distributions/support make it valid. This gives a direct error budget instead of interpreting all wrong answers as information failure.

Every complete block includes all declared rivals, a blind input, separate evaluator truth, source IDs, predictions, score arrays, independent regrouping, readable cases and full costs. Process targets, next-choice prediction and contribution weighting stay separate. A historical process gain may coexist with unchanged future prediction, and vice versa.

Controls shared across cards: exact revealing-evidence recovery; deliberately aliased histories; irrelevant evidence; repeated-source identity; reordered independent evidence; anonymous-ID permutation; missing candidate truth; impossible joint; corrupted score rejection; matched support/labels; hidden-field rejection; false-to-true, retraction and irrelevant-update contrasts. An invalid ruler is `INSTRUMENT FAILED`, not a negative mechanism result.

## 6. Branch forest

All cards below are **new proposed designs**. They are not existing implemented handlers. A card becomes runnable only after its complete consumer and independent checker pass and its measured cost fits. Each listed grid is finite; use a small discriminating screen first, then branch along a named unresolved dimension. The grid is not an instruction to run an indiscriminate Cartesian product.

### A. Explain the accuracy ceiling and the V20 metric split

**G21-A01 — Separate uncertainty from decoding error.** Reaggregate retained V20 candidate/native forecasts where locally available; add fresh supplied-law enumerations only under the new allocation. Grid: four evidence tiers × history 0/4/64 × native/changed execution. Compute exact conditional entropy, Bayes modal accuracy, Brier uncertainty and regret for each reader. Rival: the entire gap is unavoidable ambiguity. Continue to A02 where multiple incompatible targets remain; to B01 where substantial excess regret remains. Stop after every target has a valid error-budget row; do not train for this card. Output: information-versus-reader-failure table and cases usable by Sounding Line.

**G21-A02 — Equivalence-class histories.** Partition trajectories by observed evidence and then by the seven-bit target, with exact transition identities where possible. Compare exact-history, partial-history and equivalence-class scoring; grid: artifact/context/sparse/complete, inspection hidden/observed. Rival: an apparently wrong exact answer still describes a supported member of the same equivalence class. Continue when a new observable separates classes; stop if the proposed cue leaves all class likelihoods identical. Export class mass and individual-member uncertainty, never relabel coarse accuracy as exact historical accuracy.

**G21-A03 — Coarse-to-fine answer policy.** From unchanged joint forecasts, choose full process, partial field set or abstention at loss ratios 1/2/5/10 for false specificity. Match an abstaining direct rival. Score coverage, expected loss and number of alternatives retained. Rival: gains are simply fewer claims. Continue to fresh-law confirmation only if a policy improves loss at matched specificity or specificity at matched error. Stop when all apparent benefit is changing coverage. Product consumer: how to expose an honest historical tree without silently omitting plausible routes.

**G21-A04 — Witnessed inspection versus hidden inspection.** Add only the already separable observation switch, then vary missingness 0/0.25/0.5/0.75 and false-record rates 0/0.05/0.2. Include missing-at-random and inspection-dependent missingness. Compare models that recognize the observation process with a naive absent-means-no-inspection reader. Continue to D02 if selective logging matters. Stop the naive historical claim when absence has no identifying power. This tests a concrete analogue of Git history gaps, not whether version control records thought.

### B. Test genuine maker sharing rather than multiplying more features

**G21-B01 — Shared-maker conditional model.** Learn a finite maker-latent model from permitted training labels, then marginalize maker state across histories; compare V20 naive Bayes, direct MLP, direct frequency table and a grouped-history empirical rival. Grid: history 0/1/4/16/64, label budgets 512/2,048/8,192. Truth-maker identities may be used only in a separately labeled supervised diagnostic; the primary learner cannot receive them. Continue when shared-latent benefit survives label/support matching; to B02 if direct groups match; stop after one bounded fitting repair if collapsed states eliminate the intended manipulation. Consumer: whether a tiny personal predictor is an informative context producer.

**G21-B02 — Grouped evidence and dependence tempering.** Replace independent feature multiplication with episode-group likelihoods; compare full Bayes, composite likelihood with fixed calibration-selected powers 0.25/0.5/1, and a learned pooled empirical model. Grid: independent histories versus repeated/partly copied episodes at 0/0.5/0.9 duplication. Rival: B01's gain came from suppressing overconfidence rather than learning a maker. Continue if grouping improves both calibration and proper loss under fresh duplication rates; stop a maker-mechanism interpretation if generic tempering matches it. Do not tune temperature on final worlds.

**G21-B03 — Persistence strength.** Vary the probability an episode comes from the same maker at 1/0.9/0.5/0 while keeping context and episode count matched. Compare correctly informed mixture, inferred mixture and rigid same-maker model. Include shuffled-author histories. Continue if the inferred mixture detects contamination without discarding useful persistence. Stop personalization scale-up if unrelated histories help equally. This is a clean information ruler for author corpus contamination and ghostwriting in Sounding Line.

**G21-B04 — Stable preference versus changing skill.** Introduce one independently controlled change in preference, execution skill or context law; positions at 25%/50%/75% of a 32- or 96-episode stream. Compare static maker, all-fast updating, all-slow updating and a mixture over which role changed. Match direct chronological models. Continue to B05 when role-specific updating earns a held-out benefit; stop any attribution to stable values when the model cannot distinguish skill change. Use paired prefixes so horizon differences are not confounded with different streams, a known qualification in earlier Ghost work.

**G21-B05 — Compress a personal predictor without losing episodes.** Compare raw history, sufficient maker posterior, a small predictive head, source-linked episode ledger and predictor-plus-ledger at matched byte ceilings 256/1,024/4,096/16,384. Evaluate future endpoint prediction separately from past-order and inspection recovery. Rival: compression merely throws away historical information not needed for prediction. Continue if a hybrid offers a useful cost/quality frontier; stop calling predictive compression historical memory when it fails the order witness. No per-person LLM adapter is necessary in Ghost; Sounding Line tests that implementation.

**G21-B06 — Knowledge, ability and external constraints.** Extend a tiny exact world with separate knowledge-of-option, execution skill and permitted-action variables, each binary initially; keep goals constant in counterfactual twins. Compare a goal-only model, skill-only model, joint model and matched direct reader. Reveal one factor at a time. Continue when the new factor explains a specific error and improves held-out predictions; stop expansion after exact aliasing shows no available cue separates the accounts. Source inspiration: robust IRL's subjective dynamics model, not a claim that the toy variables measure human traits.

### C. Joint probability and candidate structure

**G21-C01 — Same-reader factorization ablation.** Start from one fixed joint predictor q; derive its exact marginals and form their product without retraining. Compare the unmasked joint and product, both after a common support projection, and a support-only template. A mask can reintroduce dependencies, so separate learned dependence from constraint enforcement. Grid: four evidence tiers and weak/strong planted target dependence. Rival: V20's separately trained factorized model failed from optimization rather than lost dependence. Continue to C02 if a dependence loss remains; stop a causal jointness claim based solely on separately trained heads.

**G21-C02 — Matched learned joint architectures.** Compare direct 128-way head, autoregressive seven-bit head, pairwise factor graph and low-rank joint tensor with matched evidence, label counts and either matched parameter or matched CPU frontiers. Use 2/3 registered widths, chosen on engineering worlds; report fit cost rather than claiming perfect capacity matching. Test two variable orders. Continue only if joint-loss benefit is robust to ordering and compute. Stop architecture searches at the finite registered settings; an optimizer failure earns one repair, not unlimited tuning.

**G21-C03 — Oracle support privilege ladder.** Compare no support, broad physically legal support, learned support and supplied exact support. Freeze the training source of every support rule. Grid: native and shifted laws; true support omitted at 0/0.01/0.05/0.2. All V20 rivals already shared exact support; its presence alone cannot explain their relative difference. Test the method-by-support interaction and whether any gain survives removing deployment-unavailable knowledge. Continue when coverage and gain survive without oracle support. Stop deployment promotion when gain disappears or incorrect pruning creates unrecoverable errors. Consumer: hard constraints versus tentative hypotheses.

**G21-C04 — Soft inconsistency versus hard rejection.** Supply evidence reliability 1/0.95/0.8/0.5 and compare hard masks with soft likelihood factors, with matched source truth. Include a false but apparently authoritative record. Score true-history exclusion, confident errors and proper loss. Continue if the soft model protects against incorrect exclusion while using reliable records effectively. Stop hard constraints for fields whose source model admits noise. A real commit log can be truthful about edits while its inferred semantic label is wrong; keep those levels distinct.

**G21-C05 — Candidate omission and calibrated unknown mass.** Omit 0/10/25/50% of true candidate types using train-frozen mechanism-based omission, not test-label-informed ranking. Compare forced choice, an explicit finite-unknown bucket, uniform expansion, evidence-ranked expansion and oracle supplied truth. Budgets 8/16/32/64. Continue where coverage and full-universe loss both improve; stop an apparent gain confined to candidate-event scoring. Preserve that this is finite-universe omitted mass, not open-world concept invention.

**G21-C06 — Evidence-driven model expansion.** Start with a model missing a belief, constraint or operation-order variable; offer a finite registered successor library. Compare confidence-triggered expansion, posterior predictive surprise, expected gain per cost and fixed expansion. Grid: omission present/absent × weak/strong cues × search budgets 1/2/4. Rival: a wrong model can be confidently wrong. Continue if held-out predictive checks find omitted mechanisms without chronic unnecessary expansion. Stop expanding when no successor changes likelihood distinctions. This provides a known-answer stress test for AutoToM-style variable adjustment.

### D. Acquire and correct records

**G21-D01 — Information gain versus prediction entropy.** Offer three record classes: operation event, contrasting-context episode and noisy maker cue. Compare expected information gain about historical target, action entropy, fixed cheapest, random and no acquisition at fees 0/0.01/0.05/0.2. Include a deliberately irreducibly random distractor. Score actual target-loss reduction per total acquisition plus decoder cost. Continue if selection beats the strongest cheap policy; stop if reduced entropy fails to improve the target or merely buys more records. Research anchor: active IRL's random-action trap.

**G21-D02 — Incomplete version history.** Generate true event streams, then observed records under independent thinning, squashed commits, delayed batching and event-dependent deletion. Hold final artifact fixed in matched twins. Compare endpoint-only, observed-record reader, observation-process-aware reader and full-record oracle. Rates 0/0.25/0.5/0.75; squash windows 1/2/4. Continue on a named missingness mechanism if modeling it improves held-out historical coverage. Stop treating record completeness as known when metadata cannot identify it. Consumer: Git history offers useful targets but not complete mental-process ground truth.

**G21-D03 — Copied evidence with unknown source groups.** Vary 1/2/4/8 reports from 1/2/4 independent roots; hide or corrupt source IDs in 0/10/30% of cases. Compare supplied provenance, inferred dependence, naive multiplication and a conservative correlation bound. Continue if inferred dependence gains survive source-ID permutation and new copying graphs; stop if advantage requires perfectly supplied root identities. V20 already tested known copying; this branch addresses the useful remaining uncertainty instead of repeating it.

**G21-D04 — Sequential acquisition and stopping.** Permit up to 1/2/4/8 purchases. Compare one-step greedy information, a two-step lookahead, fixed-budget acquisition and uncertainty-threshold stopping. Include evidence complementarities: neither record alone resolves order but both do. Score net loss and missed-identification opportunity. Continue if lookahead wins after charging computation; stop when savings vanish under realistic costs. Preserve separate objectives for learning a maker, recovering one episode and learning to perform the task.

**G21-D05 — Context retraction with genuinely distinct implementations.** Compare frozen conclusion, append-only summary, raw evidence recomputation and an incremental factor/episode ledger. The ledger must compute incrementally rather than call the same raw decoder. Updates: true correction, false correction, retraction, irrelevant addition, unchanged repeat. Grid: history 4/16/64, source reliability 0.6/0.8/1. Continue if incremental recomputation matches the full posterior and improves latency/storage without correction-specificity failures. Stop after one implementation repair if numerical equivalence cannot be independently established. This extends G6 instead of relabeling shared code as two successes.

**G21-D06 — Contrasting opportunities identify stable preferences.** Hold the maker fixed across 1/2/4/8 contexts with different permitted actions or costs; select contexts randomly, for diversity or by expected target information. Compare population and personal models under identical observations. Score stable-preference and skill accuracy separately, with fresh contexts held out. Continue if preferences transfer after constraints change; stop stable-value attribution when a context-only predictor matches. This is the exact analogue of seeking several kinds of a person's work, informed by environment-design IRL.

## 7. Branch dependencies and automatic continuation

Opening independent lanes: A01/A02 from retained evidence; C01 from existing forecasts; B01 from the unchanged world; D02's tiny record projection. A02 must pass before exact ambiguity is used as a scientific claim. B06/E01/E02 require new-world admission. D01/D04 need a valid scored reader, but they do not require B01 to win. F03 can begin from existing valid witnesses while learned branches run.

Branch outcomes have operational meaning:

- `PROMISING`: a useful difference survives the named rival. Admit its predeclared robustness successor and consider frozen confirmation.
- `PARITY`: estimate precise enough to rule out the chosen practical difference. Keep the cheaper method and move to an independent mechanism question.
- `UNRESOLVED`: a learning curve or interval is genuinely too broad. One predeclared precision/label expansion is eligible, whether the effect sign is positive or negative.
- `INSTRUMENT FAILED`: repair once under a new identity, then stop the affected family and continue independent work. Do not count a broken reader as a null mechanism.
- `INFORMATION BOUNDARY`: exact observation-equivalent histories cannot be distinguished in that evidence regime. Move to record acquisition or coarser answers; more fitting does not create information.
- `RESOURCE DEFERRED`: useful work does not fit measured limits or the deadline. Preserve its manifest and rationale; this is neither failure nor exhaustion.

No global gate requires every earlier family to win before any later independent family begins. No empty queue permits invention of unlimited unreviewed science. Before dispatch, each successor must state the leading explanation, the rival and the observation that will separate them. Queue-drain review promotes a predeclared independent card or writes a substantive useful-work shortfall. It never pads occupancy with duplicate valid jobs.
