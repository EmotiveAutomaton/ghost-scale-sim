## Probability and reader validity

The law-marginal posterior integrates joint evidence and target masses over all
12,500 original laws (40,500 widened laws), then normalizes. It must not average
already normalized per-law posteriors. Histories use log-domain arithmetic.
At zero history, independent multilinear coefficients permit exact integration
at their means. Finite Monte Carlo mixtures are engineering screens and must not
admit an information-boundary claim or the B/C headroom gate by themselves.

The supplied-law posterior is a privileged diagnostic. Report irreducible
supplied-law uncertainty, uncertainty about the law, and reader excess separately
in natural-log loss and **full multiclass Brier**. A half-Brier consumer divides
the full Brier by two. Per-case decompositions telescope; finite-world differences
need not all be positive. Include per-bit Bayes accuracy, modal accuracy, top-eight
coverage, incompatible mass, exact zeros, positive below-floor probabilities,
incorrect 90%-confidence answers, and costs. Keep all metric reversals visible.
Infinite uncapped loss remains infinite in raw arrays; JSON means use null with
an explicit infinite-loss fraction. Capped loss is compatibility-only.

The direct rival uses a 64-unit tanh network, minibatch Adam, learning rate .003,
batch size 256, weight penalty 1e-4, at most 120 epochs, and validation early
stopping after ten epochs without improvement of 1e-4. Save every validation
curve and the best weights. Reaching the cap is unresolved convergence, not a
converged instrument. Register a single bounded repair before any revised fit.
Retain V20's 120-step full-batch network as **fixed-schedule MLP**.

The quadrupled-label ruler checks 512→2,048→8,192, with a separately registered
large-budget follow-up. Mark the compatibility rival INSTRUMENT FAILED when its
validation improvement is below .02 nats while the converged rival improves by
more than .02. This practical sensitivity criterion is fixed before its results;
it does not alter or invalidate a historical V20 receipt retrospectively.

The learned shared-maker reader is an unsupervised 32-state mixture over the
current observation/target joint and historical episode observations, fitted by
40 EM steps. It shares latent state across episodes and receives no true maker
identities. Fewer than two effective states fails the manipulation. Compare it
with converged direct, fixed-schedule MLP, exact-match table, grouped empirical,
naive Bayes, duplicate-removed naive Bayes and the separate fresh-law diagnostic.
This particular finite mixture is an implementation hypothesis, not a causal
interpreter or a claim of universal representational superiority.

B and C screens require the exact-law headroom interval's lower bound to exceed
.02 nats, with both direct fits validation-stopped. Otherwise record PARITY when
the upper bound is at most .02, or UNRESOLVED when uncertainty/convergence remains.
Reuse identical source-bound ceilings across label budgets rather than recompute
and count them as new evidence. Independent C01 diagnostics may expose a scoring
or factorization defect without being an admitted superiority screen.

## Promotion, confirmation and operational review

The primary learned comparison is uncapped joint log loss against the strongest
same-label/evidence direct rival, with .02-nat practical margin. Full Brier,
accuracy, confidence errors and resource costs are separate outcomes, never a
single victory label. Every shift result reports each reader's native-to-shift
change and a duplicate-removed product-of-factors arm.

B07 isolates a current execution change while retaining native past records.
Its modular reader learns a native maker/policy predictor with current artifact
features withheld, and a native execution table. Adaptation re-estimates a
mixture over the registered native/evidence-flip execution kernels. This supplied
two-kernel factorization is an explicit privilege. Compare finite and fresh-law
direct readers fine-tuned on exactly the same 0/16/64/256 shifted training labels.
Use 60 fixed Adam updates at .001 and no shifted validation labels; do not hide
extra adaptation data in early stopping. The fixed target is .1-nat mean excess
over the shift-aware law-marginal ceiling; report whether the modular arm reaches
it using at most one quarter of the direct reader's labels, with uncertainty.
No benefit at any k stops this transfer claim. This does not establish unrestricted
adaptation to an unknown mechanism library.

Freeze at most three distinct mechanism promotions, their strongest rival and
decisive ablation before confirmation opens. The review registers exact criteria,
settings and exclusions in a source-bound promotion manifest; log every attempted
selection and its multiplicity. Use 32 untouched worlds with both fits nested.
If none qualifies, freeze one diagnostic that distinguishes information limits
from reader failure. No later discovery replaces a frozen selection.

Routine operational reviews occur every **six hours**, permanently replacing the
former four-hour cadence for Ghost. Their times do not move after a result ACK.
Failure, disappearance, queue depletion, source amendments and named scientific
boundaries can trigger earlier reviews. A local monitor is not proof of delivery:
verify a real bounded review with an exact event hash acknowledgement, then arm
the production watcher after the current operating turn. Preserve one review owner.

Every named result batch requires independent regrouping, a complete replay for
each new implementation family or semantic repair, readable cases, documentary
write-through and ordinary publication. Keep attempted, admitted, executed,
verified, confirmed and deferred counts distinct. One repair per failed family,
new identity and frozen source; never overwrite partial failures. Conditional
cards are not runnable jobs and the Friday snapshot is not proof of exhaustion.
