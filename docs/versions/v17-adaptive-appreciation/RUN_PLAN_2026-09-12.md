## Recovery and evidence

The queue uses one CPU worker and no GPU. Its native owner locks, thirty-second
heartbeat, immutable manifest, source identities and chunk index support
resumption. A surviving worker retains its packet lock; another supervisor waits
before entering the ordinary resume path. Completed chunks are checked, not
replaced. Source changes require a new packet and retained source snapshot.

The inexpensive watchdog makes at most three supervisor attempts and stops
recovery after repeated lack of progress or the planning target. It does not
retry a terminal scientific failure, generate additional jobs, or fill unused
time. A failed job blocks its dependents; independent families continue.
This is executable job recovery, not proof that an inactive coding conversation
will wake. A drained screen queue requests the next scientific review.

The target remains **17 September 2026 at approximately 11:16 a.m. Pacific**,
five elapsed days from the original implementation start. No clock was reset.
The finite admitted work may finish earlier; remaining analysis and selection
must then proceed rather than occupying the machine without useful work.

Local checks exercise real recipient interventions in both directions,
uninformative forecasts, malformed/private requests, actual typed expansion,
reachable withheld targets, measured effort, appropriate and inappropriate STOP,
matched cognitive cost, each consumer's literal command and resume, duplicate
ownership, corrupted chunks, and independent-job continuation after failure.
The reader export is extracted and executed in a separate directory; its
predictions match the retained reference, and a real private-file read is denied.
That guard protects the fixed trusted Python consumer, not hostile native code.

The first export is a small pilot with outcome-blind selection by native family.
Final illustrative selection still needs to cover observed advantages, reversals
and failures, marking unavailable types absent. Reader and evaluator archives
remain separate. A versioned envelope preserves V16 payloads unchanged.

## Source use and remaining scientific decisions

The [Stitch revision](https://github.com/mlb2251/stitch/tree/350804b7b35807c78bd21c313785ae5152ae2985)
is unchanged. A consistent isolated LLVM Rust build and its runtime DLL passed
startup and real learning probes. Failed GNU startup and missing-tool/DLL attempts
remain in the setup history. The resident scientific environment was not synchronized.

The audience objective is a finite reimplementation motivated by
[Acting as Inverse Inverse Planning](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16913v1);
no unlicensed implementation code was copied. Computational choice uses the
decision-quality-minus-computation-cost distinction from
[Callaway and colleagues](https://cocosci.princeton.edu/papers/callawayrationaluse.pdf).
The access/content contrast is motivated by
[Quillien and Taylor-Davies](https://quillienlab.github.io/Quillien%20%26%20Taylor-Davies%202026.pdf);
our literal operation counts differ from their information-bottleneck constraint.
The commissioned teaching paper remains a design reference; only its publisher
metadata was accessible during this implementation, so no full-paper replication
or empirical claim is attributed to it.

After the broad screen, independently reaggregate and replay the completed
packets, choose informative expansions, and freeze at most three consequential
contrasts before using the reserved fresh namespaces. All current screen
intervals are descriptive. A favorable direction, a tied score, or an interval
crossing zero does not establish confirmation or equivalence. F remains optional.
