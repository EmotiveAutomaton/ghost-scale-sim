# D1: uncertain reports, provenance and retraction

Can a finite process reader recover from a wrong relation report without treating
a repetition as independent evidence? The earlier D1 reader imposed reports as
hard constraints and could exclude the true process. This continuation changes
the report likelihood, using the same frozen native cases and complete executable
hypotheses. It does not generate additional trajectories or train another model.

Use all 512 E1 cases from the verified D1 packet: eight development coefficient
lineages and two original sampling seeds, 32 cases each. Preserve repeated cases
and their sampling weights. Base evidence contains the final artifact and stated
request. The relation concerns whether the first and last selected local goals
are equal. It is additional evaluator-selected teacher information, not an
implication supplied by rearranging the artifact. Retain neutral and self-like
priors exactly as before. A self-like prior multiplies purpose-zero weight by
three; it is a stipulated prior, not a learned self model.

Freeze report reliability at 0.5 and 0.9. A report multiplies each compatible
trajectory's mass by reliability if its relation matches, and by one minus
reliability otherwise. Aggregate complete goal/operation sequences only after
conditioning. No hidden trajectory is used to choose a likelihood, tune a prior,
select a case, or repair probabilities. There is no fitting or hyperparameter
search. The exact finite likelihood family is privileged supplied-law access.

Compare base, literal rearrangement, equal-length reread, irrelevant report,
unknown report, truthful report, wrong report, wrong then retracted, wrong then
replaced by a truthful report from the same source, wrong plus truthful reports
from independent sources, and a duplicate wrong report from the same source.
The latter has two interpretations: provenance-aware deduplication and a naive
independence assumption. Every cell has two report slots; empty slots are explicit
no-ops. The slots and full provenance operations are retained. Source replacement
replaces the old likelihood; source retraction removes it. Independently accepted
opposing reports of equal reliability cancel for this binary relation. These
identities are expected algebraic controls, not scientific discoveries.

Score complete goal/process logarithmic loss, separate local-goal and operation
loss, target support, unsupported joint mass, contradictions and abstention.
Also retain relation posterior probability and its squared error against the
native relation, posterior total variation from base, entropy, and every full
joint posterior. Report paired differences by prior and reliability, averaging
within each original lineage before 10,000 resamples with seed 190501. Sampling
seed variation remains separate; eight paired lineages do not imply universal
training uncertainty. The 0.02-nat margin remains descriptive. Do not treat lack
of a winner as evidence that humans never benefit from reorganization.

Include the original illegal-rendering fixture with artifact [2,0,0] as a
separate outside-alphabet detection control. Its unsupported family must remain
unknown under every cue. It does not test discovery of a plausible omitted cause
inside the visible alphabet. No unknown case enters finite loss averages.

Before dispatch, test analytic two-hypothesis updates, reliability-half identity,
truthful versus wrong likelihood effects, duplicate provenance, independent
conflict cancellation, replacement, retraction, malformed report rejection,
outside-support abstention, and a complete fixture run. Preserve raw prior and
posterior arrays, inputs, source, plan, environment and timings. Original and
both complete replays use the existing single worker. All costs, including review
and failures, share the original D1 four-CPU-hour cap and V19 exploration pool.

Blind consumer packets contain only anonymous base evidence and report slots
with assertion, source, operation and declared reliability. They never contain
true/wrong arm names, truth labels, hidden indices, hypotheses or output scores.
Truth, full hypothesis lists and evaluation mappings have a separate export.

Two independent alternatives remain prepared. F1 will learn native transition
counts from the original training population, then compare direct endpoint
decoding with 1/4/16 learned continuations, equal-compute direct aggregation and
oracle/wrong-law continuations. Freeze observed initial state, trajectory teachers,
smoothing, and total fit/query costs before training; it is not yet implemented.

on the saved transformer's final token state before its final normalization,
then apply that unchanged normalization and head. Retain all original pairs,
training labels, controls and prefixes; no sequence weights or model settings
change. This site is not yet implemented or admitted. The recurrent reader has
no corresponding final normalization; do not pretend this is an architecture-
matched site comparison. A class-mean purpose direction at the original site is
a separate limited candidate, not grounds to admit joint purpose/belief C2.

The local primary and research week remain open. Reserved test and confirmation
lineages stay untouched; no seed padding or additional tiny-model setting.
