# Initial local-world admission protocol

Frozen before the eight-lineage admission results. Sixteen persistent maker
combinations cross two governing priorities (functional revision / presentation),
two skill levels, two belief accuracies and two prior routines. A visible request
is an exogenous cue and need not be adopted. Four contexts cross two initial
artifacts with two requests. Coefficients use the first eight reserved development
lineages, 190000 through 190007, without selection from outcomes.

The three binary units are claim, dependent evidence and presentation. Evidence
should track claim. Local goals are claim revision, dependency repair and
presentation change. Each step samples a local goal before sampling its operation.
Inspect is compatible with multiple goals. The acquired tool changes the claim
and repairs its dependent evidence; novice repair can have a different consequence.
Belief error changes perceived claim state. Undo restores the immediately previous
artifact. These are declared generative policies, not a claim of optimal control.

Exactly three steps yield 6 choices per step: one goal-specific operation or its
inspection/undo alternative for each of three goals. Four contexts times sixteen
maker states times 6 cubed gives 13,824 weighted trajectories per coefficient
lineage. The reference includes artifact, undo buffer, step and selected local goal
as transient state. Persistent state alone is not Markov and the old 24-state
algebra is not applied to this process.

Admission checks normalize all trajectories; reconstruct transitions; test actual
dependency repair, undo and skill restrictions; and retain endpoint/process and
operation/goal aliases. Every evidence tier uses a predetermined all-inspect case.
An out-of-family rendering exercises unknown. This first casebook is deliberately
an ambiguity fixture, not an outcome-selected expertise or success illustration.

E0 contains final units. E1 adds initial units and the visible request. Sparse E2
adds the first witnessed operation and its before/after state; full E2 witnesses
all operations. Corrected E1 transparently injects a wrong report of the request
and its correction. Evaluator truth and oracle-family reader outputs are separate
from the public input export. Public fields are allowlisted and injected hidden
fields must be rejected. Both exact and uniform-template readers consume the same
projection. Joint compatible hypotheses retain links among governing purpose,
local goals and operations; their marginal summaries cannot replace those links.

No model has been trained by this protocol. The exact reader knows the generator
family and the template reader knows its executable support. Neither is labelled
a learned-law result. Subsequent prediction labels, intervention targets and
capacity matching require their own pre-fit admission record.
