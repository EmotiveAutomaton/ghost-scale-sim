cue after the first label? Freeze the conditional-acquisition library, 15 mixtures,
three reliabilities, actual copy probabilities zero/one-half/one and the four
fees zero/1/64/1/16/1/4. The nominal decision rule assumes zero copying. Evaluate
its buy/decline choices and selected masks under each actual law, without refitting
or changing its policy after outcomes. Compare with actual-law conditional choice,
always-decline and nominal always-buy. Retain signed net utility differences,
unnecessary acquisition probabilities, missed worthwhile acquisitions, fee costs,
all ties, impossible events and byte charges.

This separates mistaken acquisition from mistaken interpretation of an acquired
cue. The earlier repeated-cue comparison always supplied the second cue; it did
not select purchases after the first cue or account for fees. The new comparison
requires the conditional-acquisition parent to pass independent review first.
