# Independent review of supplied-time transient filtering

Does a complete account of a possible purpose change preserve current-state
information? Numerical adjudication of the frozen transient-filter comparison
requires independent reconstruction. Original, adjacent and extracted-source
outputs are checked for identity separately; identity cannot exclude a shared bug.

The checker consumes all completed evidence from the eight original admission
lineages. It reconstructs all native three-step trajectories and their probabilities
with the separate crossed-law ruler. Stable summation reconstructs each maker's
endpoint law. Original draw identities reproduce the complete observation roster,
context rotation, inverse-CDF sampling, duplicate copies and source-time assignments.
Every input is bound before execution. No new fit, seed or outcome roster is added.

Enumerated no-switch/switch hypotheses accumulate scaled likelihood products,
independently of the producer's log-likelihood implementation. Each repeated source
counts once; conflicting identity is an error. The checker independently remaps
initial hypotheses to current makers after the supplied time, reconstructs the
last-sixteen-distinct-source filter, and retains the full joint distribution.
Every forecast, endpoint logarithmic loss, current-state mass and logarithmic loss,
90% credible-set inclusion and size is compared. All boundary ties within 1e-14
remain included. The original 1e-300 reporting floor is unchanged. Empty support
is an instrument failure. Numerical agreement tolerance is 1e-10, separate from
the frozen 0.02-nat practical endpoint margin.

Every stream, row and summary denominator must match. Sixteen makers receive equal
weight in each observation draw. The two draws are averaged within each coefficient
lineage, then eight lineages are paired across methods. Each length, actual-switch,
source-dependence and checkpoint cell remains separate. All three pairwise method
contrasts retain five metrics, individual lineage differences and 10,000 paired
bootstrap resamples at seed 190501. Intervals condition on this design and its two
observation draws; they do not estimate universal training or human uncertainty.

Known-answer and placebo controls check direct two-path enumeration, current-state
remapping, an informative observation, a uniform law, boundary ties, duplicate
identity, conflict rejection, empty support and last-sixteen-source identity.
Isolated tests include a complete native fixture, population averaging, incorrect
distribution/source rejection and evaluator-label rejection in reader packets.
Runtime and portable replay dispatch must reach the checker. Its source, environment
and completed inputs freeze after controls and before campaign execution through
the existing serial worker. Review is charged to the original four-hour B card.

Reader packets are reconstructed exactly from context definitions, observed
endpoints and source identities. Maker state, switch assignment, coefficient/draw
pairing, true law, distributions and scores remain evaluator material. All methods
receive the true law and the possible change time; neither privilege is learned.
Duplicated streams have 24/96 distinct observations instead of 32/128, so their
contrast includes reduced evidence. Forecasting a fresh episode under the current
maker does not identify any preceding episode's local-goal path.

The two independent prepared alternatives in [the frozen filtering protocol](TRANSIENT_FILTER_PROTOCOL.md)
remain changed-tool support transfer with the saved count fits and common smoothing,
and unknown-time purpose/skill-change discrimination. Each has a four-hour cap and
requires its own implementation, controls and source admission. Neither requires a
positive filtering result. The week, untouched confirmation reserve and two-setting
training limit remain unchanged.
