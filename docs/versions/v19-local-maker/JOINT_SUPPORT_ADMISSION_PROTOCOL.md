# Public-mechanics support decoding: frozen implementation contract

This implements the prepared joint-support diagnostic without new fits. It
retains every saved probability array from the primary joint-readout comparison:
four evidence tiers, four nested budgets, two training draws, five feature seeds,
sixteen development lineages and all four original reader arms. No protected
test or confirmation lineage enters. The original learned models stay frozen.

Support depends only on anonymous public packets and legal primitive mechanics.
For an unobserved initial artifact, enumerate all eight binary triples; do not
supply the native population's two-start restriction. Enumerate both skill bits
and belief-error bits, all legal three-operation sequences, and all 27 goal
sequences independently of the operations. Undo is mechanically permitted at
every position. The hidden controller's goal-to-action restrictions, purpose,
routine, policy mass and evaluator truth never determine a mask. Requests cannot
remove goals. The separate symbolic executor checks every primitive transition.
Full witnesses therefore fix operations while retaining all goal alternatives.

Expand the original reserved unknown probability uniformly across all unseen
labels in the fixed 5,832-label universe before restriction. Renormalize only
positive legal mass. Empty support or zero predicted legal mass is an explicit
failure; retain the original input as a labelled fallback and do not accept that
row as a successful restricted forecast. No smoothing or tuning follows outcomes.

Compare original and restricted raw-history, latent-state, predictive-bank and
same-budget frequency forecasts, plus uniform probability over public legal
support. Report original metrics and each restricted-versus-original contrast;
also compare the restricted bank against all three restricted baselines and
uniform support. All original numbers must reproduce first. Native compatibility
uses the original evaluator posterior support, which is narrower than mechanical
legality. Mechanically valid output does not establish historical correspondence.

The common candidate policy ranks probability over the complete universe. Stable
ties put observed alphabet labels first, then unseen labels, in integer order.
Original alphabets already hold over 90% mass; original candidate decisions must
reproduce. A restricted reader may need unseen labels to reach 90%, so these are
explicitly included. Mode, abstention below 0.5, localization, squared and log
scores use the full distribution; original zero-probability truth is a failure.
Retain all per-lineage metrics and normalization factors; exact probabilities are
losslessly reconstructible from original arrays, masks and normalizers. No dense
forecast duplicates are required. Reader packets and evaluator data stay separate.

Average draws and feature seeds within each lineage before 10,000 paired lineage
resamples, seed 191003. Freeze all eight loss contrasts at every evidence tier and
budget and their normalized log-budget areas. Retain draw and feature-seed
variation separately. No global pooled tier replaces evidence-specific estimates.

tests, original execution, both complete replays and independent reconstruction,
within the existing A allowance. A synthetic worst-shape scoring benchmark and
complete mechanical enumeration determine tractability before scientific dispatch.
Source, input hashes, environment and this contract freeze before outcomes.

Controls cover full witnesses retaining all goals, artifact aliases, hidden-start
union, undo/tool mechanics, request invariance, private-field rejection, empty

supplied-mechanics decoding diagnostic, not a new architecture or human inference.
