# Learned temporal dependence: prepared independent alternative

Does dependence across steps in a frozen learned process forecast improve
prediction beyond three within-step goal/operation marginals? This prepared,
unimplemented A-family alternative is distinct from goal-sequence versus
operation-sequence factorization. Neither comparison is an admission prerequisite
for the other. It requires no new training setting or protected lineage.

Use the complete original joint-readout roster and full unknown tail. Decode
each of 5,832 labels into three paired goal/operation symbols, each with eighteen
possibilities. Sum three eighteen-way marginals and form their product over time.
Preserve within-step goal/operation dependence while removing between-step
dependence. Do not enforce legal paths with evaluator truth or public support.
Compare all four original readouts with their own products and separate native
posterior products. Keep original tie rules, all correspondence/coverage metrics,
full distributions, teacher access and reader/evaluator separation.

Freeze 10,000 paired sixteen-lineage resamples, seed 191006; average both draws
and all five feature seeds inside each lineage. Report product-minus-original
loss per budget and normalized log-budget area, all metric means, and separate
draw/seed variation. Controls include a temporally independent identity, an
exactly correlated two-step fixture, identical temporal marginals with different
paths, axis reversal, impossible cross paths, all unknown labels and zero-mass
targets. Scalar label enumeration independently reconstructs products and scores.

Require a tractability screen, complete source/input freeze, adjacent and
extracted-source replay, and independent numerical reconstruction before any
accepted finding. Inclusive cap: 1,800 CPU seconds inside the existing A allowance.
No new samples, fits, budget expansion or conditional transfer admission. This
is a constructed learned-distribution diagnostic, not historical or human intent.
