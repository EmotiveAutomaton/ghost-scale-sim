# Report-context disclosure: prospective implementation contract

Keep all 28,672 retained posterior rows, 505,856 source candidates, eight laws,
two evidence conditions, two draws and seven available checkpoint cells. Keep
the 48 unavailable strata absent. No new observations, fits or protected lineages.

Uniform source selection and copy probabilities 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 are fixed.
Compare eight endpoint-only reports with 32 context/endpoint reports, ordered by
context then endpoint. Context is the selected source's public query context;
sources within that context remain marginalized. All source identities, complete
hypothesis weights and law matrices are supplied evaluator information.

Retain exact report probabilities, source posteriors, support and undefined masks,
conditional source entropy, expected future multiclass squared loss and squared
forecast gain, and every refined report's squared discrepancy from its endpoint-only
forecast. Preserve all future times, four contexts and eight endpoints. Expected
refinement gain equals expected squared forecast discrepancy and cannot be negative;
expected source entropy cannot increase. These identities validate the instrument.

Weight report values by exact report law, then 128 Cartesian identities equally,
two draws within each law and eight laws equally; keep evidence/checkpoint cells
separate. No practical margin or confirmatory interval is specified. A supplied
context is extra evidence, not learned source attribution or historical correspondence.

Admission needs scalar Bayes and explicit one-hot proper-loss checks, sparse and
disjoint laws, source/context permutations, constant-context and single-source
identities, informative-context positive, all inherited input-binding controls,
and complete-support synthetic timing with raw serialization. The inclusive cap
is 3,600 CPU seconds for tests, failures, all three complete executions,
independent reconstruction, regroup and operations. B-family extension beyond
its initial 12 CPU hours requires recorded timing and stays below 24 hours;
the total 96 hours and 16 protected hours remain unchanged.

Two-report dependence and bounded retrospective forgetting are prepared independent
alternatives. Source-identity disclosure and the reachable-posterior rival retain
their recorded timing blockers; neither is silently retried.
