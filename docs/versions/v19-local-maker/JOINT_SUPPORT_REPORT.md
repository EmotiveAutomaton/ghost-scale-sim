# Public execution support: independently verified

We tested whether public execution constraints improve frozen joint-process readouts. With complete witnesses and 2,048 labels, restriction reduces the predictive bank’s logarithmic loss from 5.60252 to 0.91337 nats; matched frequencies reach 0.90746. Across training budgets, the bank’s advantage over frozen latent state stays below the declared 0.02-nat margin in every evidence tier. Independent reconstruction, all 160 paired estimates and both complete replays verify this constructed-method result; supplied operations do not establish recovered historical goals.

The frozen comparison uses four evidence tiers, four nested budgets (32, 128,
512 and 2,048 labels), two training draws, five feature seeds and sixteen
development coefficient lineages. Native frame mass is preserved within each
lineage; lineages receive equal weight. All arms share public mechanics and
the same outcome labels. No new model, episode or protected lineage is used.

Restriction discards mechanically impossible operation paths and renormalizes
the complete 5,832-label forecast, including its full unknown tail. All eight
hidden starts and both mechanics bits are enumerated, with all 27 goal sequences
retained for each legal path. Purpose, native controller rules and evaluator
posterior masses never decide a mask. Complete witnesses supply all operations;
they do not mechanically identify a local goal.

The first table reports normalized area under logarithmic loss versus log label

sixteen-lineage bootstrap intervals conditional on these fits, after averaging
two draws and five seeds within each lineage. They do not quantify all training
uncertainty. Raw-history, latent-state and frequency comparisons below restrict
both readers identically. Draw and seed summaries remain in the complete receipt.

Support improves all four readers. The bank beats raw history by more than the
0.02-nat practical margin with context and either witnessed condition, but its
advantage over latent state is smaller than that margin throughout. The whole
conditional interval lies inside the practical equivalence band for this latter
comparison in each tier. Frequency comparisons reverse across tiers and budgets:
at the largest budget, frequencies beat the bank in every tier. This is an
exploratory fixed-fit estimate, not confirmation or a universal learning result.

Mechanical legality is insufficient for native correspondence: under artifact-only
evidence the restricted bank assigns only 0.36047 probability to native-supported
processes, and its modal process is outside native support on 78.71% of native
mass. Complete witnesses force operation accuracy to one for every restricted
reader, including uniform support. That success is supplied by evidence. Goals
remain probabilistic: bank goal accuracy is 0.86630 versus frequency 0.86695.

Uniform support has complete-witness loss log(27)=3.29584 nats. Its modal goal
accuracy is 0.85767, but that number is not evidence of learned goal weights:
the frozen tie rule prioritizes labels in the training alphabet, then unseen
labels. Thus uniform probabilities are mechanical, while its single-mode and
candidate-set decisions inherit training-alphabet tie priority. This qualification
also applies to exact ties in every arm. Native target compatibility, legal support,
candidate coverage and predictive score are separate quantities.

All 1,457 deterministic outputs match the original and both complete replays.
All 603 producer sources, 606 checker sources, their complete input/output bindings,
plans and environments verify. The independent scalar executor enumerates 5,456
legal paths and 16,368 transitions; all 912 reader packets, 10,240 original score
strata and 23,040 support strata reconstruct. Maximum score discrepancy is
9.10e-13; saved normalizers agree within 2.23e-16. A separate original-row regroup
reproduces all 128 budget estimates and 32 learning areas within 2.67e-15.
Twenty-one isolated checker controls passed before execution, including corruptions.

The checker inherits previously verified fitted forecasts and native posterior
masses; it does not refit or regenerate them. Saved binary64 normalizers determine
the actual discrete rankings after independent numerical comparison. No population
outside the frozen development laws, other architecture or human intent is tested.
The purpose-built miniature remains architecture untested beyond its admitted
coefficient rosters. No confirmation or conditional A3/C2/F2 admission follows.

Reader inputs remain the 912 anonymous public packets. Truth, weights, masks,
forecasts, full score arrays and both regroupings remain scientific/evaluator
exports. Pursuit continues with operation-versus-goal uncertainty decomposition;
retrospective updating remains an independent alternative. The 3,600-second card
ceiling includes controls, failed attempts, both replays and review.
