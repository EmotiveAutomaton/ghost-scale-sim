# R3 held-out paid expansion after whole-stream calibration

Does entropy centering improve missing-law targeting once both sensors have been
calibrated over the complete purchase opportunity? The calibration report and
immutable threshold artifact must pass independent reconstruction before this
evaluation is admitted. This protocol fixes the evaluation before its outcomes.

Use exactly the 20 previously reserved coefficient indices 197000–197019, cells
0/2/8/10, all four inherited truth conditions, three evidence orders and two class
priors: 1,920 paired streams. Each contains 32 unique executable-program observations.
Truth is the supplied law, its initially available alternative, the purchasable
third law, or a law outside the catalog. Truth labels and pairing indices enter
only the evaluator. No local-world test or confirmation lineage is used.

The nine methods are the fixed supplied law, selection from the initial two laws,
their mixture without purchase, three-law selection and mixture from the start,
paid mixture at raw thresholds 1.5 and 3, and paid mixture at the separately
calibrated raw and centered thresholds. No architecture is trained or selected.
Both calibrated methods use their exact cell/order/prior threshold from the
reviewed calibration artifact. All paid methods use the same purchase mechanism.
At the first strict crossing, before an eligible observation, the third catalog
law is purchased and fitted to the entire observed prefix. It then participates
in the mixture. No crossing means no purchase. No decision follows observation 32.

The sensor uses only the initial two-law mixture and the preceding four program
surprises, optionally minus their pre-observation program-distribution entropies.
Eligible decisions precede observations 9 through 32. The implementation computes
the sensor in a separate complete pass and supplies only its first past-only
crossing to the predictor. Future-outcome changes must leave earlier forecasts
and purchase status unchanged. Conflicting copied sources are rejected; exact
copies have no effect. This is a privileged supplied-law/program task, not an
artifact-only reader or a learned local-process reconstruction.

Calibration contains exact and nearly equal maxima. Keep the original strict
floating-point comparison and all threshold bytes unchanged. For each paid arm,
record how many opportunities lie within 1e-12 of its threshold. Independent
scalar reconstruction verifies sensor values to numerical tolerance; exact
decision reconstruction uses the verified frozen arithmetic. Full source replays
must reproduce its decisions. This checks implementation identity, not invariance
of threshold crossings to a different numerical reduction. Report this sensitivity
alongside attained rates; do not retune thresholds or infer identical calibration
rates when strict ties produce different rates.

Record sequential and final logarithmic forecast loss, modal native-action match,
ever-purchase frequency, purchase time (33 means never; 0 means expanded from the
start), model entropy and work. A late purchase still assimilates the entire past,
so final predictor likelihood work is the final candidate count × 24 states × 32
observations. Each paid arm additionally incurs a complete 1,536-evaluation sensor
pass. This explicitly charges the redundant implementation work. The four paid
arms use the same accounting convention. Count the sensor's complete-alphabet
contraction terms separately: 1,536 × 65 executable programs. It would be possible
to reuse likelihood calculations in another implementation; that saving is not
assumed here. Reader CPU by method and complete native CPU including verification
are retained separately. Cache order makes per-method timing descriptive.

Retain the inherited stipulated price sensitivities: subtract one purchase unit
plus 0.00001 per likelihood evaluation, or four units plus 0.0001 per evaluation,
then divide these total costs by 32 before subtracting them from mean action
match. These are assumptions, not measured economic prices or a value-of-compute
claim. Behaviorally inert purchases must preserve predictions and subtract
exactly their acquisition and work costs.

Before dispatch, controls reconstruct scalar sensors, every mixture forecast,
native action and both cost scores; reject deliberate corruption; verify strict
ties, no-purchase behavior, future noninterference, copied-source identity and
known informative/null likelihoods. The evaluator reconstructs these checks for
every complete stream. No favorable targeting rate, utility rank or held-out
false-purchase rate is a validity condition.

Report all cell/truth/order/prior/method strata and equal-cell summaries. Average
paired contrasts within each coefficient index before a 10,000-resample paired
lineage bootstrap with seed 190501. Report truth conditions separately and both
class priors; repeated questions, orders and priors are not independent worlds.
The 0.02-nat loss margin remains descriptive. Twenty indices condition on this
fixed family and calibration sample; they do not quantify all calibration or
architecture uncertainty. Preserve all raw streams and forecasts, separate reader
and evaluator exports, full source replay and extracted-source replay.

The entire R3 branch shares its original three-CPU-hour cap across calibration,
evaluation, validation, failures, independent reviews and replays. Both immutable
threshold and independent-review hashes enter the evaluation plan. The existing
single-worker queue owns all three runs. No new tiny setting, environment change
or new scientific owner is needed. This evaluation does not close the week.
