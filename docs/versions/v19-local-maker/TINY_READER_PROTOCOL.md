# Tiny sequence readers: frozen capability pilot

Question: can a small trained sequence reader retain useful information from
observable local-world episodes without receiving maker labels or a test-world
law? Compare one causal transformer with one recurrent reader before attempting
causal access. This is G19-06's capability pilot, not completion of the local
goal/process primary and not admission to interchange by prediction alone.

Reuse B1's unchanged observed endpoint streams: the first 32 training coefficient
lineages, eight development lineages, two training draws and two initial seeds
from its frozen plan. Use all 32 positions of independent and copied streams.
Each token encodes the visible context and three-bit final artifact. The declared
source-copy flag removes duplicate observations before encoding; both architectures
receive the same ordered unique tokens. No program, local-goal, persistent-maker,
law, previous exact-bank, or lineage identifier is a model input. Copy identity
is supplied information, not a learned source-dependence detector.

Training targets are B1's exact conditional distributions of a fresh observable
endpoint in each of four public contexts. They are simulator distribution teachers,
not sampled human outcomes. Both models receive identical targets at every unique
prefix; copied positions supply no additional target. Development inputs contain
tokens and copy flags only; evaluator targets stay outside the child input capsule.
The task concerns fresh episode prediction, not the preceding local process.

Freeze two small architecture settings, conservatively consuming the entire shared
two-setting allowance once fitting begins: (1) token embedding width 32, fixed
sinusoidal positions, one causal transformer block with four heads, feed-forward
width 64, no dropout, post-normalization, GELU, and a linear 32-output head;
(2) token embedding width 32, one GRU of width 38, and a linear 32-output head.
Both have a beginning-of-history token. Record actual parameter counts before
scientific training; nominal capacities differ by less than 5%, while architecture
and state geometry differ. No architecture search or third setting is authorized.

Each architecture uses both declared seeds on both training draws. Freeze AdamW
at learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.0001, 32 epochs, batches of 32 streams,
gradient clipping at norm 1, shuffled stream order from the fit seed, and final
checkpoint selection. Each minibatch averages categorical cross entropy over its
unique prefixes and four context queries. No early stopping, learning-rate tuning,
development-label selection or model ranking changes the schedule. Checkpoints
retain weights, optimizer, shuffle permutation, cursor and random state. Save final
parameters, all development prefix forecasts, hidden states and complete learning
curves. Five-seed primary comparisons and confirmation remain separate work.

Report logarithmic loss, squared probability error and prediction variation at
8 and 32 original positions separately for independent and copied streams. Retain
per-lineage/per-fit scores. Compare the fixed training-target mean and exact-law
reference. Capability requires, in each evidence condition and scored length,
at least 0.02 nats improvement over the mean and recovery of at least half the
mean-to-exact headroom, with nonconstant forecasts. Report each fit's decision;
an aggregate cannot admit a failed fit. These are pilot engineering thresholds,
not confirmatory significance or evidence of a selective internal role.

Before science require causal-prefix invariance, exact duplicate passthrough,
no-op state/decode identity, categorical normalization, a known observable-token
learning fixture and a no-information target fixture. Test checkpoint resume
against uninterrupted updates. Toy controls use these same two architectures;
they are not additional searched settings. Causal interchange additionally needs
frozen donor/recipient factor changes, change/stay truth and native fixture replay;
this pilot alone cannot supply that admission.

Ghost is the sole fit/accounting owner. The CPU child uses an existing installed
Torch interpreter, no synchronization, one thread and below-normal priority, while
the existing scientific parent waits. Include child CPU and failed checks in the
four-hour G19-06 pilot cap and the existing exploration ceiling. Original and full
replays share that cap and do not consume additional architecture settings. Bind
the installed Torch fingerprint, public inputs, settings receipt and source before
dispatch. Keep optimizer/checkpoint timings separate from deterministic evidence.

The fixed-size portfolio design A2 and whole-stream purchase-calibration design R3
remain prepared independent alternatives as specified in [practice](PRACTICE_PROTOCOL.md).
Failure of either tiny reader cannot close the remaining scientific forest.
