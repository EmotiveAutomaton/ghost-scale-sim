# Paid expansion: centering reduces purchases without improving targeting

Does subtracting expected surprise improve purchases of a missing model? No targeting advantage was found. When the missing rule is purchasable, centered surprise buys it less often and loses 0.03400 nats of forecast accuracy to raw surprise. Both paid rules lose to the no-purchase mixture on the stipulated net-cost scores. Independent reconstruction and both full replays verify this exploratory constructed-method result; it does not establish human intent.

The frozen comparison contains 1,920 paired streams: 20 coefficient indices,
four old-world cells, four truth conditions, three evidence orders and two priors.
Each stream supplies 32 distinct executable-program observations. Nine methods
include fixed, selected and mixed supplied laws, expansion from the start, two
historical thresholds and the two calibrated purchase rules. The calibration
artifact and all strict comparison rules remain unchanged.

The table averages cells and evidence orders equally within each coefficient
index, then averages the 20 indices. Rows describe which rule generated the data.
Purchase rates are percentages of streams. Loss difference is centered minus raw
logarithmic forecast loss, in nats; positive means centering predicts worse.
Intervals are paired 95% coefficient-index bootstrap intervals, conditional on the
fixed calibration. This table uses equal class priors; the doubled supplied-class
prior is reported immediately below and completely in the numerical export.

Doubling the prior mass of the supplied class leaves purchase rates unchanged.
For the purchasable missing rule, centered-minus-raw loss is +0.03403 nats
[+0.02071, +0.04834]. In both priors the conditional interval exceeds the declared
0.02-nat descriptive margin. This is a conditional reversal, not a confirmatory
population claim: calibration-sample and architecture uncertainty are unmeasured.
The other three truth conditions have small contrasts, but no universal equivalence
or power claim is made from these conditional intervals.

Purchasing still improves some forecasts relative to never expanding. For the
purchasable rule under equal priors, raw surprise reduces loss by 0.04311 nats
[0.03006, 0.05636]; centering reduces it by 0.00911 [0.00204, 0.01830]. Expected
native-action match increases only 0.153 and 0.115 percentage points respectively.
Neither improvement pays for acquisition plus the complete separate sensor pass
under either stipulated price schedule. Across every truth/prior mean, both
calibrated paid arms have lower net action scores than the no-purchase mixture.
Centering costs less than raw purchase, without thereby becoming the best method.
Prices are experimental assumptions, not economic measurements. Method CPU times
are retained separately and are sensitive to cache order.

Strict threshold decisions remain numerically sensitive. In supplied-rule streams,
lowering the centered threshold by 1e-12 raises purchase frequency from 9.17% to
15.42%; the unchanged and raised-threshold frequencies are 9.17%. There are 64
near-threshold opportunities under equal priors and 66 under the doubled prior.
This diagnostic does not alter any result. The missing-rule and outside-catalog
rates have no such near-threshold opportunities in this roster. A lower purchase
frequency alone is not successful detection of a missing rule.

Nine known-answer controls pass before the independent checker reads outcomes:
posterior recovery, uniform-likelihood invariance, mixture and selection forecasts,
logarithmic scoring, strict ties, past-only first crossing, inert-purchase cost and
deliberate corruption rejection. Complete-prefix likelihood products independently
reconstruct every forecast, sensor, posterior, action and cost across 17,280 method
rows and all 864 original strata. The largest probability difference is 1.55e-14,
inside the frozen 1e-10 tolerance. Exact purchase/action decisions use verified
stored arithmetic; numerical closeness is not claimed to determine every tie.
All 24 paired contrasts and their 10,000 shared bootstrap draws were independently
regrouped again from the saved review rows, agreeing within 1.78e-15.

Original, adjacent and extracted-source runs match all 1,945 deterministic files.
All four source/plan/input/output sets, including the independent checker, verify.
Each timing file matches its own receipt. Prior failed reviewer-only assertions
remain retained and charged; neither scientific thresholds nor frozen code changed.
The checker imports the native world as the stipulated likelihood ruler, so this
verification does not independently validate the model's generative assumptions.

**Warrant:** exploratory constructed-method reversal for centering versus raw
purchase on the purchasable missing rule; descriptive cost disadvantage for both
paid methods at stipulated prices. Miniature — architecture untested. No historical
process correspondence, invention of a law, human intent or universal detector
advantage follows. Test and confirmation lineages of the local-world program remain
untouched.
**Pursuit:** proceed to the independent equal-size portfolio comparison. Causal
change/stay fixtures and matched-computation local rollouts remain useful separate
alternatives. This finite result does not close the research week.
