# Fixed-budget practice support mixtures: prepared alternative

Can reallocating a fixed amount of learned transition evidence restore production
on an unvisited starting context without losing useful visited-context behavior?
The E-family handler is implemented; isolated controls and source admission are
required before execution. Admission and completion remain distinct.

This replaces the context re-tabulation proposed in the prepared-alternatives
paragraph of UNKNOWN_CHANGE_PROTOCOL. That paragraph incorrectly says four
practice contexts: the retained practice learner has two functional-task contexts,
and its completed report already gives their separate successes. Repeating that
tabulation would not be new science. The frozen unknown-change experiment and
its source snapshot are unchanged; this correction concerns only a future design.

Use all eight completed practice lineages, both training draws and both acquisition
seeds, separately for active acquisition and demonstration tables. Use saved tables
at 32/128/512 episodes. For each matched pair, form five fixed mixtures of restricted
and matched-start counts with matched-start weights 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and 1. The
one-half pseudocount prior is included exactly once. These are fractional sufficient-
statistic reweightings, not five new trajectories or an on-policy learning history.
The total transition-count mass remains three times the episode budget. Treat both
endpoint mixtures as exact identities with completed work, never new replicates.

Apply the same frozen finite-horizon planner to each mixed table. Retain both
context-specific exact production success rates, their equal-context mean and
the restricted-start evaluation separately. Freeze a two-percentage-point success
margin; make all adjacent-weight contrasts exploratory and report the whole curve,
without choosing a winning weight after outcomes. Report count visitation and
policy changes to distinguish restored context support from altered probabilities
on already observed transitions. This does not identify a general embodiment
mechanism or improve inverse-intent recovery by definition.

Before execution, independently reconstruct every parent table from ordered saved
episodes; reproduce the published per-context endpoint results first. Controls
must verify both endpoint identities, exact replay, total count conservation,
one-prior accounting, no cross-context writes, identical-input identity and a
known controllable kernel. Independently evaluate the planner by exhaustive path
propagation. Retain all table/forecast/policy arrays and frozen source, plans,
inputs and environment. Reader exports, if any, use observed transitions alone;
table pairings, native kernels and scores stay evaluator-only.

Allocate a separate maximum of four CPU hours including failed attempts, controls,
all executions and independent review, within E-family/global exploration caps.
No new neural architecture, tiny setting, episode seed or reserved lineage is used.
This is independent of the separately prepared changed-tool learned support
transfer in ROUTINE_REVISION_PROTOCOL. Neither requires unknown-change filtering
to win, and both still need handlers and source-bound admission.

The changed-tool comparison is now independently adjudicated; this mixture handler
continues on its own question. It reconstructs all 576 original count tables and
per-context scores from saved ordered logs before new interpretation. The same
vector-sum planner and first-maximum tie rule remain frozen. Independent evaluation
sums all 8 cubed possible endpoint paths per context. Near-tie policy sensitivity
in the parent is retained; no alternate tie rule is selected after outcomes.

Report every adjacent mixture-weight difference and the two endpoint identities,
separately by 32/128/512 episodes, active/demonstration acquisition, both contexts,
equal-context mean and restricted-context evaluation. Average the two draws and
two acquisition seeds within each of eight paired lineages; retain their four
separate means. Use 10,000 lineage resamples with seed 190501. The two-percentage-
point margin applies to production success, with uncertainty conditional on saved
acquisition. No monotonicity, optimal mixture or inverse-inference result is assumed.

Off-midpoint purpose/skill changes and bounded primitive feedback remain two
independent prepared alternatives, specified in SUPPORT_TRANSFER_PROTOCOL.md and
PRIMITIVE_FEEDBACK_PROTOCOL.md. Neither requires a mixture advantage.
