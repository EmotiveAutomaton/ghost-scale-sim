# Public-evidence support decoding: prepared primary-readout alternative

Can publicly available execution constraints improve the already fitted joint
process readouts, and how much of the improvement is available to a cheap
uniform-support reader? This is a prepared, unimplemented G19-A diagnostic of
the main joint-process comparison. It requires no new sequence-model setting,
new fit, new episode or protected lineage. Initial cap: 3,600 CPU seconds,
including tests, original, both complete replays and independent review,
within the existing A allocation. Measure tractability before admission.

Freeze the saved G19-05-joint-readout-1 models, forecasts, four evidence tiers,
all four label budgets, two training draws, five feature seeds and sixteen
development lineages. Preserve its 5,832 syntactic joint labels and existing
unknown-tail probability. Recover the original numbers before extension.

Construct legal-process support solely from each anonymous reader packet and
the declared public original-tool mechanics. Enumerate all legal initial
conditions and maker mechanics bits allowed by that packet, retaining all
local-goal sequences. Goals cannot be removed using the hidden controller,
true policy, evaluator posterior mass, true path or test labels. Witnessed
operations and artifacts may exclude incompatible operation paths; an
unobserved initial condition remains a union over possibilities. Verify this
support against a second direct symbolic executor. Keep mechanics knowledge
explicit and identical for every compared reader.

Compare each frozen distribution, its renormalized legal-support restriction,
the same-budget label-frequency distribution with that restriction, and a
uniform distribution over legal joint labels. Do not smooth or retune after
outcomes. Empty support or zero retained forecast mass is an explicit failure,
with the unmodified forecast retained as a labelled fallback, never silently
substituted into the restricted arm. Keep exact zeros and numerical ties.

Score joint logarithmic loss, compatible mass, actual posterior coverage of
the 90% candidate set, incompatible mode, localized goals/operations and
abstention using the original definitions. Compare paired lineage learning
areas and every budget/evidence tier; average draws/seeds within lineage
before 10,000 resamples with frozen seed 191003. Report initialization and
draw variation separately. Charge support enumeration and query/storage
costs. Separate mechanically forced operation recovery from inferred goals.

Controls: complete witnesses, final-artifact aliases, undo, hidden-field
invariance, no-evidence identity where the support is full, uniform-support
normalization, empty support, zero predicted support, and deliberate removal
of a true compatible path. Require full replay, independent numerical
reconstruction and separate reader/evaluator exports before acceptance.
This is a supplied-mechanics decoding comparison, not a new architecture,
learned policy reconstruction, confirmation or human-intent result.
