# Within-step dependence after temporal factorization: prepared alternative

After between-step dependence is removed, does preserving each step's local
goal/operation dependence still improve prediction? This prepared, unimplemented
A-family diagnostic is distinct from goal-sequence/operation-sequence factorization
and temporal factorization. Its algebraic inputs are the frozen original forecasts;
no favorable outcome from either comparison is an admission prerequisite.

Use the complete original joint-readout roster and unknown tail. Compare the
product of three eighteen-way step marginals with the product of six individual
goal and operation marginals from the same forecast. Keep the original joint arm
and separate native references. The contrast isolates the additional removal of
within-step goal/operation dependence after between-step dependence is removed.
Preserve all distributions, native weighting, correspondence/coverage metrics,
unknown mass and original tie priority; no legal-support restriction is applied.

Freeze 10,000 paired sixteen-lineage resamples with seed 191007. Average the two
draws and five feature seeds inside each lineage; report each budget, normalized
log-budget area and separate draw/seed variation. Controls include within-step
dependence with independent steps, fully independent identity, equal single
marginals with different pairs, axis reversal, incompatible pairs, unknown mass
and zeros. Independent scalar enumeration checks all six marginals and products,
every metric, original score reproduction and complete paired regroup.

Admission requires a tractability screen, frozen implementation and inputs,
known-answer controls, adjacent and complete extracted-source replay, and an
independent checker. Inclusive cap: 1,800 CPU seconds within A. No fitting,
sampling, protected lineage, setting allowance or budget expansion is requested.
The result would describe constructed forecast structure, not historical process
correspondence, learned causal access or human intention.
