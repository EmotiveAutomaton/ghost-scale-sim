# Fixed report-content coarsening: prospective implementation contract

Keep all 28,672 retained posterior rows and 505,856 source candidates, both
evidence conditions, eight development laws, two observation draws and seven
available checkpoint cells. Keep the 48 unavailable strata absent. No new fits,
observations, confirmation or test lineages are admitted.

Source selection is uniform over distinct original sources. Five copying
probabilities remain fixed at zero, one quarter, one half, three quarters and
one. The fine report is the eight-valued endpoint. Three separate coarse reports
disclose just endpoint bit zero, one or two, with bit zero the least significant
bit. These are three fixed comparisons, not an outcome-selected vocabulary.
Source identity remains unknown. Full laws, hypothesis weights and source rosters
are supplied evaluator information; this is not learned provenance.

Compute each fine and coarse report's exact likelihood and posterior before
forecasting every future time, context and endpoint. Retain report probabilities,
support masks, conditional source probabilities and entropy, optimal future
multiclass squared loss, and squared forecast gain relative to no report. Also
retain every fine report's squared discrepancy from each corresponding coarse
forecast. Immutable hypotheses, laws, source rosters and the fixed content map
reconstruct every posterior and forecast. Impossible reports remain undefined.

Expected loss reduction equals expected squared forecast gain. The fine-minus-
coarse gain equals expected fine-to-coarse squared regret, and is nonnegative.
Expected source entropy cannot decrease when report content is discarded.
These are instrument controls; their satisfaction alone is not a finding.
The result distinguishes future information from source-identity information.

Average under each exact report law inside a posterior, then over 128 equally
weighted Cartesian identities per draw, two paired draws inside a law, and eight
equally weighted laws. Keep evidence conditions and checkpoints separate. Report
alphabet sizes, all denominators and unavailable cells. No fitted parameter,
practical margin or confirmatory uncertainty claim is specified.

Before dispatch require direct scalar Bayes and explicit one-hot proper-loss
checks, sparse/disjoint/constant laws, single-source and permutation controls,
retained/discarded informative bit fixtures, complete input corruption checks,
and full-support synthetic batch timing including raw serialization. Inclusive
cap is 3,600 CPU seconds for tests, failures, three complete executions,
independent reconstruction, regroup and operations within existing B/global
limits. One below-normal scientific CPU worker and one numerical thread remain.
Two-report dependence and conditional report-context disclosure remain prepared
independent alternatives; neither is claimed implemented by this admission.
