# C1: frozen rank-one factor interchange

Can a direction learned from training maker labels change the appropriate
purpose or belief prediction while preserving answers in factor-stay cases?
This follows the native fixture audit and is admitted only if both saved readers
again meet every original predictive capability threshold on that challenge.
The ordinary readers and their output heads remain frozen. This is an auxiliary
supervised access experiment, not a new architecture setting or a claim that
their ordinary training used factor labels.

Use the fixture packet's first 32 original training and eight development
coefficient lineages, all sixteen makers, two history draws and two fitted seeds.
Fit directions at prefix 32 only; evaluate prefixes 8 and 32 separately. The
shorter prefix is a transfer condition. No test or confirmation lineage is used.
All donor pairs, recipient states, native hybrid laws, and nuisance skill changes
are unchanged from the fixture protocol; none is selected by its observed effect.

For each saved fit and each of purpose and belief, regress the balanced signed
training factor label on the centered final hidden state. Freeze a ridge penalty
of 0.001 times the mean covariance diagonal plus 1e-8, with covariance divided by
the number of training examples. Solve once in double precision, normalize the
coefficient vector to unit length, and retain coefficients, intercept, training
residual and the normal-equation residual. There is no grid, iterative schedule,
development tuning or best-checkpoint selection. A zero direction is a recorded
instrument failure. The capacity is one direction in the unchanged 32- or
38-coordinate final state; this is a supervised linear probe, not a nonlinear
alignment or an optimization of intervention success.

Replace only the donor's coordinate along that unit direction in the recipient
state, then use the unchanged model head. Compare seven arms: no replacement,
learned probe direction, random equal-rank direction, within-lineage shuffled-label
probe direction, the other factor's learned direction, the training class-mean
difference direction, and full-state replacement. Randomness is fixed by fit and
factor identity before scoring. Every rank-one arm has the same swap equation.
The shuffled-label arm permutes labels separately within each balanced training
lineage. Mean-direction and ridge probes receive the same privileged training
factor labels. Full replacement must exactly decode the donor at fixed batch
shape. Stay donors preserve the selected factor but change skill; therefore a
factor-selective swap must resist nuisance changes rather than merely copy donors.

Score logarithmic loss and total variation against the known-maker hybrid law,
and total variation from the recipient's original predictions. Report factor,
change/stay, prefix, architecture, draw, seed and lineage strata. Also report
development probe-label accuracy as decodability, separately from writability.
Unknown maker identity can limit any reader's agreement with the oracle target;
oracle loss is retained. This experiment concerns static governing purpose and
belief, not historical local-goal labels or unique preceding process reconstruction.

For each arm report its hybrid-target loss minus no replacement, paired within
lineage, and learned minus random, shuffled, wrong-factor and mean-direction
controls. Average makers, contexts, draws and seeds within each of eight paired
development lineages before 10,000 bootstrap resamples. Keep per-fit variation
separate. The existing 0.02-nat practical margin describes conditional estimates;
an interval crossing it does not support selective access. A selective candidate
must improve change cases and retain stay performance, with relevant control
comparisons, in each factor/architecture. Do not pool a damaging cell into a win.
No exploratory result is confirmatory evidence.

Known-answer tests precede fitting: an orthogonal two-factor state with a true
selective direction; a constant-output placebo; random-label and wrong-factor
damage fixtures; exact no-op and full-replacement identity; normalized proper
scores; zero-direction and corrupted-input rejection. Keep original raw arrays,
saved directions, predictions, per-maker scores and timing, with plan, source,
input and environment hashes. Adjacent and extracted-source full replays use the
same serial worker and share C1's four-CPU-hour cap, including failures and review.

Reader exports remain anonymous observed codes and novelty flags. Factor labels,
directions, pair mappings and native targets are auxiliary-training or evaluator
evidence, never blind reader inputs. The two shared sequence-model settings stay
consumed; no Torch execution, new model architecture or sibling science edit is
required.

Two independent alternatives remain prepared if this access comparison fails.
F1 will fit native local transition counts on the original training population,
then compare direct state decoding, 1/4/16 sampled continuations, matched extra
decoding and oracle/wrong-law continuations for endpoint distributions. It must
freeze its initial state evidence, transition teachers and cost accounting before
fitting. D1's probabilistic cue-correction extension uses reliability priors
0.5/0.9, equal cue counts, truthful/wrong/retracted relation reports, and a declared
outside-family case. Both are independent of a C1 win; neither is implemented or
admitted by this protocol. C2 composition still requires a frozen selective site
and separate commuting-factor and noncommuting-dynamic reference tests.
