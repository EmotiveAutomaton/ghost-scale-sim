# Within-step dependence: frozen execution contract

Does preserving goal/operation dependence inside a step improve prediction after
between-step dependence has been removed? This implements the previously prepared
within-step protocol. Temporal results do not determine the roster or admission.

The complete original joint-readout population remains four evidence tiers,
32/128/512/2048 labels, sixteen development coefficient lineages, two training draws,
five feature seeds and four readouts. No fitting, new sample, protected lineage or
new tiny setting is used. Exact source, plans, environment, inherited forecasts,
reader inputs and native targets are bound before execution. The accepted temporal
score rows are bound too; every original and temporal metric must reproduce.

Compare the original joint forecast, the product of three eighteen-way step-pair
marginals, and the product of six individual goal/operation marginals. All derive
from the same full 5,832-label forecast including its expanded unknown tail.
Sum each pair marginal into its three-way goal and six-way operation marginals;
multiply the six probabilities in time order. Store all three pair, three goal
and three operation arrays. Native posterior versions are separate evaluator
rulers. No legal-support restriction or evaluator truth alters learned forecasts.

The primary contrast is six-coordinate product loss minus three-pair product loss.
Positive values favor retaining within-step dependence. Preserve original joint
metrics separately. Report 64 budget contrasts, sixteen normalized trapezoid areas
over log budget and 192 metric means from 30,720 learned rows. Expected native
coverage is 192 rows, plus 145,920 learned frame forecasts and 912 anonymous packets.
These are expected counts until independently adjudicated.

Use 10,000 paired sixteen-lineage bootstrap resamples with seed 191007. Average two
draws and five seeds inside each lineage; retain draw and seed variation separately.
Intervals condition on these fits and do not estimate universal training variation.
Logarithmic loss, squared error, native excess, compatible mass, nominal 90%
candidate coverage/size, unsupported mode, confidence, abstention, unknown mass
and per-step goal/operation accuracy remain separate. Reuse executed alphabet-first
tie priority, full-label priority for native rulers, and first cumulative crossing.

Before dispatch, known-answer tests cover within-step dependence with independent
steps, fully independent identity, equal single marginals with different pairs,
axis reversal, incompatible pairs, unknown tail, point masses, invalid inputs,
complete synthetic execution and corrupted original/temporal/native evidence.
The isolated suite also checks native budget enforcement and portable dispatch.

The independent scientific checker must enumerate integer label memberships and
accurate scalar sums directly from the original joint distribution. It must verify
all nine saved marginal arrays, both products, every native/learned metric, original
score reproduction, complete strata and paired regrouping without importing the
producer's factorization, score or regroup routine. Verify saved binary64 products
before tie-sensitive scoring. Numerical tolerance stays 1e-12 for marginals and
products, 1e-10 for scores and regroup. Complete adjacent and extracted-source
replays are required in addition to independent numerical reconstruction.

Inclusive cap: 1,800 CPU seconds within A, including preparation, failures, tests,
execution, both replays, independent review and write-through. A synthetic runtime
screen must fit the remaining allowance before admission. It is an estimate, not
an extension of the native cap. One existing worker, one thread, CPU only.

This is a constructed-method structural diagnostic, not a new G-P1 comparison,
historical correspondence, causal access or confirmation. No outcome changes
A3/C2/F2 prerequisites. Retrospective updating and witnessed-goal factorization
are independent prepared alternatives; each needs its own implementation and gates.
