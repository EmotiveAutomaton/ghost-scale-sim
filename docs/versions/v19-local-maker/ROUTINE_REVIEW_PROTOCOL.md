# Independent review of routine and current-request revision

Does a fixed revision opportunity distinguish retaining the prior routine from
explicitly adopting a current request? The completed original and both complete
replays agree on all 27 deterministic files. Their numerical conclusion remains
unaccepted until this independently implemented checker passes.

Reconstruct every retained native path, probability, goal, operation and transient
undo state with the separate native execution checker. Rebuild all collapsed
revision states, mass and source-index assignments. No producer revision-policy,
task-optimization or scoring function is imported by the checker.

Independently calculate the five frozen policies, including all tied task-optimal
operations, illegal tool exclusions, truthful request adoption and the independent
fair-bit prior for hidden current and undo presentation. Re-execute every outcome
and its success, operation cost, inspection cost, repaired failure and destroyed
success. Verify all 1,280 native population strata and the exact public projection.
Hidden maker traits and reference inputs never become reader evidence.

For the four paired contrasts (native minus keep, request-adopted minus native,
visible-task minus native, full-state oracle minus visible-task), preserve every
purpose/skill/routine stratum and the native population pooled over those traits.
Report all six metrics. Positive success differences favor the first policy;
positive cost, revision or destroyed-success differences mean more of that event.
Do not reinterpret the latter as a universal improvement. The 10,000 bootstrap
resamples use paired coefficient lineages and seed 190501, as already frozen.
The practical margin remains 0.02 absolute success units. These exact populations
contain no fit-seed uncertainty, learned competence or human observations.

Known-answer controls run before campaign inputs: undo, legal tools, explicit
purpose adoption, unchanged keep, hidden-bit invariance, intact oracle identity,
net task costs and deliberate corruption rejection. An isolated full native
fixture exercises every path and the weighted regroup. Admission pins inputs,
source, environment, tests and the original complete receipt. The existing serial
queue owns this verifier and charges it to the same four-hour revision card.
It samples no new population and consumes no tiny setting or protected lineage.

Two scientific alternatives remain prepared in
[the revision protocol](ROUTINE_REVISION_PROTOCOL.md): static/reset/coherent-mixture
filtering with fixed purpose changes and duplicate-source controls; and changed-tool
support transfer with both saved fits and common final smoothing. Each has its own
four-hour cap and still needs a handler, controls and frozen-source admission.
Neither is claimed as an executable job. A verifier result does not close V19 or
make either alternative depend on a positive result here.
