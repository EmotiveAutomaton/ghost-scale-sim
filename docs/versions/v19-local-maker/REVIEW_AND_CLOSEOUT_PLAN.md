- The final claim/evidence ledger, raw evidence, role-separated exports, cumulative
  costs, validation limits and remaining frontier are recorded and cross-linked.

  complete, with the remote head independently verified.
- No Ghost scientific worker or optional test child remains alive. The supervisor,
  event reviewer and project-owned one-shot helpers have finished or been shut
  down at verified ownership boundaries; final native state is recorded.

The immutable endpoint is the scheduled completion target. A documented blocker
can require an earlier owner decision; a missing final receipt is not evidence of
completion. New campaigns or a larger CPU ceiling require a separate decision.
