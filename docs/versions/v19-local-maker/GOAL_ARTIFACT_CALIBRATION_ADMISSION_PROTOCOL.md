# Endpoint-artifact calibration: executable contract

Does pooling complete witnesses across visible endpoint artifacts conceal opposing
errors in local-goal probabilities? This implements the prepared endpoint-artifact
alternative independently of the operation-group result. Retain sixteen development
laws, two training draws, five fit seeds, four budgets, four readouts, two frozen
forecasts, three goal classes and three positions. Native and equal-frame populations
remain separate. No new fitting, labels, sampling or protected lineages are used.

Enumerate all eight three-bit endpoint artifacts in lexicographic order. Group
membership uses only the final artifact in the public packet. Use the same group
at each scored position. Retain empty groups even if an endpoint is absent from
the fixed public roster. Never select a subset using targets, losses or outcomes.

Freeze ten integer-tenth probability bins. Preserve raw admitted binary64 values
and joint group/bin weight, forecast sum and native-correctness sum before any
division. Every empty conditional mean is NaN exactly where weight is zero. Store
group mass, conditional signed error, pooled and grouped absolute discrepancy and
their difference. Artifact marginalization must reproduce ungrouped class sums.
This cancellation diagnostic does not change forecasts or add evidence.

Save 320 full array bundles with axes law, population, position, goal, artifact
and bin, 92,160 metric rows, native self-forecast arrays, 704 unchanged reader
packets and 5,120 parent loss/accuracy identities. Preserve 576 means, 576
within-readout cancellation estimates, 1,296 bank-versus-baseline budget contrasts
and 324 normalized log-budget areas. Seed 191017 fixes 10,000 paired resamples of
sixteen laws; two draws and five seeds stay inside each law. All intervals condition
on retained fits. No new practical threshold or architecture winner is declared.

Before dispatch, require scalar known cancellation, no cancellation, native self,
empty groups, group/frame/class permutations, mass conservation, exact and adjacent
bin edges, a complete synthetic handler, exhaustive public endpoint assignment and
corruption checks. Test native and portable dispatch. Freeze inputs, source, plan,
environment and group/bin definitions. Synthetic full-size timing must cover original,
both complete replays, independent reconstruction, tests, failures and operating work
within the inclusive 1,800 CPU-second D card. A timing failure is a scoped blocker,
never an implicit cap extension or permission to sample fewer cells.

Numerical acceptance requires independent reconstruction of every array, parent
identity, roster and paired estimate after execution. Replays alone do not establish
numerical acceptance. Reader packets exclude forecasts and evaluator truth; scientific
and evaluator exports retain them separately. This is exploratory constructed-method
calibration, miniature — architecture untested beyond the fixed roster. Historical
goal correspondence and human intent remain unestablished. Retrospective updating
and declared-purpose calibration remain prepared independent alternatives.
