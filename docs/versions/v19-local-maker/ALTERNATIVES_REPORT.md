# Independent frame and jointness results — 21 September 2026

Does preserving goal–process dependence improve local reconstruction? With final artifacts alone, multiplying separate goal and process marginals adds 2.32802 nats of joint logarithmic loss and assigns 86.44% of probability outside the supplied compatible hypothesis family. Complete operation witnesses remove that gap. A truthful supplied relation lowers loss by 0.61413 nats under the neutral prior, but adds evaluator-selected information; rearrangement of unchanged evidence produces no gain in the stipulated identity control. These are exploratory constructed-method results, miniature — architecture untested; they establish neither learned reorganization nor human intent.


Joint logarithmic loss measures the probability assigned to the actual combination
of the three selected local goals and three operations, in nats; lower is better.
Each experiment has 512 sampled cases, from eight development coefficient lineages
and two trajectory-sampling seeds, with 32 trajectories per lineage and seed.
There are no fits. Each lineage retains all 13,824 trajectories with total mass one.
D1 has 7,168 score rows; D2 has 6,144. Repeated arms, seeds and projections are not
independent worlds. Intervals resample the eight paired lineage means, conditional
on this finite architecture and its declared sampling; they are exploratory.

The table compares the exact coherent joint posterior with the product of its
goal-sequence and operation-sequence marginals. The second column is their loss
difference, with a 95% paired lineage interval. The third is probability outside
the evidence-compatible supplied policy family. The fourth is the fraction of
sampled truths covered by selecting just the modal joint account; it is sensitive
to numerical tie selection and is retained as a descriptive original score.

The independent product preserves both sequence marginals exactly. Its loss and
support failures concern their association. Complete witnesses fix the operation
sequence, so multiplying by that point mass leaves the joint distribution unchanged;
the observed tie is an algebraic identity. In the other tiers, outside-family
mass refers to the supplied goal-conditioned policy support and observed evidence.
It is not a general claim of physical impossibility.

Twenty-four separate constructed rectangles (one for each lineage in the first
three evidence tiers) contain four executable goal/process combinations. Equal
diagonal and off-diagonal mixtures have identical marginals and opposite joint
association. These fixtures demonstrate that marginals cannot specify association;
they do not show that the given artifact distinguishes those two mixtures. No full-
witness rectangle exists because that evidence fixes the operation sequence.

In the frame experiment, neutral-prior base joint loss is 3.35574 nats; the truthful
new relation lowers it to 2.74161, a difference of -0.61413 [-0.64564, -0.58280].
The self-like prior weights functional purpose three times as much: its base loss
is 3.40998 and truthful-relation loss 2.79089, difference -0.61909
[-0.64930, -0.58860]. The relation states whether first and last local goals agree;
its truth/wrong classification is selected from evaluator truth. This is additional
teacher information, not an organization of identical propositions.

Accepting the wrong relation as a hard constraint excludes the actual account in
every sampled case and produces infinite true-joint loss. Its remaining accounts
can still lie entirely within the base-evidence family, and the single-account
contradiction rate can remain zero. Thus neither of those two measures alone catches
the wrong relation: truth coverage and the infinite-loss fraction must accompany
them. Correction is implemented as explicit retraction back to the base posterior.
Literal rearrangement, reread and irrelevant frames are stipulated identity maps.
Their unchanged outputs are controls, not tests of a learned reader's ability to
reorganize or correct itself. Outside-family illegal-rendering fixtures abstain;
that is a narrow absence-of-support test, not open-ended hypothesis invention.

Independent reconstruction found floating-order ties: summing weights before
normalizing preserves joint probabilities within 1e-12 but can change the modal
account. Original localized accuracy and single-best scores are preserved and not
used as stable selection evidence. Reconstructing the recorded arithmetic order
reproduces every metric exactly. The joint-loss and outside-support findings do
not depend on modal selection. Tie observations remain in the independent record;
the two failed review attempts that exposed the issue are retained in accounting.

**Warrant:** exploratory method advantage from retaining joint support under
partial evidence, and a ceiling under complete witnesses. D1 is an information-
addition demonstration with a forced wrong-cue failure, not a reorganization win.
Exact-family and known-law privileges remain explicit. Neither result validates a
learned local reader, causal access, simultaneous aims, multiple contributors,
human intention, calibration outside the family, or general architectural robustness.
**Pursuit:** retain joint packets; next test representation sufficiency and the
probability-valid readout rival independently. A further frame study would need a
genuine bounded inference operation or independently observed correction evidence.

**Validation and evidence:** all 13,312 score rows and every aggregate were rebuilt
from retained trajectories using separate grouping and scoring code. Both adjacent
and freshly extracted-source full replays match every deterministic output. Sources,
plans, environments and raw hashes remain intact. Reader archives expose only
declared base evidence; evaluator truth, cue selection and raw trajectories are
separate. No test or confirmation lineage and no shared tiny-model setting was used.
