# Noisy prior disclosure and mistaken certainty - 25 September 2026

We tested whether an imperfect cue about the source prior improves storage choices. With a cue that is correct two-thirds of the time, accounting for its errors improves retained coverage in 147 of 420 distinct problems. Treating the cue as certain performs worse than ignoring it in 276. Independent rational reconstruction and both full replays verify this constructed-method result; it does not establish forecast accuracy or process correspondence.

Retained coverage means the probability mass of source records kept by a storage
mask. The experiment crosses 28 finite mask libraries with 15 prior mixtures
whose weights are in quarters, and three symmetric cue channels. The cue names
the true prior with probability one-third, two-thirds or one; other labels share
the remaining probability equally. The channel and mixture are supplied, not learned.

At two-thirds correctness, informed cue use improves over optimal fixed storage
in 147 of 420 structural problems. Treating the label as certain loses relative
to the correctly interpreted cue in 336 problems and relative to optimal fixed
storage in 276. One-third correctness carries no information; perfect disclosure
matches the fully informed value. These are distinct structural problems, not
420 independent sampled worlds. The same answers recur across source rosters.

Independent reconstruction verifies seven structural cost records, 28,672 source
rosters, 84 prior-specific optima, 28 libraries and 58 candidate entries. Complete
contingent-policy enumeration uses rational arithmetic to check all 1,260 channel
problems, joint and conditional weights, every score and tie, certainty choices,
information values, penalties and byte charges. All 57,344 relational rows and
their 2,580,480 joined evaluations verify. The regroup preserves 20,160 paired
strata, 10,080 law strata and 1,260 equal-law cells, averaging two draws within
each of eight equally weighted laws. No new sampling or fitting occurred.

Zero-probability labels have undefined conditionals and zero expected contribution.
The largest-mask tie choice remains recorded; every mask meets the byte budget
individually. Original and both full replays match byte for byte. All 1,027 source
pins and source/input/environment/output bindings verify, as do 113 ancestor
provenance members and 1,029 isolated review-source files. Seventy-six isolated
producer, independent-review and runtime controls pass.
