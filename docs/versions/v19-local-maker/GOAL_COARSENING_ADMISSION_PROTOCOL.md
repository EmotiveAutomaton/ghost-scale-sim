# Exhaustive goal coarsening: implementation contract

The prepared protocol fixes all three binary partitions, fine goals and the
one-class endpoint. Class zero names the singled-out goal in each binary partition.
Both mapping the fine coordinate mode and selecting the coarse marginal mode are
retained, even at endpoints where they coincide. Each has always-report and
separate-step abstention at costs 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5. Exact modal ties select the
smaller class; threshold equality abstains using executed binary64 arithmetic.

All 320 frozen forecast arrays and 704 full-witness packets are inherited from
accepted goal abstention. There are sixteen development laws, two training draws,
five fit seeds, four nested budgets, four readouts and two forecasts. Native
posterior policies are separate supplied-law rulers. No new fit or sample is used.
The output roster has 307,200 learned rows and 960 native rows; 30,720 fine-endpoint
policy/cost identities must reproduce the accepted parent. Reader packets remain
unchanged and contain no forecasts, reports or targets.

Lossless projection sums all 27 fine paths into 27, eight or one coarse path.
Precomputed tables cover every partial report and every native law/frame, including
empty reports and zero-support extensions. Coarse correctness never means fine
identification. Report fine alternatives per opportunity and per reported claim;
the fine and one-class endpoints have one and three alternatives respectively.
Retain native/forecast cost, correct and incorrect mass, coverage, partial/full
incompatibility, coarse loss and unchanged fine loss. Conditional errors and
specificity per claim use pooled numerators and denominators; zero coverage is
undefined, never zero error.

Paired estimates compare abstention minus always for every partition/choice and
coarse-mode minus mapped-fine for each binary partition/policy. Bootstrap coverage,
incorrect mass, native cost, coarse loss and alternatives per opportunity; other
metrics and ratios remain descriptive. There are 7,680 budget contrasts, 1,920
normalized log-budget areas and 1,920 means. Seed 191013 fixes 10,000 lineage
resamples; draws and seeds stay inside lineages, with separate variation summaries.
The 0.02-nat primary prediction margin does not apply to coarsened goals or costs.

Known-answer controls cover scalar mass projection, all partial-report scores,
coarse versus fine correctness, modal noncommutation, one-class certainty, exact
and near thresholds, empty reports and deliberate input corruption. Isolated
controls and a conservative synthetic original/replay/review timing estimate must
fit the inclusive 1,800-second D card before source admission. Complete original,
adjacent and extracted-source executions precede independent numerical review.
The checker must independently reconstruct projections, reports, support, scores,
all inherited identities and every paired estimate; it is not yet implemented.

This is exploratory constructed-method evidence about specificity and coverage,
not historical fine-goal correspondence, human intent or universal preferences.
