Reconstruct the completed A2 result from immutable inputs in the existing serial
queue. This checker shares A2's original five-CPU-hour cap. It changes no scientific
method, population, selector, threshold, target, or fitted model setting.

Before outcome access, validate known linear recovery, a constant-target placebo,
informative selection, stable exact ties, probability repair, a known logarithmic
score and deliberate corruption rejection. Syntax and input-provenance admission
alone do not claim that numerical controls have passed.

Import the stipulated world's likelihood equations and query definitions, but no
producer feature builder, posterior updater, scorer, selector or readout fitter.
Rebuild all 4,608 training/development histories' feature encodings, uniform-prior
likelihood products, distribution teachers, exact banks, targets and references.
Check declared source deduplication, every identifier and four-class membership,
all split denominators and the reader/training array field inventory.

Regenerate four seeded random-feature bases. Independently solve ridge through
centered covariances and an unpenalized intercept, compare the saved acquisition
heads, and reconstruct learned banks and declared Gaussian perturbations. Check
all original/random/diversity/targeted selectors and candidate losses using only
their training partitions. Rebuild the three-penalty validation grid, its selected
penalty and all 48 target heads. Record separate pre-repair noise variance and
post-repair error; their equality is not assumed. Verify every saved forecast,
probability-invalid flag, score, capacity and acquisition-cost denominator.

Independent coefficients must agree within 1e-7, selection/validation losses within
1e-8, and probabilities/references/scores within 1e-9. Selector and ridge choices
must agree exactly; numerical closeness does not excuse a changed choice. These
are verification tolerances, not scientific effect margins. A discrepancy is
retained for review, never used to retune a scientific result.

Rebuild all 3,072 score strata and 2,048 numerical spectra. Average draws, feature
seeds and queries within each paired coefficient lineage, retaining four separate
classes plus the equal-class population. Reproduce the original 45 contrasts with
10,000 paired lineage resamples, seed 190501. Export fit/draw variation separately.
These conditional intervals do not quantify universal training uncertainty or
confirm the primary local-goal comparison.

The operating reviewer must adjudicate controls, choices, strata, noise effects,
costs and both complete replay maps before accepting numerical conclusions.
Raw scientific/evaluator records remain separate from anonymous artifact histories
and separately declared training teachers. Good old-world prediction does not
establish local process correspondence or human intent.
