# Dependence between steps: independently verified reversal

We tested whether dependence between steps improves frozen joint-process prediction. Removing it lowers the predictive bank’s logarithmic loss by 0.07256 nats with complete witnesses and 2,048 labels; matched frequencies improve by 0.05747. Independent reconstruction, all 80 paired estimates and both complete replays verify this constructed-method reversal. The native reference instead loses accuracy, so the learned improvement does not establish historical process correspondence or human intent.

Each forecast covers 5,832 three-step paths. The comparison multiplies three
eighteen-way goal/operation marginals from that same forecast. Dependence inside
each step remains; dependence between steps is removed. Unknown probability is
expanded over the full label universe before factorization. No public-mechanics
or evaluator support mask restricts either forecast.

The roster remains four evidence tiers, budgets 32/128/512/2048, sixteen
development coefficient lineages, two training draws and five paired feature seeds.
Raw history, frozen latent state, predictive bank and matched frequencies receive
the original matched training outcomes. Native posterior products are separate
evaluator rulers. No new fit, episode, protected lineage or confirmation is used.

Logarithmic loss is the native weighted negative natural logarithm of forecast
probability, measured in nats; lower is better. The first table reports product
minus original loss at 2,048 labels. Negative values favor removing dependence.
Brackets are paired 95% sixteen-lineage bootstrap intervals, with both training
draws and all five seeds averaged inside each lineage. Native penalty is the
separate reference comparison. These intervals condition on the retained fits.

Every learning-area contrast favors removing temporal dependence. This is a
reversal for the frozen learned forecasts, not a general independence claim.
The native reference has the opposite sign in every evidence tier. Its loss
increase is 1.26356 nats for final artifacts and only 0.00143 under full witnesses.
The learned improvement is consistent with reducing inaccurate dependence in a
finite fitted forecast, but this diagnostic alone does not identify that cause.
Matched frequencies share the gain, so it establishes no predictive-bank advantage.

With full witnesses at the largest budget, the bank's loss falls from 5.60252 to
5.52997 nats. Native-compatible probability nevertheless falls from 0.01123 to
0.00932. The nominal 90% candidate set grows from 175.5 to 227.41 labels on average,
and native candidate coverage rises from 0.84190 to 0.88078. Probability assigned
outside the original training alphabet rises from 0.000488 to 0.12746. Per-step
goal and operation accuracy remains unchanged because each pair marginal remains
fixed. Better logarithmic loss therefore does not establish better localization,
smaller uncertainty sets or historical correspondence.

All 1,994 deterministic files match across original, adjacent-source and complete
extracted-source replay. The 627 producer and 630 checker sources, source archives,
plans, environments, input/output bindings and separate timing hashes verify.
Independent reconstruction covers 145,920 learned frame forecasts, 14,592 native
frame/law combinations, 20,480 learned strata, 128 native rows and 912 packets.
Maximum marginal/product error is 6.11e-15 and maximum score error is 6.83e-13,
within frozen tolerances. Every original metric reproduces within 6.54e-13.
Separate direct-index regroup of the original rows reproduces all 64 budget
contrasts, 16 learning areas and 128 means against both checker regroupings within
1.43e-13. Resampling uses 10,000 paired draws and seed 191006; fit uncertainty
and universal training-population uncertainty are not inferred from these intervals.

Thirty-five isolated controls passed before checker dispatch. Integer division
and remainder assign labels to step pairs, with accurate scalar marginal sums.
The checker imports no producer distribution, factorization, scoring or regroup
routine. Saved binary64 marginal products are scored after numerical agreement
to preserve executed ties. Candidate sets stop at the first 90% crossing; modes
retain alphabet-first priority for learned forecasts and full-label priority for
native references. Native targets and fits are inherited accepted inputs, not
independently regenerated by this diagnostic. The first checker suite's missing
portable-dispatch failure remains retained and charged.

Reader inputs remain 912 unchanged anonymous artifact, context and witness
packets. Native truth, weights, fitted forecasts, marginals, scores and regroupings
remain separate scientific/evaluator exports with raw evidence and complete hashes.
This exploratory constructed-method reversal is miniature — architecture untested
beyond admitted rosters. It does not promote the primary bank-versus-latent/history
contrast, selective causal access or conditional A3/C2/F2 admission.

Within-step dependence is the next distinct prepared comparison. Retrospective
