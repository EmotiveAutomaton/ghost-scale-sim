# Cue acquisition cost: prepared alternative

At what price does consulting a source cue cease to improve a storage decision?
The prior results measure gross coverage and charge no cue acquisition fee.
Freeze the same 28 libraries, 15 supplied mixtures, three cue reliabilities and
original byte budgets. Use declared fees 0, 1/64, 1/16 and 1/4 in retained-source
probability units. These are hypothetical utility tradeoffs, not measured costs.

Choose before seeing the cue between a fixed feasible mask without payment and
a complete feasible three-label policy paying the fee once. Maximize exact
expected retained mass minus the fee under each known channel. Retain all ties,
the chosen policy, gross coverage, net utility, bytes and the exact break-even
fee. Compare free access, paid access and no access at matched supplied evidence.
Every realized mask remains individually feasible. Retain the original population
joins and paired/equal-law summaries; no random draws, training or new reader inputs.

Controls: zero-fee identity with the accepted noisy-disclosure result, known
break-even equality, identical-prior/no-information null, monotone acquisition
with rising fee, finite-law exact integration, byte corruption and zero-prior
support. Reconstruct ancestors and every calculation independently, freeze sources,
time complete synthetic support and require both full replays within a prospective
inclusive 1,800 CPU-second cap. Prepared, unimplemented and unadmitted. This tests
finite supplied-law information value, not forecast accuracy or process recovery.
