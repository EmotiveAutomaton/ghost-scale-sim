# Operation and conditional-goal uncertainty: execution and replay verified

We tested whether frozen joint-process forecast error comes from operations or from goals conditional on those operations. All 1,289 deterministic files match across the original run and both complete replays. The independent scalar checker passes 33 controls and has begun execution; numerical acceptance remains pending. This is a constructed-method diagnostic, not evidence of historical process correspondence or human intent.

The diagnostic uses the original frozen joint-process forecasts, before public
support restriction. It keeps four evidence tiers, four nested label budgets,
sixteen development lineages, two training draws, five paired feature seeds,
the raw-history, frozen-latent and predictive-bank readouts, and matched frequencies.
No model is fitted, no episode sampled and no protected lineage consumed.

Each of 5,832 labels identifies a sequence of three local goals and three
operations. Summing over all 27 goal sequences gives the 216-operation-sequence
marginal. Expected logarithmic loss, measured in nats, is the native weighted
negative natural logarithm of forecast probability. It decomposes into operation
loss plus goal loss conditional on the operation. The supplied native posterior's
entropy decomposes separately; subtracting each entropy from its corresponding
loss measures excess loss. The conditional component includes uncertainty about
goals even when the operation sequence is supplied. These are diagnostic components
of the original joint comparison, not replacement primary outcomes.

The reserved unknown probability is distributed over every unseen label before
calculation. No support constraint, true policy or goal is supplied to a readout.
Native laws and posterior masses enter evaluator scoring only. Parent localization,
candidate coverage, compatibility and abstention fields remain unchanged inherited
evidence; the new checker tests their identity, not a fresh reconstruction of them.

All 1,289 deterministic files match across original, adjacent-source and complete
extracted-source execution. All 611 producer sources, 1,285 input bindings per
execution, source archives, plan, environment and output/timing hashes verify.
The original reproduction receipt is retained. Its new numerical components and
paired results remain subject to independent adjudication.

The checker passed 33 isolated controls with no skips or warnings. It imports no
producer distribution, component, score or regroup function. It enumerates operation
marginals by label modulo 216 using scalar accurate sums, constructs each native
operation posterior independently, and computes conditional ratios directly.
It checks joint/operation/conditional losses, all entropies and excesses, three
chain identities, and agreement with all 10,240 previously accepted joint losses.
Its expected coverage is 145,920 frame forecasts and 912 public packets.

Independent grouping uses scalar within-lineage means and bootstrap multiplicities
for 10,000 paired sixteen-lineage resamples, seed 191004. It retains all 288
per-budget contrasts, 72 normalized log-budget learning areas and 64 means,
including separate training-draw and feature-seed summaries. A second regroup on
the original raw rows isolates aggregation discrepancies from reconstructed-score
roundoff. Conditional lineage intervals do not capture universal training uncertainty.

Controls cover uniform forecasts, perfect sparse posteriors, identical operations
with different goals, identical goals with different operations, full unknown mass,
axis reversal, nonfinite/negative/unnormalized forecasts and targets, duplicate and
missing strata, and a complete synthetic producer/checker fixture. Deliberate score,
entropy, inherited-field, reference, private-input, hash and summary corruptions
are rejected. The frozen checker contains 614 sources and 1,292 inputs. Its actual
execution was observed through the existing single-worker queue, below normal,
with one numerical thread. Completion review is a separate event.

Public reader evidence contains only the unchanged artifact, context and witnessed
operation fields. Native truth, fitted forecasts, posterior weights and score arrays
are separate scientific/evaluator evidence. All original raw evidence and inherited
inputs are retained in bounded archives, with source/plan/environment bindings and
both complete replays. No result is accepted from a favorable producer score alone.

The inclusive card limit remains 1,800 CPU seconds within the A family and the
accepted exploration pool, covering preparation, tests, failures, execution,
both replays and independent review. Pursuit stays open. Retrospective evidence
updating and learned joint factorization are prepared independent alternatives;
neither has an implemented or admitted handler. Conditional portfolio transfer,
joint causal composition and selective learned transfer retain their prerequisites.
No confirmation flag is promoted and no campaign completion is claimed.
