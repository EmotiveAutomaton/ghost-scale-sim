# Whole-stream purchase calibration verifies, with ties and numerical sensitivity

Can whole-stream calibration limit unnecessary model purchases? On the calibration data, raw surprise buys in 9.375% of every stratum; entropy-centered surprise buys in 4.6875–9.375%, with ties lowering some rates. Independent reconstruction and both full replays pass. This validates a constructed calibration method; held-out missing-law targeting remains untested.

This is the calibration stage of the retained R3 question: does subtracting the
model's own predictive randomness help target a paid missing-law purchase? Each
of 128 coefficient indices is paired across four old-world cells, three evidence
orders and two candidate-law priors, giving 3,072 streams. Each stream has 32
unique observations and supplied-law truth. The sensor sees actual executable
programs and supplied candidate laws; it is not artifact-only local-goal inference.
No held-out evaluation, local-world confirmation or new model fit occurred.

For each sensor and cell/order/prior stratum, the frozen threshold is the 116th
of 128 ordered whole-stream maxima. Maxima cover the 24 eligible opportunities
before observations 9–32, using the preceding four observations. A purchase needs
a strict threshold exceedance; nothing can trigger after the final observation.
Raw surprise is the observed program's negative log probability; centered surprise
subtracts entropy, the predicted average surprise over the same program alphabet.

The table gives empirical purchases under the frozen threshold. Rows distinguish
the two sensors. Each sensor has 24 paired strata of 128 streams; the range spans
strata and the pooled fraction weights all strata equally. These are calibration
counts, not independent validation estimates or guaranteed future rates.

A separately labelled post-calibration numerical diagnostic moves each threshold
by plus or minus 1e-12, without changing the scientific artifact. Across the 24
strata, centered purchases then range from 250 to 460 rather than the original
257; raw purchases range from 288 to 312 rather than 288. There are 210 centered
maxima and 24 raw maxima within that tolerance. This measures sensitivity of
strict comparisons, not new evidence about missing-law targeting. Matching source
replays establishes deterministic identity in this environment; it does not make
these borderline comparisons invariant to another floating-point reduction.
