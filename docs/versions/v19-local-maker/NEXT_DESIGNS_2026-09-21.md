# Readout and process-retention successors

Prepared after the first old-world and local screens; these are explicit designs,
not claims that executable handlers are admitted. The original fits, scoring rule,
sources and reports remain intact. Test and confirmation lineages stay reserved.

## Probability repair without a new representation

Question: does the relative readout advantage survive a probability-valid head
when every arm gets the same labels and a nested-label mean comparator?

Reuse the retained training/development arrays and saved 128-coordinate target
features from G19-04 and G19-05. Freeze a multinomial logistic target head with
129 coefficients per target category, zero initial weights, unpenalized intercept,
L2 coefficient penalty 0.01, and 200 full-batch gradient steps of size 0.1.
Use stable log-softmax arithmetic. Fit separately at the same four local budgets
and the same two old-world budgets, for both retained feature seeds and training
draws. No representation retraining, tuning, new draws or selected checkpoint.
Include the mean of exactly the same nested target labels as a prespecified arm.
Use identical old and new teacher access across the four representations; the
exact bank remains a privileged diagnostic. Preserve goal and operation scores
separately as well as the original equal-query area. Report optimization loss
and gradient norm; a fixed schedule can fail to converge and does not guarantee
a capable instrument. Known-separation and constant-target controls must pass
before admission, with a numerical-gradient check on a small fixture.

This tests a readout/regularization rival. It does not silently replace G-P1 or
the ridge screen. Initial cap: 3 CPU hours including controls and full replay,
within the existing conditioning allocation. It consumes no tiny sequence-model
setting. A source/input-hashed executable plan is still required before fitting.

## Exact bank versus the witnessed process

Question: can two evidence histories imply the same distribution over persistent
maker states, and hence the same fresh-episode bank, while requiring different
answers about the preceding local operations?

Use only the first eight already admitted local development lineages, the complete
retained trajectory enumeration, and all four existing evidence projections.
Group by exact visible packet first. Retain the normalized posterior over all
16 persistent makers and the full local-goal/operation answer distributions.
Search deterministically for evidence-packet pairs with equal persistent-maker
posterior and different process answers. Numerical near-equality is a candidate,
not an exact certificate: require an analytic support/symmetry argument or an
explicit symbolic equality before claiming information loss. Otherwise report
conditioning and tolerance dependence, with full residuals and no impossibility
claim. Include a synthetic exact-alias positive fixture and an invertible
near-singular negative fixture. Preserve reachable histories and their masses.

Report the witness in evaluator evidence separately from its two blind reader
packets; do not put the hidden posterior or process answer into those inputs.
Initial cap: 2 CPU hours including admission and replay, within conditioning/local
analysis. No fits, new random seeds or tiny settings. This alternative can proceed
regardless of whether the probability-valid readout succeeds. A frozen handler
and input manifest are still required before dispatch.

Frame and jointness batches already have their own admitted scientific runs and
adjacent replays. Their next action is independent event review and portable
verification, followed by their specifically warranted successors. The campaign
remains open; these prepared comparisons do not exhaust its scope.
