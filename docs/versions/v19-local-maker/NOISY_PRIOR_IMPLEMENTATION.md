This implements the previously prepared noisy-prior protocol without inspecting
its scientific outcomes. The comparison keeps the same 28 finite storage-mask
libraries, 15 mixtures in quarters, 28,672 source rosters and byte budgets.
Three symmetric channels report prior identity correctly with probabilities
1/3, 2/3 and 1; each incorrect label shares the remaining probability equally.

For each possible label, rational arithmetic records its joint prior weights,
probability, conditional prior, every mask score and tie, and the largest-mask
choice. A second choice treats the reported identity as certain but is scored
under the actual channel. All realized masks independently meet the byte budget.
Zero-probability labels have undefined conditional probabilities and retained
mass; their expected contribution is zero. No-cue and perfect-disclosure values
remain explicit, as do the penalty from mistaken certainty and expected bytes.

learned reliability, future outcome, fee, new observations or protected lineage
