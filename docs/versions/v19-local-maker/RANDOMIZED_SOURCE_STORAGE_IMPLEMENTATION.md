# Randomized source storage: frozen implementation contract

Implement the prepared alternative on the same 28,672 verified source rosters,
seven checkpoints, eight development laws, two draws, paired evidence roles and
two byte budgets. Three supplied source priors and twelve policy identities
define the same finite mask library. No new observations, fits or protected laws.

Architecture and capacity: exact rational basic-solution enumeration of lotteries
over individually byte-feasible masks. A basic optimum needs at most three masks
for three prior constraints. Enumerate support sizes one through three, each
matching active-prior set, and solve the normalization and active equalities by
rational Gauss-Jordan elimination. Reject negative weights and violated prior
constraints; collapse repeated solutions including zero-weight degeneracies.
Retain every optimal basic lottery. Choose the lexicographically largest full
rational weight vector in ascending mask order. A continuum of optimal mixtures
can exist; retained basic solutions are not claimed to enumerate that continuum.

Teacher inputs are evaluator source priors, structural costs and the verified
parent allocation library. There is no training schedule or reader input. One
complete pass emits 57,344 rows followed by adjacent and extracted-source full
replays. Expected prior masses, minimum expected mass, deterministic baseline,
worst realized mass, expected bytes and largest realized byte charge remain
separate. The prior is fixed before the lottery draw. No mask may overspend even
when its probability is small. Storage metadata and law costs follow the parent's
existing schema; no claim covers the computational cost of drawing a lottery.

Controls precede scientific execution: known two- and three-mask gains, empty/full
budgets, one prior, duplicate identities, degenerate ties, prior/item permutations,
independent full-constraint enumeration using determinant-based exact solutions,
corrupt costs/masks/masses/baselines and rejection of average-only feasible masks.
Complete retained-support timing uses synthetic costs and includes all libraries,
rows, serialization and hashes. Separate inclusive cap: 1,800 CPU seconds with
failed attempts, controls, executions, independent verification and write-through.

Independent review must reconstruct rosters, costs and parent prior optima; verify
all basic lotteries with a separate solver, every rational weight and mass,
ties and deterministic choice, realized byte feasibility, every original row,
paired/equal-law summaries and both complete replays. This is candidate-library
expected protection, not pointwise improvement, unrestricted robust knapsack,
forecast accuracy, learned provenance, historical correspondence or human intent.

Precision-choice stability and randomized source-prior regret remain prepared
alternatives, requiring independent implementation and admission.
