# Prior-mixture misspecification: prepared finite alternative

Does a storage policy chosen for the wrong source-prior mixture lose the value
of a correctly interpreted cue? Freeze the same 28 mask libraries, 15 mixtures
in quarters and three declared symmetric cue channels. Choose each contingent
policy using a nominal mixture, then evaluate it under every actual mixture.
The channel is correctly known in both steps; uncertainty about the mixture
is separate from treating a noisy cue as certain.

Retain all 15 by 15 nominal/actual mixture pairs. Compare the nominal policy
with actual-mixture-optimal cue use and the actual-mixture-optimal fixed mask.
Record exact rational expected mass, regret, signed gain over ignoring the cue,
all conditional choices and ties, and individually feasible byte charges.
Zero nominal probability uses the frozen largest-mask tie rule; an actual cue
can have positive probability despite nominal impossibility, so preserve that
case explicitly. No optimization against actual outcomes chooses a nominal policy.

Controls: matched-mixture identity, identical-prior null, known harmful mismatch,
point-mixture limits, joint prior/label permutation, exhaustive contingent-policy
enumeration and corrupted charges. Full original-row joins and paired/equal-law
summaries are required. No new sampling, training, protected lineages, fees or
reader inputs. This is sensitivity to supplied-prior error, not learned reliability
or process correspondence.

Prepared, unimplemented and unadmitted. Full-support timing must include every
nominal/actual pair, retained populations, raw serialization, two full replays
and independent reconstruction before admission under a prospective inclusive
1,800 CPU-second cap. Precision-choice stability remains separately prepared.
