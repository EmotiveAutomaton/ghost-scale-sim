# Shared-law rounding envelope: prepared independent ruler

How large can future probability error be under a quantized supplied law, before
conditioning on any particular history? This separates the law's worst possible
rounding error from the errors realized by a retained state. It remains useful
whether the complete retrospective precision comparison is admitted or blocked.

Use all eight existing development laws, all sixteen maker states, four contexts
and eight endpoints. Freeze float64, float32 and float16 casts crossed with direct
and one float64 row normalization. Retain every original and cast array. Empty
positive rows fail without floors. No new histories, fits, lineages or observations.

For each context and endpoint, record the minimum and maximum law difference
over maker states and its attaining states. Every normalized nonnegative state
mixture lies within this interval. Record the largest squared law-row difference
as the convex upper bound on squared forecast discrepancy, plus the maximum row
mass drift and zero-support losses. These are algebraic rulers, not discoveries
of learned access. Treat direct unnormalized forecasts with an explicit expected
one-hot loss calculation; do not replace that calculation with an assumption of
normalized forecasts. Do not infer historical process from a bound.

Before native admission, verify all vertices, fixed rational two-state mixtures,
uniform mixtures, endpoint/context/state permutations, deterministic and rare
endpoints, a deliberately undersized bound and a corrupted support mask. Separate
empty-row failures and exact zeros from numerical errors. Retain original,
adjacent and extracted-source runs, independent scalar extrema and original-row
regroup with equal law weights. No population or confirmation claim follows.

Prepared, unimplemented and unadmitted. The inclusive 1,800 CPU-second ceiling
competes within the existing family and global allocation, including controls,
failures, three executions, independent reconstruction and write-through. Complete
support timing and source/plan freeze are mandatory before dispatch. This finite
alternative does not authorize extending any previously blocked card's cap.
