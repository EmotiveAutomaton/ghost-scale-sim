# Three source-prior tables: prospective admission contract

Can a fixed bank of aggregate tables support a later change in the source prior
within its declared convex hull? The frozen basis contains uniform, time-proportional
and reciprocal-time priors. The seven query mixtures are the three vertices,
three equal pairwise mixtures and equal three-way mixture. Weights are selected
before the report. Joint report numerators mix before Bayes normalization.
The rival mixes already-normalized vertex posteriors with those same weights.

The implementation retains one future-group probability, three eight-endpoint
joint tables per group, and three eight-endpoint copied-report distributions.
That is 25 float64 values per group plus 24 float64 values. Single-prior storage
uses nine float64 values per group plus eight float64 copied probabilities.
The 512-value supplied law, 21 mixture coefficients and structural schedules
are counted separately. The source identities, likelihood arrays and source
probabilities retained in scientific raw outputs are evaluator reconstruction
material, not part of the proposed persistent state or a new reader input.
Acquisition still reads every source; no optimized-latency or minimality claim.

The complete retained population has 28,672 posteriors and 505,856 source
candidates, eight laws, both evidence conditions and two paired draws. The seven
mixtures, five copying probabilities and eight reports produce 1,003,520 summary
rows and 8,028,160 posterior/report queries. Every future time, context and endpoint
is checked. All 128 maker/change/duplication identities remain inside each draw.
The 48 unavailable checkpoint strata remain absent. No fit, new observation,
changed source roster or protected lineage is used.

Raw outputs preserve basis and group masses, copied probabilities, source priors,
mixture identities, report support, reference probabilities, future discrepancy,
group total variation and the normalized-posterior rival's squared prediction
regret. Undefined report outcomes remain NaN. Expected errors use each mixture's
own report probabilities. A support error is separate from a prediction error.
The finite-world method does not establish historical correspondence or human
intent, and priors outside the fixed convex hull remain outside scope.

Admission requires scalar source-weighted Bayes and explicit proper-loss checks,
vertex identities, a positive unequal-evidence-mass counterexample, certain-copy
and constant-law placebos, source/group/endpoint/basis permutation controls,
corrupted mass/support/storage checks and complete synthetic parent integration.
Both native and portable entry points must dispatch the same frozen implementation.

Complete-support synthetic timing must cover all seven available structures and
both source densities, regular/random/single-context rosters and complete batch
serialization. Charge all checks, failed attempts, original and two full replays,
independent reconstruction and separate original-row regroup to the inclusive
3,600 CPU-second card within the existing family and global ceilings. No timing
estimate may substitute a smaller sampled scientific roster. Full replays and
independent reconstruction are required before numerical acceptance.

Highest-probability source retention and finite-precision aggregate-state retention
remain independent prepared alternatives. A timing failure is an engineering
disposition of this implementation, not a null scientific finding.

## Measured disposition

The source-prior basis prototype passed 81 isolated controls, but complete-support timing projects 11,966.09 CPU seconds against its 3,600-second cap. No native scientific job was admitted; this is an engineering blocker, not a scientific null.

The initial inclusive estimate was 11,896.30 seconds. The final estimate adds
the measured 69.80-second benchmark charge. Three full executions alone project
7,483.50 seconds. No repair promising the required reduction is currently
identified; the initial implementation already projects joint masses before
mixing copying probabilities. A different implementation may remain possible.
The roster, scores, tolerances, reserve and native queue remain unchanged.
