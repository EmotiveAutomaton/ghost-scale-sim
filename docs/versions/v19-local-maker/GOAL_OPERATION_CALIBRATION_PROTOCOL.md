# Operation-conditioned goal reliability: prepared alternative

Can calibration pooled across witnessed operations conceal errors with opposite
directions? This independent diagnostic uses accepted goal-decision forecasts,
without requiring a favorable class-reliability result. It is prepared and
unimplemented. No new training, sample, protected lineage or model setting.

Group every retained frame by its public witnessed operation separately at each
of the three positions. Preserve all legal operation labels, all three goal
classes and explicit empty groups. Retain the complete sixteen-law, two-draw,
five-seed, four-budget, four-readout, two-forecast roster and both native and
equal-frame populations. Never choose groups from score outcomes or evaluator
goal labels. Fixed integer-tenth bins preserve joint operation/bin/class weight,
forecast and correctness sums. Marginalizing operation must reproduce the pooled
sums; dividing by operation mass happens only after those sums are retained.

Compare pooled absolute bin discrepancy with the sum of absolute discrepancies
before operation pooling. Their gap measures cancellation from grouping public
operations, not newly acquired evidence. Preserve signed group errors and each
group mass. Required controls include opposite operation errors that cancel when
pooled, identical group errors, empty groups, permutation invariance and complete
mass conservation. Paired law bootstrap seed 191016 with 10,000 resamples keeps
draws and seeds inside laws; no resampling of frames as independent worlds.

An inclusive 1,800 CPU-second D card must cover source-admitted implementation,
tests, all failures, both complete replays and independent reconstruction before
dispatch. This tests a constructed calibration method, not historical goal
correspondence. Public operation labels are supplied evidence; predictions and
evaluator truth stay outside reader packets. Retrospective updating remains an
independent alternative if this branch cannot be admitted within its cap.
