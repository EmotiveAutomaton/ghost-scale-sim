# Source disclosure: prospective implementation contract

Implement the already prepared source-disclosure comparison on all 28,672
retained posteriors, 505,856 distinct sources, five copying probabilities and
eight report endpoints: 20,234,240 source/report queries. Choose the source
uniformly before emitting content. Compare exact forecasting given content
with exact forecasting given both content and source. No fitting or new data.

The primary descriptive estimand is the expected reduction in multiclass
squared loss, summed over future endpoints and averaged over all future times
and four contexts. Under the conditional law it equals the expected squared
change between those two forecast vectors. Compute both quantities separately;
direct controls sum the one-hot endpoint losses. Retain each source/report's
joint probability, support, squared forecast change, proper score reduction,
future-schedule total variation, largest future-coordinate difference and mean
future total variation. Entropy reduction from revealing source identity is its
remaining conditional entropy after content; source alphabet size is the stated
one-answer cost, not a measured latency. Undefined pairs retain NaN.

Repeated identical future-time state maps may share computation only with their
complete inverse map and integer multiplicities saved. Reconstruct every original
time exactly; do not sample coordinates. Given bound posteriors, source identities
and laws, all forecasts and hypothesis posteriors are reconstructible. All new
exports are scientific/evaluator evidence; no new reader input.

Average each source/report under its true joint law inside a posterior, then 128
Cartesian identities equally per draw, two draws inside each of eight equally
weighted laws. Preserve both evidence conditions, all seven available checkpoint
cells and the 48 absent strata. No confidence interval or practical threshold is
prescribed. The result is supplied-law value of information, not learned source
identification or historical process correspondence.

Admission requires direct scalar Bayes and one-hot proper-loss controls, constant
and sparse laws, one source, equal source likelihoods, heterogeneous positive
controls, permutation, exact time multiplicities, malformed inputs, and parent
binding/identity corruption checks. Freeze architecture (complete finite Bayes),
capacity (all support, zero fitted parameters), teacher access (evaluator laws
and candidate-source identities), schedule, source and full-support synthetic
timing before outcomes. Inclusive cap is 3,600 CPU seconds for tests, failures,
original, both complete replays, independent reconstruction and operations, within
existing B/global limits. Timing failure records a blocker, never a sampled roster.

Two-report dependence and source-prior mismatch remain prepared independent
alternatives. Both need implementation, controls, timing and source admission;
their declarations are not executable handlers.
