# Source identity omission: prediction similarity and uncertainty loss

Does counting copied observations as distinct sources change the maker account? For the filter allowing unknown change time and type, final prediction differences stay within the declared 0.02-nat margin, but its 90% current-maker set includes truth 1.56–5.86 percentage points less often. Independent reconstruction and paired intervals verify this constructed-method result. Supplied laws and visible timestamps limit its scope; prediction similarity does not establish preserved uncertainty, historical process correspondence or human intent.

The comparison reuses 4,096 midpoint/stationary streams across eight coefficient
lineages, sixteen equally weighted makers and two observation draws. Source IDs
alone become distinct presentation IDs; contents, observation times and original
source times stay visible. These five supplied-law filters do not infer dependence
from the retained timestamps. This is identity-only omission, not complete
provenance blindness or learned trust.

All eight original final source-factor/change intervals for this arm lie within
the predeclared plus/minus 0.02-nat prediction-loss margin. This supports practical
prediction equivalence for these cells conditional on the retained roster, not
across all checkpoints or filters. Each final copied-stream set-inclusion interval
lies below zero. No practical margin was declared for inclusion, and the result
does not establish universal posterior calibration.

The reset filter behaves differently: at the 32-observation skill-change endpoint,
omission lowers prediction loss by 0.03372 nats, with a 95% interval from -0.05075
to -0.01595. The interval establishes a directional improvement but crosses the
-0.02 practical boundary. Reset retains sixteen supplied source identities;
omission therefore changes which true observations remain in memory. This is not
evidence that counting copies is universally beneficial. At 128 observations its
final copied-stream losses increase; not every interval stays inside the practical
margin. All 6,600 contrasts remain available, including prefixes and other filters.
