# Prior-mixture misspecification: executable contract

Freeze all 15 nominal and 15 actual quarter-grid mixtures of three source priors,
crossed with the same three known symmetric cue channels. Select each policy
only under the nominal mixture, using the frozen largest-mask tie rule. Score
that policy under each actual mixture without changing its choices. Compare
the actual-mixture optimum with cue access, the actual-mixture optimal fixed
mask, and the nominal-mixture fixed mask evaluated under the actual mixture.

Retain rational policy scores, all conditional ties, signed gains, regret,
expected byte charges and maximum realized charges. Labels impossible under the
nominal mixture can occur under the actual mixture; preserve their probability
and frozen fallback choice explicitly. Every mask is independently byte feasible.

All 28 original finite libraries and 28,672 source rosters remain fixed. Each of
57,344 roster/budget rows joins to 675 nominal/actual/channel conditions, yielding
38,707,200 evaluations but only 18,900 distinct structural comparison problems.
These joins are not additional independent observations. Independent review must
reconstruct the libraries, all policies and rational scores, all population joins,
paired strata and equal-law summaries before acceptance. Original and both full
replays retain source/input/environment/output identities.

Teacher access consists of evaluator-supplied source priors, mixtures, channels
and costs. There is no fitting, learned reliability, new sampling, future target,
fee, protected-lineage use or new reader input. The claim ceiling is sensitivity
of finite-library storage to prior error, not forecast or process accuracy.

Known-answer controls cover matched mixtures, harmful mismatch, identical priors,
point priors, prior/label permutations, full contingent-policy enumeration,
impossible nominal labels and corrupted byte charges. Full-support synthetic
timing, including serialization and replay/review allowance, must fit the inclusive
1,800 CPU-second cap before admission. Precision-choice stability and channel
reliability misspecification remain independently prepared alternatives.
