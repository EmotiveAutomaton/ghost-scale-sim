# Independent reconstruction of the completed purchase evaluation

This verification consumes only the completed, immutable R3 evaluation and its
frozen thresholds. It fits nothing, samples no new population, and changes no
purchase rule, score, threshold or evidence condition. It follows both full
evaluation replays in the existing serial queue and shares the original R3
three-CPU-hour cap, including its preparation and failed attempts if any.

The short verifier imports the stipulated native world's likelihood matrices,
but no purchase/calibration reader, recursive filter or scoring implementation.
Before reading outcome inputs it checks known posterior recovery, uniform-data
invariance, known mixture and selection forecasts, logarithmic scoring, strict
threshold ties, first eligible crossing, inert-purchase prices and deliberate
corruption rejection. Source parsing and input hashes are admission checks;
they do not claim these numerical controls already passed.

For all 1,920 saved streams, reconstruct every candidate/state posterior from
complete prefix likelihood products. Reconstruct all nine methods' sequential
and final artifact forecasts, program-alphabet sensor forecasts and entropy,
rolling surprise, class weights, native modal construction, final posterior,
forecast scores and complete sensor/predictor costs. Verify every original
cell/truth/order/prior/method aggregate. Audit the reader field allowlist and
content hashes independently of the evaluator's truth and pairing identifiers.

Keep numerical closeness and exact decisions distinct. The independent scalar
calculation must match predictions and sensors within 1e-10. Exact strict purchase
and action decisions use the verified saved arithmetic, retaining the frozen
thresholds and 1e-10 action tie rule. Record all opportunities within 1e-12 and
the purchase frequency under diagnostic threshold shifts of plus/minus 1e-12;
these diagnostics never replace the scientific purchases or calibration artifact.

Report all 864 original strata plus equal-cell/equal-order summaries by truth,
prior and method. Within each of the 20 coefficient indices, average paired
contrasts across cells and orders before resampling indices. Use 10,000 shared
paired bootstrap draws, seed 190501. Preserve each truth and prior separately.
The centered-minus-raw, centered-minus-no-purchase-mixture and raw-minus-mixture
contrasts include forecast loss, native action match, purchase frequency/time,
work, stipulated net scores and model entropy. These conditional intervals do
not quantify calibration-sample uncertainty or establish universal power at the
0.02-nat descriptive margin.

On completion, an operating review must inspect these records, compare all
original/adjacent/extracted-source deterministic bytes and separately bound
timing, then finish scientific interpretation, exports and documentary write-through.
Until then the evaluation is execution-complete, verification-pending; no accepted
targeting or cost advantage is claimed. A2's training-only portfolio comparison
and C1's native change/stay fixtures remain independent prepared alternatives.
Neither is implemented or admitted by this verification job.
