# Aggregate state for a later source-prior mixture: prepared alternative

Can a small fixed bank of source-prior tables support a declared later change of
source probabilities without retaining every source separately? Freeze three
source priors: uniform, proportional to source time, and reciprocal source time.
Retain one future-group/endpoint joint table and copied-endpoint distribution for
each prior. Compare direct source-level Bayes with mixtures of the three tables
at the three vertices, three equal pairwise mixtures and equal three-way mixture.
Mixture weights are selected before reports, never from observed accuracy.

Use all saved posteriors, eight laws, both evidence conditions, two paired draws,
five copying probabilities, eight endpoints and every future coordinate. Report
memory, construction and supported-report error against full hypotheses. A
mixture of priors mixes joint numerators before Bayes normalization; directly
mixing normalized posteriors is a declared rival. Priors outside the frozen convex
hull remain outside scope. This is neither arbitrary reweighting nor learning.

Prepared, unimplemented and unadmitted. Require scalar weighted-source Bayes,
vertex identities, permutation covariance, constant-law and certain-copy controls,
unsupported-report masks and a positive unequal-evidence-mass rival failure.
Complete-support timing and frozen source precede native outcomes. The inclusive
prospective cap is 3,600 CPU seconds for controls, failures, three executions,
independent reconstruction and original-row regroup within existing ceilings.
No fit, new observation or protected lineage. No minimum-state, optimized-latency,
historical correspondence or human-intent claim is licensed.
