# Prior information before storage: frozen implementation contract

Question: how much expected source probability is gained when the identity of a
supplied source prior is revealed before choosing storage? The comparison uses
the admitted finite mask library, its three prior vertices, 28 distinct roster,
cost and byte-budget problems, and all 15 prior mixtures with denominator four.

For each mixture enumerate all fixed masks and retain every optimum. Compare the
best fixed mask with choosing a prior-specific best mask after revelation and
with the existing maximum-minimum-coverage lottery. The revelation advantage is
nonnegative by construction; its magnitude and variation are descriptive results.
The linear average objective cannot be improved by a fixed lottery beyond the best
fixed mask. Do not report weak dominance as an empirical discovery.

Freeze exact rational arithmetic and choose the largest mask among tied optimal
masks. Preserve every prior-specific tie, even for zero-weight prior vertices;
zero-weight vertices do not affect expected or maximum realized byte charges.
No prior-observation fee is charged, so this estimates gross value of information.
It does not establish a net acquisition benefit or forecast improvement.

Architecture: finite enumeration; no fitted capacity, optimizer, training schedule
or outcome labels. Teacher: supplied evaluator priors and structural costs only.
Schedule: the same eight development laws, two source draws, paired evidence
conditions and seven checkpoints. Protected confirmation/test lineages are unused.

Raw storage is relational: all 57,344 roster/budget rows link to their selection
record; each of 28 selections contains all 15 rational mixture results. Joining
the two gives 860,160 paired evaluations without duplicating structural answers.
These are not independent scientific replications. All 420 distinct problems,
mask scores, optima, lottery comparisons and byte charges remain retained.

Validate with exhaustive adaptive-policy enumeration, all denominator-four fixed
lotteries on reduced fixtures, known strict/zero values, point mixtures, ties,
permutations and input corruption. Complete-support timing includes serialization
and hashes. Admit only if original, adjacent and portable replays, independent
reconstruction, paired/equal-law regroup and documentary work fit an inclusive
1,800 CPU-second cap and remaining family/global allowances. Freeze source,
plan, inputs and environment before outcomes. Reader inputs: none.

Precision-choice stability and partial prior disclosure are prepared independent
alternatives. This is a supplied-law constructed method, not learned access,
historical process correspondence, human intent or unrestricted storage search.
