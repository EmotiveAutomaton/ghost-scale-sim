# Robust cue-channel regret - 25 September 2026

We tested whether guarding against uncertain cue reliability improves storage beyond the policy chosen for two-thirds reliability, and found no reduction in worst expected regret in these 420 problems. It improves on fixed storage in 147. Independent rational reconstruction and both full replays verify this constructed-method result; forecast accuracy and process correspondence remain unestablished.

Retained coverage is the probability mass of source records kept by a storage
mask. Regret is the gap from the best expected coverage achievable when cue
reliability is known. We minimize its worst value over correctness from one-third
to one while the source-prior mixture is correctly supplied. The same 28 finite
mask libraries cross 15 quarter-grid mixtures: 420 distinct structural problems,
not 420 independent sampled worlds.

The robust choice and the policy chosen for two-thirds cue correctness coincide
in every problem. Compared with ignoring the cue, worst expected regret falls in
147 problems and ties in 273. Positive regret remains in 174 problems, with a
maximum of 0.125 probability mass. Twenty-eight problems have multiple optimal
policies; all ties and the predeclared lexicographically largest choice are retained.
This finite comparison does not prove that nominal reliability always gives
robust choices in other libraries or under other channel intervals.

Independent rational reconstruction verifies all 420 policy problems and all
2,995 distinct per-problem score-line intersection/diagnostic points. The maximum
regret agrees with endpoint reduction for every policy. We reconstruct 28 libraries,
58 candidate entries, 84 prior-specific optima, seven structural cost records and
28,672 source rosters. All 57,344 relational rows and 860,160 joined evaluations
verify, preserving 6,720 paired strata, 3,360 law strata and 420 equal-law cells.
Two draws are averaged within each of eight equally weighted retained laws.
Structural repeats add no laws. Expected byte charges and maximum realized byte
charges and coverage losses remain separate from regret in expected coverage.

The original and both complete replays match byte for byte. All 1,048 producer
source pins, source/input/environment/output identities, 113 ancestor provenance
members and 1,050 isolated review-source files verify. Forty-four isolated producer,
independent-review and runtime controls pass. The complete regroup is losslessly
archived with its uncompressed member hash. Reader inputs remain unchanged.

finite supplied-prior storage library, not forecast accuracy, learned channel
