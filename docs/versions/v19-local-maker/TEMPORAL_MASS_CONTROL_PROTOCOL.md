# Temporal product versus matched alphabet mass: prepared alternative

Does the temporal product improve prediction because it redistributes probability
outside the observed training alphabet, rather than because independent steps are
better estimated? In the accepted temporal comparison, both changes occur together.
This new diagnostic separates them without fitting or choosing weights from truth.

Use every original joint-readout tier, budget, development lineage, training draw,
feature seed and readout. Preserve the original expanded forecast and temporal
product. Build a third forecast by assigning the temporal product's total known
and unknown alphabet masses to the corresponding original conditional distributions.
Thus the third arm preserves the original relative probabilities inside each group
while matching the product's two group masses. Neither group is chosen using native
support. Empty/zero-mass groups require an explicit identity or impossible-case
disposition, never invented normalization. No new learned parameter or sample.

Primary contrasts are mass-matched minus original and temporal product minus
mass-matched logarithmic loss. Keep all original compatibility, localization,
candidate, abstention and unknown-mass metrics. Use 10,000 paired sixteen-lineage
resamples, seed 191009; average draws/seeds inside lineage and retain their variation.
Report each budget and normalized log-budget area. Native targets remain evaluator
inputs and all reader packets stay unchanged.

Controls include identical group masses, group-conditional identity, known-only
and unknown-only supports, point/uniform forecasts, disjoint native support,
candidate ties and a scalar loss decomposition into group mass and conditional
loss. Independently reconstruct both new distributions, every score and paired
summary; reproduce both accepted parent comparisons before claims. Full adjacent
and extracted-source replay, frozen sources/inputs/environment and raw exports
remain mandatory. Prepared and unimplemented, with a separate inclusive 1,800
CPU-second A-family cap subject to tractability and admission. No protected lineage,
tiny setting, primary-contrast promotion or human-intent claim is implied.
