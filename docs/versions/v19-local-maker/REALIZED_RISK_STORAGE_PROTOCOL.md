# Realized-risk-constrained storage: prepared finite alternative

Can a source-storage lottery retain its expected advantage while guaranteeing a
minimum retained mass for every realized mask? The verified coverage comparison
improves expected protection in 26 of 28 problems, but each improving lottery has
a worse least favorable realized mask than its deterministic baseline.

Freeze the same 28 finite-library problems, individually feasible masks and three
prior vertices. Compare realized-mass floors of zero, half the deterministic
maximum-minimum mass, and the full deterministic maximum-minimum mass. Retain
only masks meeting each floor under every prior, then optimize minimum expected
coverage by exact rational basic-lottery enumeration. Each feasible set includes
the deterministic baseline. Preserve all basic optima and the existing rational
lexicographic tie rule. No new laws, observations, random draws, fitting or
protected lineages. Teacher inputs are evaluator priors and structural costs.

Report minimum expected mass, worst realized mass, loss relative to unrestricted
randomization, individually realized byte feasibility and exact surviving mask
sets. A prior is fixed before each draw. Expectation and realized protection
remain separate, and the claim is restricted to the supplied finite library.

Admission needs known risk/gain tradeoffs, zero/full-floor identities, monotone
feasible sets and optimized objectives, degenerate ties, permutations, corrupted
costs/floors, and an independent full-constraint rational solver. Complete-support
timing, original plus two full replays, independent reconstruction, original-row
regroup and full write-through must fit a separate inclusive 1,800-second cap.
Freeze source and full design before numerical outcomes. No reader inputs.

Prepared, unimplemented and unadmitted. Precision-choice stability is an
independent alternative. This protocol does not claim forecast improvement,
learned provenance, historical correspondence, human intent or exhaustion.
