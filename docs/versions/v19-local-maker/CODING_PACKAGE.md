## 1. What changed after the critique

Claude’s review identified real targeting problems. The earlier plan left the local-goal world conditional, treated a heterogeneous roster as one mechanism test, and described linear readout as more open-ended than the world’s algebra warrants. Those are corrected here. The extension forest remains broad, but a runnable opening queue now precedes it.

Several stronger claims require correction:

- **An ill-conditioned bank is not automatically missing information.** A singular direction discarded by numerical tolerance differs from an exact null direction. A nonzero residual after truncation may be a numerical decision, not a mathematical impossibility.
- **Good old-question prediction plus injectivity does not guarantee accurate new answers.** Estimation error can be amplified by inversion; learned banks need not lie exactly on the feasible state simplex.
- **In-span, well-conditioned failure is not uniquely an access failure.** The learned bank may itself be inaccurate, the training may fail, or the query encoding may be inadequate. Compare exact and learned banks on the same roster.
- **Linear transfer is world-conditional.** A linear map may exist separately for every world while learning one map across unknown worlds remains difficult. If query inputs carry the full law, the experiment has supplied the solution.
- **A tiny trained transformer still needs admission.** Knowing the generator gives a reference, not an automatically competent neural reader.

The current V18.4 decoder comparison is promising pursuit evidence. Claude supplied a paired lineage bootstrap that retains the sign of the diversified-bank benefit. This planning pass inspected the world and decoder implementation but did **not** re-download and independently recompute the roughly 146 MB of paired scientific archives. The precise intervals and class means below remain externally recomputed results to reproduce natively before treating them as a new accepted result.

## 3. Scientific rulers before learning

Let `p_h` be the posterior over hidden states after history `h`; `B` maps that posterior to a bank of old-question probabilities; and `a_q` maps it to one new answer probability:

\[
b(h)=B p_h,\qquad y_q(h)=a_q^\top p_h.
\]

For a fixed known world, exact transfer from the bank exists for every feasible posterior when `a_q` lies in the row span of the augmented matrix `[B; 1ᵀ]`. The extra row accounts for posterior normalization. Equivalently, every vector `v` satisfying `Bv=0` and `1ᵀv=0` must also satisfy `a_qᵀv=0`. These are algebraic conditions, not learned mind-reading results. [Predictive representations of state](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf)

For every world/query family record:

1. The full and tolerance-dependent rank, singular spectrum, affine dimension and target-span residual.
2. The tolerance and scale used; test stability under a tighter numerical tolerance or higher precision on small cases.
3. An exact-bank readout and a learned-bank readout, so state-estimation error is not blamed on decoding.
4. If claiming nonidentification, produce two nonnegative normalized posteriors with the same bank and different target answers. Label whether both are reachable under the admitted histories/prior. An algebraic witness over the whole simplex need not occur in the experiment’s reachable subset.
5. If conditioning is the issue, measure perturbation amplification and noise-weighted forecast error. Raw minimum singular value alone ignores which future questions matter and how accurately bank entries can be estimated.

Do not declare an out-of-span certificate from a truncated pseudoinverse alone. A full-rank bank may identify every finite-state query exactly while making recovery unusably sensitive to learned error.

### Three access conditions

- **Oracle-law diagnostic:** exact `B`, target map and allowed latent states. This asks what information the chosen bank can in principle retain.
- **Learned-law task:** public domain mechanics/operator semantics, development observations and declared training outcomes; no test-world policy matrices or hidden rule label. This asks how much data is needed to learn usable access.
- **Unknown-operator task:** an operator whose semantics were not provided or identifiable from training. Treat the missing information as a real limit. A useful result may be a set of compatible predictions or a request for evidence.

Match outcome supervision across bank, latent-state and raw-history decoders. An exact Bayes reference may receive more information, but must remain an explicitly separate ceiling.

## 4. The small local-goal world: core implementation

Build a finite dependency-edit world using existing native execution/record conventions. It is a ruler for local intentions and process alternatives, not a miniature natural-language psychology.

### Minimal world contract

- Three visible artifact units, each with a small finite set of renderings. Templates can make units readable; the underlying semantics remain symbolic.
- A small set of governing purposes and local subordinate goals. A local controller selects its goal **before** choosing an operation; do not assign goal labels afterward from the observed action.
- Operations such as edit one unit, repair a dependent unit, move/replace a unit, accept a tool proposal, inspect/keep, and undo. Start with a short horizon and a bounded set of trajectories.
- At least one real dependency between units and one optional presentation tradeoff. The same operation can serve different local goals.
- Maker skill changes reachable operations or their expected consequences; beliefs change perceived state; current purpose and learned routine remain separable when the automaticity extension is admitted.
- Evaluator records include governing/local goals, controller states, actual operations, dependency edges, tools, observations and visible artifacts. Public projections expose only the declared evidence condition.
- Include endpoint aliases: different processes yielding identical final artifacts, and identical operations serving different aims. Exact inference should return an equivalence set where appropriate.

Keep the first configuration exactly enumerable: begin with no more than 32 persistent maker states, short episodes of at most three substantive operations, and a tractable explicitly counted trajectory set. These are engineering defaults; if the existing executor needs a different count, preserve exact tractability and record the change before outcomes. The full hidden state must include whatever is necessary for the Markov/reference calculation; do not apply the old 24-state algebra to a process with omitted transient state.

### Deliver immediately

One fixture each for final artifact only, truthful added context, sparse operation history, complete witnessed history and misleading context followed by correction. Oracle truth stays in a separate evaluator fixture. A deterministic exact reader and a direct finite likelihood/template baseline must consume the same public projection. Export passage anchors, local-goal alternatives and process alternatives on day 1 or 2 even if the first learned model fails.

The reader may know the domain’s legal operations without knowing which operation the maker chose or its policy. Giving it the actual program, local controller label or all state-conditioned answer matrices is a different evidence condition.

## 7. Extension forest and outcome-directed continuations

### G19-A. Conditioning, portfolio choice and learned access

**Question:** Is the remaining loss caused by noisy estimation along fragile predictive directions, limited supervision, or inadequate query representation?

Compare the original question portfolio, random size-matched portfolios, a training-only noise-aware optimal-design portfolio, and a simple diversity heuristic. An oracle design using the true law is a separate upper reference. Evaluate total bank-acquisition and fitting cost, not only the final decoder.

Cross exact/noisy/learned banks with linear ridge, probability-valid learned decoder and the existing hull oracle. Hyperparameters are selected on development worlds. A linear readout that emits invalid probabilities is not silently fixed with evaluator knowledge; score invalids and specify any projection available to every arm.

**Branches:**

- Exact bank succeeds, learned bank fails, and error follows conditioning: compare weighted design and regularization at equal size/data; then transfer portfolio choice to a held-out law.
- Exact bank also fails under a purported certificate: stop the inference claim and repair the reference/tolerance/target mapping; preserve the failed run.
- Only oracle-designed portfolios help: law knowledge is the bottleneck; compare learning portfolio selection from development outcomes.
- All well-conditioned access arms tie: preserve the ceiling; move to recursive updating or genuinely new target semantics.

**Budget:** initially 8 CPU h; up to 16 after timing. Do not add dozens of nearly identical ridge penalties. A small declared grid plus diagnostic spectra is enough.

### G19-B. Recursive state, retention and accumulated error

**Question:** Can the representation update without rereading the whole past and preserve the information needed by later local questions?

Compare full-history recomputation, cached direct features, a learned predictive-bank updater, a flat recurrent state, and one compact stochastic/mixture state. Reuse the best two admitted representations rather than install all candidate frameworks.

Train one-step and rolled-in update variants with equal evidence; test histories of 8/32/128 observations where native domain length permits. Count initial acquisition, each update, storage and future-query cost. Include duplicate/correlated evidence, transient goal changes and skill changes separately. Exact filters are oracle references where available.

**Branches:** drift with accurate one-step updates leads to rolled-in training or bounded resets; apparent savings disappearing after initial fit leads to a break-even report; point-state failure only under ambiguity leads to a small coherent mixture; all models matching full history leads to a cost result rather than invented accuracy superiority.

**Budget:** 12 CPU h initially, up to 24 for the informative continuation. [PSIM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/sun16.html) and [RPSP](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/hefny18a.html) supply established algorithms; their original assumptions and legacy implementations do not automatically cover this controlled maker setting.

### G19-C. A small capable instrument for causal access

**Question:** Does an internal state selectively mediate the right local-goal/belief counterfactual?

Train the tiny transformer on permitted histories and observable outcome targets. Do not supervise its ordinary task with test latent labels. An auxiliary latent-labelled arm is allowed only when every compared architecture receives the same labels. Admission compares its held-out task performance with no-history and exact references, checks constant-answer collapse, and verifies the counterfactual fixtures.

Fit belief readouts and a bounded DAS-like alignment on development pairs. Compare learned interchange, random equal-rank, shuffled-label, wrong-variable, mean-direction and full-state replacement controls. Include cases where the correct output should change and where it should stay fixed. Train single changes and test coupled changes. Independent static factor assignments should commute where the reference says they do. Separately test an ordered pair of dependent state updates whose reference transitions genuinely do not commute. Do not manufacture a requirement that every goal/belief swap be order-sensitive; factor interchange and sequential dynamics are different tests.

**Branches:** decodable but not selectively writable gets one alternative layer/site attempt; interventions changing every answer are damage, not evidence of a role; selective transfer goes to a new law or local-goal composition. Training a representation specifically for this task establishes a learned controlled mechanism, not a spontaneously present human-like state in a language model.

**Budget:** 8 CPU h initially, up to 16. Ghost owns generator/reference data; Sounding Line owns the shared causal consumer and may perform training there if resources require. Only one environment pays for the same fit. The two initial model settings are a combined allowance across both repositories, not two attempts each; record the training owner and remaining attempt allowance in both handoffs. [Belief geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15943), [DAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02536).

### G19-D. Reorganization, missing hypotheses and correlated aims

**Question:** Can a new organization of old evidence improve understanding without receiving a hidden answer?

Maintain complete executable maker hypotheses. Compare a single best account, a joint mixture and independently combined marginals. Include two models with identical local marginals but different dependencies. Score process compatibility and new local relations, with future responses as an additional discriminator.

Separate three cue classes: rearrangement of identical established assertions; a newly proposed explanatory relation; and newly observed evidence. For each cue record atomic content, what E0 entails, and any use of hidden outcomes in selecting it. Cross correct, plausible wrong and irrelevant frames. Begin from a self-like and a neutral prior in separate cells. Include an outside-family cause and allow unknown.

**Branches:** old-fit improvement without independent correspondence goes to overfitting/complexity; wrong frames equally persuasive goes to evidence-selection and correction; actual candidate expansion working goes to untouched operator combinations; unresolved alternatives become packet uncertainty. ### G19-E. Expertise, practice distribution and apparent mistakes

**Question:** When does acquired productive competence reveal choices that observation alone misses?

Use equal-capacity learners and a genuine persistent update. A frozen stateless reader receiving exactly the same prompt/state, tools and randomness is an identity control; calling one history “practice” cannot change it. Compare off-policy demonstrations, exact replay of a learner’s trajectory, and actively chosen training trajectories. Record weights, optimizer state, retained memory and reset rules.

Vary mismatch between demonstrator-state and learner/test-state distributions while holding sample/feedback budgets fixed. Covariate-shift correction is a specific rival: benefits may track support mismatch. It does **not** imply a universal monotonic curve or guarantee zero difference between actively trained and replay-trained systems with different update histories. Test the curve as a hypothesis. [DAgger](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686)

Independently cross functional success with audience/display success and intact/degraded observations. Verify reader production skill separately. Add prior routine versus current objective and an inspection/revision opportunity; a skilled automatic choice need not express current endorsement.

**Branches:** benefits explained by distribution coverage become a learning result; inversion gains remaining after exact information/update matching motivate a mechanism audit; degraded-evidence-only gains motivate partial-observation transfer; apparent mistakes reversing with the maker’s task support goal–expertise interaction.

**Budget:** 8 CPU h initially, up to 14. Conditional production-related advantages in [human action recognition](https://academic.oup.com/scan/article/18/1/nsad046/7265365) motivate this interaction, not a claim that the toy learner reproduces embodiment or human flow.

**Budget:** 10 CPU h initially, up to 18. Succession is from G19-B or G19-C, not from a requirement that the primary bank arm wins. Successor features are a later alternative if the specific goal-versus-dynamics distinction remains open; they are not a new mandatory dependency this week.
