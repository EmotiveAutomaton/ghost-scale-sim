# Conditional-goal mass: independently verified partial explanation

We tested whether redistributing probability between goal paths seen and unseen in training explains the gain after all operations are witnessed. At 2,048 labels, redistribution lowers the predictive bank’s logarithmic loss by 0.10244 nats; changing probabilities within those groups adds a 0.04608-nat reduction. Independent reconstruction, all 40 paired estimates and both complete replays verify this constructed-method decomposition. It does not establish historical goal correspondence or human intent.

Complete public operation witnesses restrict each frozen forecast to 27 goal
paths. The control matches the goal product's probability in two groups—paths
present in the inherited training alphabet and its complement—while preserving
the original relative probabilities inside each group. Membership never uses
native truth. The remaining product-versus-matched difference changes only those
within-group probabilities. This is an exact logarithmic-loss decomposition along
this declared sequence, not a unique causal attribution to model components.

The fixed roster contains sixteen development coefficient lineages, two training
draws, five paired feature seeds, four readouts and budgets 32/128/512/2048. There
is no new fit, sample, protected lineage or native-support restriction. Operations
are supplied; historical goal paths remain uncertain.

Logarithmic loss is the native weighted negative natural logarithm of the forecast
probability, in nats; lower is better. The table shows changes at 2,048 labels.

mass-matched. Negative numbers mean improvement. Brackets are paired 95% intervals

loss. No primary bank advantage or confirmation is promoted.

Redistribution is not uniformly better evidence recovery. At 2,048 labels, the
bank's probability on native-compatible paths changes from 0.999703 to 0.990676,
then to 0.999475 for the goal product. Probability outside the training alphabet
changes from 0.000301 to 0.011262 and is identical in the last two arms. Accuracy
of step goals selected through the joint modal path is 0.866301, 0.866301 and
0.865466 respectively. These decisions retain the inherited alphabet-first tie
order. They do not measure coordinatewise modal decisions; the separate prepared
goal-decision protocol fixes smallest full-label ties for that comparison.
