# Noisy metadata replies: numerical result verified

We tested whether accounting for noisy replies preserves their predictive value. With 75%-accurate replies and supplied native laws, calibrated conditioning reduces forecast loss by 0.01789 nats on original tools and 0.02566 on changed tools. Treating replies as certain assigns zero probability to the true endpoint on 2.53% and 4.00% of native reply mass, making total logarithmic loss infinite. Independent reconstruction, all 14,256 paired estimates and both complete replays verify this constructed-method result; learned access and human intent remain unestablished.

The comparison enumerates both possible replies for each of 640 retained queries,
eight development laws, original and changed tools, native and uniform legal
conditional laws, and native and equal-query populations. Reply reliability is
0.5, 0.75 or 1. No request, fixed skill, fixed belief and entropy-selected requests
are separate. Both readers use the same field selected under the actual channel;
only the conditioning rule changes. No new fit, episode or confirmation is used.

Logarithmic loss measures forecast error in nats; lower is better. The table uses
75%-accurate replies and entropy-selected fields. Each row names the tool rule,
supplied conditional law and scoring population. The first numerical column is
calibrated loss minus no-request loss; the second is the reply-weighted mass where
the certain-reply reader assigns zero probability to truth. Brackets are 95%
paired development-law bootstrap intervals. Positive mass in the final column
means infinite total logarithmic loss, regardless of its finite contribution.

At a stipulated cost of 0.02 nats per request, the native-law native-population
loss changes become +0.00211 nats on original tools and -0.00566 on changed tools.
At 0.1 nats both worsen. This is a forecast benefit with a cost-dependent reversal,
not an unconditional reason to request information. The original-tool forecast
benefit lies below 0.02 nats; the changed-tool interval exceeds that reference
margin. No new practical threshold is used to change a validity gate.

At reliability one-half calibrated replies add no information. At reliability one
both readers recover the truthful-parent forecasts; there is no zero-target mass
under native weighting. Squared forecast error, probability assigned to truth,
residual ambiguity, request rates and all prices remain separate in the full
regroup. Expected scores average realized reply scores, never the score of the
averaged forecast. Finite loss contributions are never total loss when infinite
mass is positive. Under equal-query weighting a native law can omit endpoints
that are mechanically legal, so those support limits are reported separately.

The independent scalar checker reconstructed all 4,608 cells, 23,808 channel-law
rows and 1,228,800 forecast vectors; maximum discrepancy was 3.33e-16. It checked
legal completions, actual reply probabilities, zero-mass fallbacks, infinite loss,
costs, evidence roles, reliability-half identities and truthful-parent forecasts.
Native masses inherit the independently verified parent. Zero modeled reply mass
uses the frozen uniform likelihood-supported legal fallback, or the whole legal
group if support is empty; those conventions are not inferred evidence.

Independent scalar sums changed 100 field choices within the declared 1e-14
entropy tolerance. At most 48 of 640 queries changed choice in a stratum. The
logarithmic and squared score effects were exactly zero; probability assigned
to truth differed by at most 1.11e-16. The vector-sum alternative changed no
query choices. Choice identity and score stability remain distinct findings.

A second regroup uses original cells and an independent bootstrap multiplicity
matrix, reproducing all 14,256 estimates within 4.44e-16. Eight paired laws are
the uncertainty units; 10,000 resamples use seed 190978. Repeated questions,
reply strings and prices are not independent lineages. Intervals condition on
the fixed roster and supplied laws; no fitting or training-population uncertainty
is measured. Some equal-query uniform-law intervals are degenerate because the
same law-independent values repeat, not because general uncertainty is absent.

All 3,454 original deterministic files match both complete replays. Source, plan,
environment and input/output hashes verify for all executions and the checker.
Nineteen isolated checker controls preceded the complete reconstruction, including
deliberate numerical, evidence, choice and denominator corruption. Original
execution evidence remains retained alongside this numerical acceptance.

The 3,224 reader packets contain only visible artifacts and operations, requested
field, reported binary value and stipulated reliability. Hidden truth, laws,
conditional weights, targets, reply probabilities and selection maps remain
separate scientific/evaluator evidence. Supplied mechanical reliability is not
human report reliability; predictive scoring does not establish historical process
correspondence, learned access or human intent.

Disposition: exploratory constructed-method advantage, null at an uninformative
channel, and cost-dependent reversal. Pursuit remains open. Repeated independent
versus copied replies tests provenance and information accumulation; retrospective
evidence after future-schedule grouping remains independent. Neither requires this
comparison to win. No protected lineage or tiny fit is released.
