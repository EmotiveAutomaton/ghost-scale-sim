# Operation and conditional-goal decomposition: implementation contract

The prepared uncertainty protocol is implemented as a bounded diagnostic over
the original frozen joint-readout forecasts, without support restriction. Labels
encode one of 27 goal sequences and one of 216 operation sequences. Summing the
goal axis yields operation probabilities; true expected logarithmic joint loss
equals operation loss plus conditional-goal loss. Native posterior entropy is
decomposed independently with the same chain rule. Their differences give joint,
operation and conditional-goal excess loss. All identities use a 1e-10 tolerance.

Keep all four original arms, four tiers and nested label budgets, sixteen
development lineages, two training draws and five paired feature seeds. Reproduce
all 10,240 original joint-loss strata. Original localization, abstention, candidate
and compatibility fields are retained as inherited independently verified metrics,
not relabeled as freshly recomputed controls. Full unknown-label mass remains.

For each of six loss/excess components, compare the predictive bank with raw
history, frozen latent state and matched frequencies. Report 288 paired budget
contrasts and 72 normalized log-budget areas, with draw/seed summaries separate
from 10,000 paired lineage resamples using seed 191004. Also retain all 64
arm/tier/budget means. Averaging draws/seeds within lineage precedes resampling.
Negative differences favor the bank. Components are explanatory diagnostics;
the original joint primary comparison and 0.02-nat margin remain unchanged.

Known-answer controls cover uniform predictions, perfect posterior predictions,
same operations/different goals, same goals/different operations, unknown-only
truth, invalid mass, swapped axes, complete synthetic execution, original-score
corruption and input/reader-role corruption. Independent scalar reconstruction
of every frame/law and all original-row regroupings is required after both full
replays. No new numerical outcome is accepted at admission.

The 1,800 CPU-second ceiling includes preparation, controls, failures, original
execution, both full replays and independent review, within A and the existing
global exploration pool. No model is fitted, no episode sampled, and no protected
lineage consumed. Retrospective evidence updating and learned joint factorization
remain two independent prepared alternatives. This is a constructed-method error
decomposition; architecture, causal mediation and historical goals are unestablished.
