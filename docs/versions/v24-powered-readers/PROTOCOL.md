This protocol declares preparation choices before any V24 scientific pilot.
It does not alter V23's frozen results or commission confirmation in the pilot
window. These are constructed methods; no result alone identifies human intent.

## Mixture scores and evidence

For candidate probabilities p and comparator q, use p_e=(1-epsilon)p+epsilon*q.
When q assigns the observed label positive probability, the log-score gain is
log(p_e/q), bounded below by log(epsilon). This is a modified probability reader,
not clipping the observed loss. Epsilon candidates are 0.01, 0.02, 0.05 and 0.1;
choose the largest with strictly less than 2% loss of the retained discovery
mean, only when the original mean is positive. Freeze the choice before new
pilot or confirmation outcomes. A missing eligible mixture is an explicit hold.

The evidence unit is an independently generated complete balanced block. Under
the declared null E[X_t | past] <= m=0.02 and a valid lower bound L, a predictable
stake lambda in [0,1/(m-L)] makes 1+lambda(X_t-m) nonnegative with conditional
expectation at most one. The implementation chooses from
{0,0.1,0.25,0.5,0.9}/(m-L), starting at 0.25/(m-L), then selects the best
cumulative *past* fixed-stake log wealth. Ties use the first grid entry. The
current observation never selects its own stake. Support violations fail;
observations are never clipped.

This explicit grid strategy uses the predictable-betting construction described
by [Waudby-Smith and Ramdas](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/86/1/1/7043257).
It is not claimed to reproduce their optimal strategy. An exhaustive ten-step
fair-sign null checks expected wealth and threshold-crossing error; a positive
fixture verifies that the instrument can accumulate evidence.

Sample sizing chooses enough complete blocks for the best frozen candidate
stake's empirical expected log evidence to reach 1.5 times the threshold. The
forecast does not guarantee power or that adaptive staking achieves the fitted
rate. New excluded pilot data must test calibration and measure all costs. The
64-hour future feasibility bar includes replay and checks; an infeasible family
requires a separately declared fixed-sample design outside e-LOND. It does not
increase the 40-hour pilot budget. Confirmation order, final thresholds and
production admission remain unfrozen.

## Search and extensions

N1's implemented scheduling primitive uses eta=3 over all 240 cells, retaining
80, 27, 9, 3 and 1 in successive rounds, with cumulative per-cell allocations
1, 3, 9, 27, 81 and 243 complete worlds. Ties retain original roster order;
missing or nonfinite scores stop selection. This is one successive-halving
schedule, not all of [Hyperband](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume18/16-558/16-558.pdf).
The scoring target, initial worlds justified by costs, and full N1 integration
still need freezing before admission.

History matching uses I=abs(mean-target)/sqrt(emulator variance + discrepancy
variance + observation variance), rejecting I >= 3. This follows the October 17
direction; it does not use the older ambiguous I-squared >=3 wording. The
emulator and observational target are not yet implemented or fitted.

Seed expansion requires a complete initial look, a prospectively frozen
promisingness rule, and one disjoint extension of at most the original count.
The primitive refuses incomplete or already-expanded looks. The persistent
wrapper now freezes the initial seed roster, protocol hash and mean threshold
before data. A complete initial mean at or above that threshold permits one
extension. Both positive and negative decisions consume an exclusive, flushed
receipt; a partial receipt after interruption is a hold, not another look.
Family-specific thresholds remain to be declared. This descriptive pilot
helper does not authorize fixed-sample inference after optional expansion.
The implemented direct-reader rank instrument uses
100 independent posterior draws and an independently recorded uniform draw to
break discrete ties. Truth must be sampled from the prior and observations from
its likelihood; fixed-truth sweeps do not satisfy the calibration contract.
Rank histograms remain diagnostics without an automatic pass verdict. This
follows the joint-distribution requirement in
[Talts et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1804.06788v2). Unsupported readers stay
unavailable; no posterior sampler has been invented for point-only readers.

Six new conceptual directions, full T1 integration, full-grid export acceleration,
dependency isolation and further promotion decisions remain separate work in
CARDS.json. Their pending status is not a scientific null or a runnable queue.
