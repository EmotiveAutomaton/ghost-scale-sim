# D5 exact-fit cost/control pilot

Can the certified grouped fit execute a fresh complete D5 cost/control world
with exact replay and independent verification? One excluded fresh world
completes all five 128-target paths and 70,704 chronological fits in
1,694.875 CPU-seconds across original execution, replay and independent review.
All 30 scientific files replay byte for byte. This verifies a cost/control
pilot only. It does not decide D5's forty-world primary, repair the retained
V23 failures, support a human claim, or admit production.

## Scope and result

The frozen pilot retains eight writers, sixteen rounds, four options, the
48-target future-label prefix, and separate public-input and evaluator-truth
streams. Its one fresh world includes the planted, uninformative and constant
conditions plus complete future-label and option-permutation controls. Each of
the five paths has 128 chronological targets.

The maximum independent reconstruction errors are 1.14e-13 for the objective,
5.97e-15 for the gradient and zero for predictions. The reconstruction also
regenerates the inputs, checks canonical ledger transport, every selected ridge
penalty, convergence certificate and prediction. The pilot makes no effect
interval or primary decision from its single excluded world.

## Integrity and cost

The immutable plan, source archive, acceptance contract, certified upstream
engine commit, canonical schema snapshot, interpreter and resident NumPy/SciPy
files match their recorded hashes. The private grouped adapter remains separate
from the canonical engine. The unexecuted predecessor is preserved; its sole
repair throttled heartbeat persistence while leaving every CPU and deadline
check active.

All 30 indexed scientific files in the original pass match the fresh-process
replay byte for byte. Their nested byte counts and SHA-256 hashes also match
both raw indexes. A third process independently reconstructed all 70,704 fits
and 640 targets. The completion receipt binds the plan, source, summary, replay
and review records. The exact terminal event hash is
`4e60f9dff0abf5f8985ca45a4e0c5891355778bf525982a3490f01e667604b7c`.

Original execution used 643.390625 CPU-seconds, replay 537.5625 and independent
review 505.921875. The native packet total, including parent overhead, is
1,694.875 CPU-seconds (0.4708 CPU-hours), below its 5,400-second reservation.
A purely linear forty-world projection is about 18.8 CPU-hours for the same
original/replay/review structure, including about 7.1 CPU-hours for original
execution alone. This clears the protocol's 64-hour future-feasibility bar but
does not fit the current D5 four-hour family allowance and is not authorization.

## Disposition

**Accepted as a verified cost/control pilot only.** It establishes that the
grouped certified arithmetic can complete the full one-world D5 path with exact
replay and independent numerical review. The original forty-world criteria and
failures remain unchanged. Any powered D5 study needs a separately frozen
design, budget and admission; this pilot allocation admits no production, and
production cannot begin before October 26 at 08:00 Pacific.
