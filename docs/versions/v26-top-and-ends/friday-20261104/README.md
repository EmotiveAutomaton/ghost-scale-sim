The implementation frontier has eight themes:

- **Hierarchy:** cross library size, overlap, episode length and observation dose
  to determine whether the weak expert-reader contrast is stable or hides a narrow
  region where it reverses. Preserve unknown-piece support and report unavailable
  likelihoods explicitly.
- **Circulating values:** measure false first crossings under fixed values,
  persistence of crossings under circulating values, and effects of genuinely
  changed menus. Specialist-only shift aliases and irrelevant thresholds remain
  labelled as such. Retained-data analyses precede fresh extended histories.
- **Revision:** cross revision completeness, discovery timing and document length,
  separating initial-goal recovery from evidence about the locally settled goal.
- **Pauses:** map observation dose and difficulty burden while retaining the
  explicit difficulty model and its misspecified comparator.
- **Own-model priors:** cross prior concentration and library mismatch to map the
  self/population/mixture crossover and its uncertainty.
- **Cached signatures:** cross learning rate, innate mixing and cue type to locate
  when an amplifier assumption misleads and when generator evidence is weak.
- **Source learning:** quantify uncertainty in the training-length threshold and
  sensitivity to source strength and claim dose, preserving joint source-policy
  inference and whole-world uncertainty.
- **Alias-breaking interventions:** distinguish true intervention-strength changes
  from nominal setting labels, then map the information gained over passive data.
