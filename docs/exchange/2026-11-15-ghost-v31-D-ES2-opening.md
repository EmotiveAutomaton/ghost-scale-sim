# D: first diary calibration table

How does a diary estimator recover one dial or competing sensitivities? In the first 18 constructed settings, the split-day estimator, its same-prompt diagnostic and a random-slope moment estimator are fully replayed and independently reconstructed. This opening map covers 2,272 people, ten days with six prompts, three event rates and six populations; it does not cover the full diary grid or establish a human trait.

The table holds the mean event rate at 0.20. Targets are correlations between reactivities, not their logarithms. Each estimate averages 200 independent worlds; intervals use eight world blocks. Same-prompt values above one are retained estimator failure diagnostics.
