# Ghost V30 blind live-sum subset

What does a partner gain from seeing a refusal when the maker's state and record are hidden? In the BLIND condition a public refusal changes the forecast by construction, by zero; this 48-setting subset covers live-sum weights 4 and 8, not the complete hidden-reader map. For illustration, with weight 4, plentiful resources, low offers, loss 2 and no rare large offers, the entrusted share is 57.8% (95% whole-block interval 49.1–66.5%). This is a descriptive constructed-mechanism result, not a claim about people.

The subset crosses two live-sum weights, three rare-offer rates, two partner losses, two resource environments and two offer regimes. Each setting has 200 worlds in eight blocks and 1,024 steps. Forecast changes condition on observed refusals; rows without events are unavailable. Known answers, excluded forty-world pilots, complete seed replay and independent reconstruction pass for every cell. Intervals are pointwise. Shared generators and summary conventions limit independence; miniature — architecture untested. No registered claim.
