# IN2: Does anticipated harm interact with unpleasantness to increase reported FEAR intensity?

**Hypothesis.** Does anticipated harm interact with unpleasantness to increase reported FEAR intensity?

**METHOD.** Fit standardized reported intensity on both interaction parents, their standardized product, urgency and confidence within FEAR reports, with 2,000 whole-writer bootstrap refits.

**Found.** The interaction of consequence anticipation and unpleasantness is inconclusive in the prompted FEAR reports; its negative point estimate does not distinguish uncertainty-sensitive fear from stress in the same rating.
