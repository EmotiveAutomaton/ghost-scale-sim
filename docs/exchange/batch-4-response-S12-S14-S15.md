## S-15 · Recovery error shrinks with more artifacts, converges, and the residual is priced

**The convergence exists and is clean.** With the theory's assumptions intact (bounded six-member
profile family, expert reader, clean tier), profile identification goes **0.53 at one artifact →
0.98 at fifty**, monotone at every grid point (Spearman 1.0). The theorems' irreducible ambiguity
is real and small here: the L1 residual at fifty artifacts is **0.009**.

**The assumptions were then removed one at a time, and only one of them bound.**

- **Convergent midbrains (the bounded family) is worth 0.24 L1.** Replace the six-profile family
  with 64 random profiles that do not contain the truth and the asymptote rises from 0.009 to
  **0.249**. That is the measured value of sharing a hypothesis family with the maker, and it is
  the largest single number this batch produced.
- **Expertise did not bind — the pre-registered criterion FAILS and is reported as failing.**
  A reader with signatures corrupted halfway to random (d = 0.5) ends at L1 **0.0096** against
  the expert's 0.0093 — margin 0.0003 against a locked bar of 0.05. The diagnosis is the same
  ceiling this simulation has flagged before: at 24 observations per artifact, goal
  identification saturates even through a half-corrupted template, and construction A routes all
  profile evidence through the goal. **The expertise assumption's price is invisible at this
  observation length**, not demonstrated to be zero. A shorter-artifact / lower-α restatement is
  queued (with T-11's off-ceiling work); until it runs, quote C2 as *half-established: the
  family is priced, expertise is not*.
- **The corpus price (C4): n\* = 20.** At CURATOR-tier transmission with an inexpert reader
  (d = 0.25), profile identification crosses 90% at **twenty artifacts per maker**. That is the
  power analysis for the follower-corpus design: below ~20 works per maker at that noise, values
  do not separate, whatever the instrument.

**The construction discrimination (S-13's question, answered as far as a simulation can).**
Under **conjunctive satisfaction** (every emission partially satisfies all channels — the G54
account) one artifact identifies the profile at **0.97**. Under **amplification** (one goal
drawn per occasion from the standing weighting — the §3 account) one artifact reaches 0.53 and
fifty are needed. Your own real-text record — every single-artifact values attempt failed, every
within-maker multi-work design worked, no acceleration at stacked motivations — **is the
amplification signature.** If human values worked like conjunctive satisfaction, your
single-artifact instruments should not all have died. Treat that as the strongest
mechanism-suggestive reading this batch supports, with the bridge stated: it borrows its force
entirely from measurements you already made.

Gates: convergence positive, shuffled-makers null at exactly chance (0.167), all-uniform world
reads uniform, construction gap live. All green.

## S-14 · The absent drive is recoverable — under commission, and only through the pursuit

**The aperture is revealed by commission, not by observation.** Masked makers (a channel at hard
zero) against unused makers (the same channel at ε = 0.02): in **spontaneous** standing work
they discriminate at **0.61** — the trace is nearly invisible. Under **commission toward the
missing channel**, with amplification multiplicative in the standing drive (attention can only
amplify what exists), discrimination goes to **1.00** at twelve artifacts. The commission gap is
0.39 against a locked bar of 0.20.

**And the discriminator is the pursuit channel and nothing else (C6).** Both maker kinds deliver
the commissioned surface identically; strip the pursuit channel (λ = 1, pure compliance) and
discrimination collapses to exactly 0.5. The reader is reading *how* the goal is pursued — the
substitute drives the masked maker routes through — which is the made-under-duress mechanism in
its first measurable form.

What must hold outside: commissioned work with known briefs exists; instruction amplifies rather
than replaces; compliance and pursuit are partly separable in real artifacts.

## S-12 · The smear is real, and G22's residual instrument sees through it

**A three-locus structure with variable per-unit loci reads as ONE mid peak in 100% of runs**
under the position-averaging instrument every published depth profile uses. The smear reproduces
in 100% of twenty random re-parameterisations — architectural, which for this claim is the
point: *any* variable-locus world smears, so the field's mid-layer consensus is consistent with
a three-locus truth. A rider found by the module's own gate: amplitude alone can also do it —
with the default middle bump, even *fixed* loci read unimodal, because the middle's shoulder
outgrows the side peaks.

**The residual route separates what the profile cannot: AUC 0.87.** Fit each unit its own single
peak, correlate the early-third and late-third residuals across units — the shared early–late
gain (your ratio-variance structure) survives in the residual when it is invisible in the mean.
**The honest bound from the severity rider: the separation reproduces in only 25% of random
re-parameterisations.** The instrument works where the shared gain is strong relative to noise;
it is not a free detector. Before running it on real models, calibrate the expected gain
strength — the placebo (two batches of the same one-locus world, AUC 0.64, inside its tolerance
but not comfortably) says the statistic wants generous unit counts.
