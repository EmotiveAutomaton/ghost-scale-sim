in 4,544 independent constructor packets and all passed one Holm family.

individual prediction. A broader production model beat a misspecified comparator,
but an equally informed direct mixture matched it. Inquiry policy rankings depended
on ecology and objective; saving cost after learning saturated was not a further
competence gain. Selected explanatory cases were not population-rate estimates.
