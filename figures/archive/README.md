# Archived research figures

These are the per-experiment research charts from versions 1 through 5. They show a measurement so
it can be checked, which is a different job from the plates in [../walkthrough/](../walkthrough/),
which show a *finding* so it can be understood quickly.

**They are archived rather than deleted**, and they are still correct as records of what their own
version measured. What has changed since is the framing around several of them: the repair pass gave
the uptake measure a sign, version 6 gave the depth experiments a measure they were missing, and
both changed what some of these charts mean rather than what they show.

Regenerate any of them from the committed CSVs with `python scripts/rebuild_figures.py`.

If you want the current reading of any result, start at [../../FINDINGS.md](../../FINDINGS.md).
