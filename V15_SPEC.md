## 0. Executive command

Build V15 around one question:

> **Under which combinations of latent coupling, evidence overlap, scarcity, history, context, and model misspecification does a reader need a coupled and expandable maker model to predict what happens next—and when is a cheaper factorized reader equally good?**

V14 established several useful rulers but did not answer this question. Its process, episode-goal, and preference routes were deliberately disjoint and individually informative. In that regime, exact joint inference beat independent marginals by only `0.011` nats. That is a result about one easy-to-factor construction, not a general verdict against joint reconstruction.

Three V14 positives also require stronger rivals before they can inform Sounding Line:

1. competence and practice history were made orthogonal by construction, and the reader received their matching feature channels;
2. equal-skill acquisition paths carried fixed planted transition signatures;
3. the off-audience source action was generated directly from the hidden source belief and read with the matching likelihood.

V15 therefore replaces identity demonstrations with boundary tests. It must:

- vary coupling and access continuously rather than compare one joint world with one factorized world;
- compare fixed, factorized, joint, particle, model-expanding, and direct-prediction architectures under the same observations and effective compute;
- generate expertise and residue from learning histories rather than handing the reader a history feature;
- distinguish one foreground goal with switching from simultaneous weighted control, habit, and hierarchy;
- distinguish persistent preference change from changed context, concealment, and lagging expertise;
- make strategic-source hypotheses predict noisy counterfactual behavior rather than expose a private-belief readout;
- compare learning progress with changepoint-aware and robust information-seeking policies;
- score understanding only through hidden continuation, next edit, stopping, changed-context choice, or a declared identifiability boundary.

No curator decision is required before implementation. The unresolved design choices are the crossed hypotheses of the program.

## 1. Claim ceiling and novelty ledger

### 1.1 Permitted claims

V15 may establish:

- a logical identity or identifiability boundary in a declared constructed family;
- a phase boundary where one estimator begins to beat another on prospective prediction;
- a method result that reproduces across at least two independently implemented generator families;
- a failure mode of a declared reader under misspecification, scarcity, duplication, shift, or equifinality;
- a constructed ruler that Sounding Line may test on real records;
- whether a full maker-state representation predicts better than correct but decontextualized labels in these worlds.

V15 may not establish:

- that a human performs Bayesian inverse planning, particle filtering, rapid goal switching, or any implemented computation;
- that simulated history is human expertise, character, value, embodiment, or previous attention;
- that a simulated appraisal is felt affect or valence;
- that a human source is sincere, fanatic, deceptive, propagandistic, trustworthy, or aligned;
- that a static artifact uniquely identifies its maker, production history, director, or terminal value;
- that an exact reader's access is psychologically available;
- that success in a constructed world transfers to language models or people.

### 1.2 Mandatory claim classes

Every result carries exactly one primary class:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `CONSTRUCTION_IDENTITY` | The generator made the answer directly readable or orthogonal; useful only as a positive ruler. |
| `METHOD` | An estimator, test, or scheduler behaved as declared. |
| `BOUNDARY` | Several states are observationally equivalent, or a phase boundary was measured. |
| `SIMULATOR_DISCOVERY` | A nontrivial result survived generator, estimator, and lineage rivals in constructed worlds. |
| `BRIDGE_CANDIDATE` | A bounded ruler is ready for a separate real-record test. |
| `INSTRUMENT_FAILURE` | The intended construct was not live, leaked, or could not be scored. |
| `VOID` | Runtime, lineage, provenance, or preregistration integrity failed. |

`LANDED` describes record completion only. It must never imply that a criterion held.

### 1.3 Publication-relevance ledger

For every promoted flight, write `PUBLICATION_MAP.json` fields:

- `established_component`: closest existing inverse-planning, pragmatic, identifiability, curiosity, or control result;
- `project_specific_delta`: what this result adds beyond that component;
- `evidence_grade`: identity, method, boundary, simulator discovery, model evidence, or human evidence;
- `strongest_missing_rival`;
- `independent_generator_count`;
- `external_validation_needed`;
- `paper_shape`: none, benchmark/resource, methods note, simulation study, model study, or human study;
- `maturity`: context only, seed, hardened seed, or submission-ready.

The ledger is a novelty audit, not a request to write a paper.

## 3. Constructed ontology

### 3.1 Maker and episode state

At episode `t`, define:

\[
M_t=(C_t,K_t,H_{1:t},V_t,G_t,Q_t,\Pi_t,B_t,U_t,S_t,A_t,R_t,O_t).
\]

| Symbol | Constructed meaning |
|---|---|
| `C` | context, constraints, affordances, role, audience, and available tools |
| `K` | current competence and learned transition model |
| `H` | dated training, attention allocation, feedback, instruction, constraint, and reward history |
| `V` | standing preference distribution across contexts, without assuming a terminal horizon |
| `G` | foreground episode goal or switched sequence of foreground goals |
| `Q` | control architecture: single-switching, simultaneous-weighted, habitual, or hierarchical |
| `Pi` | context-realized policy/process over action and revision chains |
| `B` | maker belief about the relevant world state |
| `U` | intended audience state and action |
| `S` | evidence-selection and presentation policy |
| `A` | maker appraisal variable, separate from audience and reader response |
| `R` | source reliability history and current correction policy |
| `O` | opportunity set, rejected alternatives, costs, and unobserved options |

The generator must allow the same final surface to arise from different histories, control architectures, source motives, and reward classes. It must also allow different surfaces to arise from the same higher-order state.

### 3.2 Attention, foreground goal, and expertise are distinct

For V15:

- **attention** is a limited allocation of observation, computation, rehearsal, or control precision;
- a **foreground goal** is a currently selected target state that organizes policy;
- **expertise** is a learned transformation and prediction structure produced by attention, feedback, instruction, constraints, embodiment analogues, and practice;
- **history residue** is any lasting bias from that learning history;
- **standing preference** is a cross-context choice tendency not reducible, by definition, to any one residue.

Attention may help create expertise, but V15 must not define expertise as previous attention and then report their relationship as a finding.

### 3.3 Labels are pointers, not maker states

A label such as `warn`, `accurate`, `defensive`, or `explore` is a lossy pointer. Compare it with a context-realized candidate containing:

- relevant context and opportunity set;
- predicted action/revision distribution;
- process constraints;
- conditional stopping rule;
- expected changes under an intervention.

A correct label that does not improve a hidden event is not scored as understanding.

### 3.4 Reader architectures

All applicable cards compare a subset of:

1. `surface`: frequency, last action, local transition, and artifact-template baselines;
2. `label_only`: correct or inferred decontextualized latent labels;
3. `independent`: separately inferred process, goal, and tendency marginals;
4. `staged`: fixed commitment orders and an adaptive order chosen from current uncertainty;
5. `joint_exact`: exact posterior in worlds small enough to enumerate;
6. `factor_graph`: structured message passing without enumerating the full grid;
7. `particle`: sequential Monte Carlo over evolving maker-state hypotheses;
8. `expand`: uncertainty- or residual-triggered addition of latent variables, candidate policies, or earlier timesteps;
9. `direct_predictor`: predicts hidden events without explicit maker-state labels;
10. `oracle_model_space`: correct candidate variables and likelihood family, but never hidden truth at test;
11. `oracle_state`: upper bound only; never a competitor eligible for promotion.

The expandable architecture uses a finite, predeclared symbolic proposal library. No language model is required in Ghost Scale. This isolates hypothesis management from language generation and creates a later bridge for Sounding Line.

### 3.5 Information and compute matching

Non-oracle readers receive identical raw observations. Report both:

- **information-matched** performance with the same observation set;
- **budget-matched** performance with the same number of likelihood evaluations or measured CPU budget.

Exact inference anchors small worlds. Approximate algorithms must report divergence from exact there before running at scale. A method that buys more observations must debit their cost. A method that searches a larger model space must debit proposal and likelihood evaluations.

## 4. Prospective endpoints, gates, and evidence trajectories

### 4.1 Primary endpoints

Every substantive card uses at least one event that was hidden during inference:

- next action in the current chain;
- next edit or reversal;
- stopping versus continuing;
- next episode's first choice;
- changed-context choice;
- source's next evidence selection or correction;
- next intervention issuer in a collaboration;
- realized held-out information gain per cost.

Retrospective latent accuracy is secondary. Free-form explanation quality is not an endpoint.

### 4.2 Required gates

Every causal card carries:

- live manipulation;
- bit-exact or tolerance-declared placebo;
- known-answer positive;
- no-oracle and no-label-leak;
- surface collision where historical or motive equivalence is claimed;
- prospective endpoint;
- calibration, abstention, and equivalence-class coverage;
- generator-family replication or explicit `CONSTRUCTION_IDENTITY` disposition.

Architecture cards additionally require information and compute receipts. History cards require final-skill matching and withholding of path identity. Source cards require noisy behavior and motive collisions. Foraging cards require unlearnable noise, silent change, and no-information abstention.

### 4.3 Evidence trajectories

Save the posterior and predictive score after every observation. Report:

- first evidence dose that improves the endpoint;
- reversals after contradiction or context change;
- error and calibration by similarity, access, and coupling;
- residual self, habit, history, and source-model bias;
- model expansions proposed, accepted, rejected, and falsely introduced;
- equivalence-class mass before and after resolving evidence.

## 5. Experimental families

Construct at least three independently coded families:

1. **chain world:** discrete action/revision chains with exact enumeration;
2. **composition world:** parts, edits, stopping, and role-relative constraints;
3. **communication world:** evidence selection, audience effects, correction, and private costly action.

No family may merely relabel another family's transition table. The shared ontology may be common, but emission and transition code paths must be independent. At least two families must support every promoted general claim.

Systematically vary:

- latent coupling from independent through redundant to synergistic;
- route overlap and shared cause;
- evidence dose and missingness;
- context visibility and opportunity-set visibility;
- policy temperature and competence;
- exact and approximate equifinality;
- correct, incomplete, and wrong model spaces;
- stationary, abruptly changing, and gradually drifting laws;
- maker-reader pairwise similarity, separate from family typicality.

Use a locked factorial spine plus a scrambled Sobol coverage sequence. Conditional effects come before pooled summaries. If an intervention is designed to change sign, a global average is forbidden as the headline.

## 8. Scores, inference, and promotion

### 8.1 Primary scores

- paired hidden-event log score in nats;
- calibration error and proper Brier score;
- risk–coverage and abstention curves;
- equivalence-class coverage and maximum unjustified member mass;
- realized information gain per declared cost;
- model-space recall, false expansion, and likelihood-evaluation cost;
- transfer regret on untouched generator families.

Report intervals at the independent world, maker, history, source, or team level. Rows, actions, tokens, posterior doses, and repeated edits are not independent makers.

### 8.2 Effect thresholds and multiplicity

Each card declares its smallest effect of interest before discovery, derived where possible from a fraction of a live positive control on the same score. Do not recycle V14's `0.02`-nat bar when the new live effect has a different scale.

Use:

- paired bootstrap by independent unit;
- equivalence tests for declared nulls;
- false-discovery control within predeclared exploratory families;
- sensitivity and phase-boundary intervals across generator parameters;
- generator-family replication before a general simulator claim.

No confidence statistic substitutes for magnitude. No pooled mean may headline a known qualitative interaction.

### 8.3 Promotion

A flight is promotion-eligible only if:

1. its instrument and live positive pass;
2. its prospective endpoint beats the strongest cheap rival by the locked magnitude;
3. it survives the attacks that target its mechanism;
4. it holds on an untouched lineage;
5. it reproduces in a second independent generator family, unless the claim is explicitly family-bound;
6. its causal-distance audit does not reduce it to a direct readout or fixed signature;
7. its novelty ledger names the closest prior result and the remaining project delta;
8. its claim sentence stays within the constructed-world ceiling.

A clean null or equivalence boundary can be promoted under the same integrity requirements. A dramatic retrospective classification cannot.

## 9. Continuous seven-day runtime contract

### 9.1 The 168-hour window

Once opened, one immutable UTC deadline is `start + 168 hours`. Restarts inherit it. The scientific scheduler is work-conserving:

- hours `0–150`: discovery, transfer, attacks, and balanced coverage stream;
- hour `150`: freeze flights and confirmation rules from committed discovery records;
- hours `150–166`: untouched confirmation, boundary replication, and confirmation controls;
- hours `166–168`: clean-clone reproduction, aggregate regeneration, ledger reconciliation, and final integrity checks;
- at or after hour `168`: generate the single curator packet.

No result prose, HTML, Markdown summary, bridge packet, or curator-facing chart may be emitted before the deadline. Machine-readable checkpoints and per-card verdicts may be written for recovery but must not contain narrative conclusions.

### 9.2 Preflight occurs before the window

Complete before opening:

- all 112 card implementations and 24 attacks;
- full smoke of every card, attack, resume path, and report guard on scratch output;
- structural and scientific preregistration locks;
- discarded timing pilot on disjoint seeds;
- resource and coexistence calibration;
- a frozen balanced coverage sequence large enough for at least four times the predicted seven-day capacity.

Pilot outcomes cannot alter hypotheses, criteria, factors, or estimator membership. They may choose only batch size, replicate floor, Sobol coverage length, and safe worker count.

### 9.3 Opening guard

The run must refuse to open unless all are true:

1. conservative upper forecast for the mandatory core is at most 150 hours at `W_safe`;
2. conservative lower forecast for core plus locked coverage exceeds 252 hours, protecting against a machine three times faster than the pilot median;
3. confirmation and integrity queues contain at least 24 forecast worker-hours independent of discovery candidates;
4. expected cells, independent-unit floors, and scheduling order are hashed;
5. the early-report guard, immutable deadline, restart, and orphan-kill recovery tests pass;
6. V14-sized and empty-queue fixtures are rejected.

If the guard fails, enlarge scientifically meaningful factor coverage or improve implementation before starting. Do not open the window and hope the clock supplies duration.

### 9.4 Work-conserving requirement

During hours `0–166`:

- never sleep to manufacture duration;
- never repeat identical rollouts, copy rows, or add seeds without increasing independent coverage;
- dispatch the next predeclared balanced cell whenever a safe worker is free;
- maintain at least one scientific worker whenever the machine is available;
- target at least 80% of the integral of safe worker capacity after subtracting recorded governor throttling and scheduler overhead;
- record parent and full descendant CPU time, worker occupancy, queue depth, and reasons for every gap over five minutes.

If the scientific queue empties, workers wait solely for the deadline, or balanced coverage is replaced by decorative repetition, set `RUNTIME_FAILED=true`. Results may still be reported after the deadline, but V15 is not complete and cannot claim the seven-day contract.

### 9.5 Balanced truncation at freeze

The coverage stream is a preregistered scrambled Sobol/factorial sequence ordered in complete balance blocks across family, coupling, access, misspecification, and reader architecture. At hour 150, finish the current atomic block and freeze. The executed prefix is therefore scientifically interpretable even though the total number of coverage cells is determined by machine speed. The mandatory core is not truncatable.

### 9.6 Coexistence

Remain CPU-first. Detect any active Sounding Line workload and reserve its declared cores, memory bandwidth, and data-loader headroom. The governor may reduce `W_safe`, priority, or batch size; it may not erase the deadline, change hypotheses, or silently mark skipped cells complete. A governor pause over ten minutes requires a machine-readable reason.

## 11. Research anchors and exact import

These sources constrain design; they are not evidence for V15 outcomes.

### 11.1 Inverse planning and expandable mental-state models

- Baker et al., [rational quantitative attribution of beliefs, desires, and percepts](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-017-0064)
- [AutoToM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15676): uncertainty-guided addition of mental variables and timesteps
- [LLM-Augmented Inverse Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03682): language-proposed hypotheses with Bayesian weighting
- [ThoughtTracing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11881): sequentially generated and weighted mental-state hypotheses
- [EnactToM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09826): literal mental-state answers can fail to support functional action

Import: compare hypothesis management architectures and require action-level prediction. Do not import an LLM or benchmark label as the theory.

### 11.2 Partial identifiability and informative imperfection

- Skalse et al., [partial identifiability in reward learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/skalse23a.html)
- Poiani et al., [suboptimal experts can shrink reward ambiguity](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/9bcd1fa0c05e5f25ba7a1261f1852e82-Abstract-Conference.html)
- Jara-Ettinger et al., [joint inference of competence, costs, and rewards](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25867996/)

Import: report feasible classes, use mistakes and competence variation as possible information, and never force a unique value when the record cannot identify it.

### 11.3 Strategic communication

- Barnett, Griffiths, and Hawkins, [a pragmatic account of the weak-evidence effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03799)
- Wu et al., [LLM sensitivity to motives behind communication](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19687)

Import: a listener's interpretation depends on a source-selection model, but motive salience and matching recursive assumptions can both help and mislead. V15 must test novel strategies and wrong audience models.

### 11.4 Exploration and robust design

- Ten et al., [humans monitor learning progress in curiosity-driven exploration](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8514490/)
- Poli et al., [expected learning progress and perceptual novelty](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9194910/)
- Go and Isaac, [robust expected information gain under prior ambiguity](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v180/go22a.html)

Import: learning progress is one candidate controller, not a synonym for epistemic foraging. Compare change detection, model expansion, standard and robust information gain, cost, and abstention.

### 11.5 Self projection

- Zhao and Tamir, [egocentric projection as a conditional strategy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10473870/)

Import: pairwise similarity may license a cheap local prior; it does not license an unconditional self-first advantage. C12–C13 are the final constructed adjudication unless a new estimator changes the comparison.

## 13. Pre-mortem

V15 will look sophisticated and still fail if:

1. architecture comparison replaces the artifact-reconstruction question;
2. a fashionable model wins by receiving more hypotheses, observations, or compute;
3. joint inference is tested only where latents were wired to require it;
4. independent inference is tested only where routes were wired to be disjoint;
5. correct labels are called understanding without predicting an unseen event;
6. model expansion fires on noise and is praised for flexibility;
7. a direct predictor wins in domain and its intervention failure is hidden;
8. attention, foreground goal, expertise, history, and preference are defined into the desired hierarchy;
9. learning history is recovered from a fixed signature or a supplied history feature;
10. a cost is translated directly into motivation without competence, constraint, signaling, and cost-function rivals;
11. changed value and better concealment differ by a telltale template;
12. private source action reads hidden belief directly;
13. a recursive listener knows the maker's audience model by oracle;
14. an equivalence class is collapsed into one compelling story;
15. a static artifact is allowed to name a director or acquisition history uniquely;
16. learning progress is renamed epistemic foraging and its change failure is ignored;
17. conditional sign reversals disappear into an overall mean;
18. repeated actions or rows masquerade as independent makers;
19. a `LANDED` card is reported as a held criterion;
20. a construction identity is promoted as a simulator discovery;
21. a simulation result is described as human, limbic, motor, affective, moral, or historical evidence;
22. the seven-day duration is manufactured by sleep, deadline waiting, duplicate rollouts, or ornamental seeds;
23. the mandatory core is silently compressed because the coverage stream is large;
24. a checkpoint, bridge file, or dashboard becomes an early curator packet;
25. a stale export is accepted after later cards land;
26. a failed runtime contract is softened into “short run” and called complete.

## 14. Definition of done

V15 is complete only when:

- all 112 mandatory cards have valid states and separate criterion statuses;
- all 24 attacks have outcomes or validated non-applicability;
- three genuinely independent generator families pass their identities;
- the coupling/access boundary atlas is conditionally reported;
- all promoted architectures are information- and compute-matched;
- exact anchors constrain approximate readers;
- label-only and full context-realized state are prospectively compared;
- expertise/history claims do not use a direct history channel or fixed signature;
- source claims do not use a direct private-belief readout;
- foreground switching, simultaneous control, habit, and hierarchy have matched rivals;
- value change, context change, concealment, and residue are crossed;
- foraging faces noise, silent change, prior ambiguity, cost, and null abstention;
- equivalence classes remain explicit;
- discovery, transfer, attack, and confirmation lineages are disjoint;
- the mandatory core completes before freeze;
- useful scientific work remains continuously dispatched through hour 166;
- worker occupancy and descendant CPU satisfy the runtime receipt;
- no early packet exists;
- the final packet is generated only after hour 168;
- fresh-clone validation regenerates all scientific aggregates and ledgers;
- every publication-relevance row names prior art, project delta, and missing evidence.

A complete negative boundary map is a successful V15. A week of computation without stronger rivals, prospective endpoints, and claim discipline is not.
